Oversight

Three guarantees. Built in. No add-ons.

Confidence thresholds

Every Aiva has clear scope. Ambiguous or out-of-scope work is paused and routed to you (or your team) instead of guessing. You stay in the same channel.

Governance layer active

Every message, tool call, and automation run passes through built-in governance monitoring. Volume spikes, repeated failures, and out-of-scope actions are caught and paused before your client sees a problem.

Full tamper-evident audit log

Every action is logged with a cryptographic content hash, stored by VERLOX, and available on request. If a client or auditor asks what your Aiva did on a given date, you can show them the exact sequence of events.
