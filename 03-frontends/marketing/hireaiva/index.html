Questions

Before you start the trial

What does Aiva actually do?

It answers customer messages, books appointments, chases follow-ups, and runs recurring routines you define, across whichever channels you connect. You give it a role, context about your business, and boundaries; it carries the work through to done instead of handing you a draft to finish.

How autonomous is it, does it act without me?

Within the scope you set, yes. Outside that scope, no: confidence thresholds pause anything ambiguous and route it to you instead of guessing. You decide how wide the scope is when you set the role up, and can narrow or expand it any time.

What counts as one AI employee?

One configured Aiva: one role, one memory, one set of connected channels. The Starter plan includes one; Team includes up to five, each with its own role and context; Business is unlimited.

Which channels and tools does it connect to?

WhatsApp, email, Slack, Telegram, web chat, Discord, SMS, Microsoft Teams, Google Chat, and more, all sharing one memory. Google Calendar connects directly for real booking and scheduling. See the Channels section above for the full list.

Do I need technical skills to set it up?

No. Registration, workspace setup, and channel connection are guided steps in the workspace itself, not a developer integration. Most people are live on their first channel within minutes of signing up.

What happens when something is outside its scope?

It pauses and routes the message to you (or your team) in the same channel, rather than guessing or making something up. You can also arrange optional human escalation for judgment-heavy cases.

Is the governance and audit log optional?

No, it is built into every plan at no extra cost. Every message, tool call, and automation run passes through governance monitoring, and every action is logged with a cryptographic content hash you can produce on request.

How does the 30-day free trial work?

No card required to start. You get full access to your chosen tier for 30 days, so you can run real conversations and automations on your own account before deciding to pay.

Can I change plans or cancel any time?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from the workspace whenever you need to; see the cancellation policy for the specifics.

How is my data handled?

Current practice is documented on the security page, which we keep to facts that match the live product rather than unreviewed claims. If you have a specific security questionnaire, that page tells you what to ask for and where to send it.

What is a business line?

A business line is a configured outreach vertical: pipeline stages, reply intents, qualification fields, and channel bindings for one kind of work (for example property deal sourcing or finance outreach). Team includes one line; Business includes unlimited lines.

What does Outreach include on Team?

The Outreach engine covers CRM leads, pipeline stages, sequences, campaigns, approvals, and business-line packs. Team includes one business line. You can try simulate-inbound on a line during your free trial before you upgrade.

How does Property work on Business?

Business unlocks the Property workspace: UK due diligence (Land Registry, EPC, Companies House), 28 native investment calculators with step-by-step working, saved searches, discovery signals, and floor-plan-to-BOQ refurb estimates. It is built for investors and deal sourcers, not just lettings enquiries.

What is included with Propreneur on Business?

Business plans unlock the Aiva bridge to Propreneur, our UK property MIS - connect your own Propreneur account (a separate subscription) and your Aiva can read and update portfolio, deals, tenancies, contacts, and maintenance records in Propreneur while you stay in Aiva. Starter and Team do not include bridge access.

What is the difference between Aiva and Labyrinth Scout?

Aiva is the AI employee itself, built-in governance included. Scout is a separate, deeper governance product for teams running AI agents at scale who need policy templates, a kill switch, and compliance reporting across more than one system. Aiva and Scout are independent products from VERLOX; you don't need Scout to use Aiva.
