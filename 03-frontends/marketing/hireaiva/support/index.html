Customer support

How to get help with Aiva

We handle billing, account access, onboarding, and product issues by email. We aim to reply within one UK business day.

Contact

Email support@carinaai.uk. Response target: within one UK business day (Monday to Friday, excluding UK public holidays).

What we support

  • Billing questions, invoices, and subscription changes
  • Account access, login, and workspace setup
  • Onboarding and connecting your first AI Employee
  • Product issues with email handling, approvals, or governance

Security incidents

If you believe there is an urgent security issue, mark your email subject as urgent (for example: Urgent - security incident) and include what happened and when.

What to include in your email

  • The account email you used to register
  • Your workspace name
  • Relevant screenshots or error messages
  • Stripe receipt or invoice reference for billing issues

Billing and cancellation

See our refund policy and cancellation policy for how refund and cancellation requests are handled.
