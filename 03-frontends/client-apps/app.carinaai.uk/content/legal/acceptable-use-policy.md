# Aiva - Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective date:** 2026-07-14
**Last updated:** 2026-07-14

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") sets out the rules for using Aiva, provided by Verlox
Limited ("Verlox Ltd", "we", "us", "our"). This policy forms part of the Terms of Service.
Violation of this policy may result in suspension or termination of the Customer's account.

---

## 1. General Conduct

1.1 The Customer shall use the Service only for lawful business purposes and in compliance
with all applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards.

1.2 The Customer is responsible for all content, data, and communications processed through
the Service by the Customer or its Authorised Users.

---

## 2. Prohibited Uses

The Customer shall not use the Service to:

2.1 **Illegal activity.** Conduct, promote, or facilitate any activity that is illegal under
the laws of England and Wales or the jurisdiction in which the Customer operates.

2.2 **Harmful content.** Generate, store, or distribute content that is defamatory,
obscene, threatening, harassing, discriminatory, or that incites violence or hatred.

2.3 **Spam and unsolicited messages.** Send bulk unsolicited messages, spam, or phishing
communications through any channel connected to the Service.

2.4 **Deception.** Impersonate any person or entity, or misrepresent the Customer's
identity or affiliation. This includes configuring AI workers in a way that misleads
recipients into believing they are communicating with a human when they are not.

2.5 **Data harvesting.** Scrape, crawl, or extract data from the Service or from third-party
platforms through the Service in violation of those platforms' terms.

2.6 **Security interference.** Attempt to gain unauthorised access to the Service, other
accounts, or the systems and networks connected to the Service. This includes probing
for vulnerabilities, brute-force attacks, denial-of-service attacks, and reverse
engineering.

2.7 **Circumvention.** Bypass rate limits, usage quotas, access controls, or any other
technical restrictions built into the Service.

2.8 **Malware.** Upload, transmit, or distribute viruses, trojans, ransomware, or any other
malicious software or code.

2.9 **Regulated industries without compliance.** Process data subject to specific regulatory
requirements (such as health records under NHS/HIPAA, payment card data under PCI-DSS, or
classified government data) unless the Customer has verified that its use of the Service
meets those requirements.

2.10 **Child safety.** Process personal data of children under 13 (or the applicable age of
digital consent in the Customer's jurisdiction) without verifiable parental consent.

---

## 3. AI-Specific Rules

3.1 **Disclosure.** The Customer shall ensure that people interacting with the Customer's AI
workers are aware they are communicating with an AI system, unless the context makes it
obvious.

3.2 **Human oversight.** The Customer shall maintain appropriate human oversight of AI
worker actions, particularly where those actions involve financial transactions, legal
commitments, or sensitive personal data.

3.3 **Escalation.** The Customer shall configure escalation paths so that AI workers can
hand off conversations to a human when the situation requires it.

3.4 **Accuracy.** The Customer acknowledges that AI-generated content may contain errors.
The Customer is responsible for reviewing and verifying AI output before acting on it or
sharing it with third parties.

---

## 4. Resource Limits

4.1 The Customer shall not use the Service in a way that consumes disproportionate
resources, degrades performance for other customers, or interferes with the operation of
the Service.

4.2 Verlox Ltd reserves the right to throttle or suspend accounts that exceed reasonable
usage patterns, with notice where practicable.

---

## 5. Reporting Violations

5.1 If the Customer becomes aware of any violation of this policy (by its own users or by
a third party), the Customer should report it to contact@verlox.uk.

5.2 Verlox Ltd investigates reports promptly and may take action including warning the
account holder, suspending access, or terminating the account.

---

## 6. Enforcement

6.1 Verlox Ltd may suspend or terminate the Customer's access to the Service, without
refund, if the Customer violates this policy.

6.2 Where practicable, we will provide notice and an opportunity to remedy the violation
before taking enforcement action. However, we reserve the right to act immediately where
the violation poses an imminent risk to the Service, other customers, or third parties.

6.3 The Customer may appeal an enforcement decision by emailing contact@verlox.uk within
14 days of the action.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

7.1 We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via
email or an in-app notice at least 14 days before they take effect.
