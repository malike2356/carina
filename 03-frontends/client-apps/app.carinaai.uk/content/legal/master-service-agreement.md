## 6. Service Levels

6.1 **Availability target.** Verlox Ltd targets 99.5% monthly uptime for the Service,
measured as the percentage of minutes in a calendar month during which the Service is
available, excluding planned maintenance.

6.2 **Planned maintenance.** We will give at least 48 hours' notice before planned
maintenance that may affect availability. Where possible, maintenance is scheduled outside
UK business hours (09:00 to 18:00 GMT/BST, Monday to Friday).

6.3 **Exclusions.** The availability target does not include downtime caused by: (a) the
Customer's equipment, network, or third-party services; (b) force majeure events; (c)
actions or omissions of the Customer or its Authorised Users; (d) features or integrations
in beta or preview.

6.4 **Service credits.** If the Service falls below the availability target in any calendar
month, the Customer may request a service credit equal to 5% of the monthly fee for each
full percentage point below the target, up to a maximum of 30% of the monthly fee. Service
credits are applied to the next invoice. Credits are the Customer's sole remedy for
availability failures.

6.5 **Credit request process.** The Customer must request service credits within 14 days
of the end of the affected month by emailing contact@verlox.uk with the dates and times of
the outage. Verlox Ltd will verify the claim against its monitoring records and apply
eligible credits within one billing cycle.
