# HMO Licensing - UK (Carina / Nexio)

Tags: `hmo`, `licensing`, `compliance`

## When a licence is required

- **Mandatory HMO licensing (England):** 5+ occupants forming 2+ households, and the property meets the storey test (3+ storeys historically; check current local rules - some areas use “5+ occupants” without storey test under national extensions).
- **Additional licensing:** Councils can designate areas where smaller HMOs need a licence.
- **Selective licensing:** Non-HMO private rented sectors in designated areas.

## Article 4 directions

Planning permission may be required to convert a dwelling to an HMO where an Article 4 direction removes permitted development rights. Always check the local planning authority before acquiring or converting.

## Licence conditions

Typical conditions cover: maximum occupiers, room sizes, amenity standards, fire precautions, management standards, waste storage, and contact details for the licence holder and manager.

## Enforcement and penalties

Operating an unlicensed HMO where a licence is required is a criminal offence. Councils can issue civil penalties, rent repayment orders, and prohibition orders. Fines can be unlimited for individuals; companies face substantial penalties.
