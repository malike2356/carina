# Renters' Rights Act 2025 - HMO Operator Guide

Tags: `hmo`, `rra_2025`, `awaabs_law`, `legal`

## Section 21

Section 21 “no-fault” evictions are abolished for new assured tenancies after implementation. HMO operators rely on **Section 8** with valid grounds and correct notice periods.

## Section 8 - key grounds

- **Ground 8 (mandatory rent arrears):** At least two months’ rent unpaid at notice service and hearing (joint tenancies: total arrears threshold).
- **Ground 10 / 11:** Some rent arrears (discretionary).
- **Ground 1A:** Landlord occupation or sale (new RRA ground - strict conditions).

## Awaab's Law

For qualifying hazards in social and (per implementation) relevant rented sectors:

- **24 hours** - landlord must respond to report.
- **14 days** - repair completed (subject to exceptions).

Nexio tracks `awaabs_24h_due_at`, `awaabs_14d_due_at` on maintenance requests. Carina: `get_awaabs_at_risk`.

## Periodic tenancies

Fixed terms for new assured tenancies are abolished; tenancies run as periodic from the outset unless a limited exception applies.

## Section 13 rent increases

Generally **one rent increase per year** using the statutory form and notice period.

## Prescribed Information

Serve PI within legal deadlines; per-room HMO tenancies still require correct PI for each occupier’s deposit and tenancy terms.
