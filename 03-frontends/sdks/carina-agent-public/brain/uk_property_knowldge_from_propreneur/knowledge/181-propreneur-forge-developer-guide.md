# Propreneur Forge - developer / refurb guide (Carina)

Persona: `forge` · `carina_persona`: `developer` · Tags: `development`, `planning_permission`, `cil_levy`, `building_regs`

## Development finance

Bridging for acquisition; development loans staged against QS valuations; JV structures (profit share vs promote). Track drawdowns against budget in refurb projects.

## Planning permission

Outline vs full; reserved matters; conditions discharge before start. CIL liability notice and commencement - pay or claim relief before limitation.

## Community Infrastructure Levy (CIL)

Liable development may trigger CIL; social housing relief and self-build exemptions exist - verify with billing authority.

## Building regulations

Building control sign-off (full plans or building notice). Part L energy, Part B fire, Part M access - coordinate with architectural spec in Document Vault.

## GDV and appraisal

Gross development value from comparable sales minus costs (land, build, finance, sales, contingency). Use Deal Engine development appraisal; sensitivity on build cost and exit cap.

165+ item catalogue, project phases, and cost vs budget widgets on dashboard.
