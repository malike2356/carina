## Article 4 and planning controls

Many councils use Article 4 directions to remove permitted development rights for whole-dwelling C3 → C4 (HMO) or for short-term lets. Check the local plan and licensing register before listing. Planning permission may be required where PD rights are withdrawn.

## 90-day rule (Greater London)

The Deregulation Act 2015 (London) limits use of a dwelling as temporary sleeping accommodation to 90 nights in any calendar year unless planning permission is granted. Track nights per property; Carina should warn when approaching the cap.

## SA yield

Gross revenue minus voids, platform fees, cleaning, utilities, council tax (where landlord liable), insurance, and finance costs. Use the Deal Engine SA calculator; benchmark against comparable ADR and occupancy.

## Dynamic pricing

Seasonality, events, lead time, and length of stay drive ADR. Review channel calendars weekly; avoid race-to-bottom on single platforms.

## Fire safety (SA)

Higher occupancy and turnover increase risk. BS 5839-6 smoke/heat alarms, escape routes, furniture fire labels (Furniture and Furnishings Regulations), and risk assessment for common parts where applicable.

## Operations

Furnishing standards (inventory photos), check-in/out procedures, keyless codes with audit trail, and guest communication templates via Comms.

## Channel managers

Sync calendars to prevent double bookings; viCal integration for direct bookings. Document which platform owns the guest contract for disputes.
