# Council Tax - HMOs (UK)

Tags: `hmo`, `council_tax`, `finance`

## Mandatory HMO definition (council tax)

For council tax purposes a **mandatory HMO** is often defined as a property occupied by five or more people forming two or more households, with shared facilities - aligned with licensing concepts but decided by the billing authority.

## Who pays?

- **Mandatory HMO:** The **landlord** is usually liable for council tax (not individual occupiers).
- **Non-mandatory shared houses:** Occupiers may be individually liable depending on structure and local classification.

## Local Government Finance Act 1992

Billing authorities classify properties; landlords should confirm status in writing when acquiring an HMO.

## Modelling in Propreneur

Record council tax as a **landlord expense** on HMO properties (portfolio running costs, deal analysis, room yield net calculations). Do not assume per-room council tax unless the authority confirms occupier liability.
