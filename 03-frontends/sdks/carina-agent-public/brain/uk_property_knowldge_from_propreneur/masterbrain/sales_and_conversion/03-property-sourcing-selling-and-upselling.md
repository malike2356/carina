# Property Sourcing: Selling, Packaging & Upselling

**Source:** TENX Property Sourcing Manual Module 7
**Purpose:** How to package and sell deals, bespoke sourcing, getting paid, and upselling additional services
**Audience:** Carina can guide a sourcer through the sales process, contract terms, and add-on services

---

## Bespoke Sourcing

Charge investor upfront -- typically **£1,000 + VAT**. Scope: find 6 properties matching their specific criteria. Finder's fee on top (typically ~£4,000 total per bespoke deal).

If you present 6 matching properties and they don't buy any, the contract is fulfilled.

Benefits:
- Filters time-wasters (willing to pay = serious).
- Provides focus -- you know exactly what they want.
- Unsold deals can be offered to other investors on your database.

## Staging and Set Up (Additional Service)

Once a deal completes, the investor may pay you to furnish and stage the property. Average profit per staging deal: **£4,000-£5,000**.

Two models:
1. Investor buys everything, you assemble and set up.
2. You buy everything, charge a higher fee.

## Deal Packaging Process

Five-step workflow for taking a deal from discovery to sale:

1. **Area Due Diligence** -- Pull Goldmine research data (PropertyData branded PDFs).
2. **Find Deal** -- Rightmove search, phone agent, book viewing.
3. **View + Full DD** -- 10-15 min walk-through, check damp, structure, roof, services.
4. **Get Offer Accepted** -- Negotiate with agent/vendor.
5. **Package and Sell** -- Prepare deal with photos, figures, area data.

## Creating the Offer (What to Send Investors)

**Initial email to database:**
- DO NOT give full address or postcode.
- Include: property type, bedrooms, location (town/city only), purchase price, estimated refurb cost, estimated end value, estimated rental income, ROI.

**Example:**
"3-bed terrace in Leicester. Purchase: £60,000. Refurb: £20,000. End value: £100,000. Rent: £750pcm. ROI: 15%."

Investor gets full details only AFTER paying finder's fee and signing T&Cs.

## Sales Call

After sending deals email, call interested investors. Strict rules:
1. Add value first.
2. Solve their problems.
3. Ask questions to qualify.

Qualifying questions:
- "What does your current portfolio look like?"
- "What interested you in this particular deal?"
- "How are you financing this purchase?"
- "What would make you happy you bought this?"

Qualify properly -- don't sell to someone who can't actually buy.

## Getting Paid

Stick to your rules:
- Get paid before giving full property information.
- Have T&Cs and invoicing system ready before first sale.
- Payment flow: investor pays finder's fee -> goes into client holding account -> cooling-off period expires -> transfer to day-to-day account.
- Don't spend the money before it's cleared.
- Record all sales calls for review and improvement.

## Upselling

Easiest to sell to an existing client. After deal completes, upsell:
- **Staging and set up** (furniture, appliances, assembly, styling).
- **Tenant find** (vet and place tenants).
- **Property management** (ongoing management for a monthly fee).
