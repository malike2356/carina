# Discovery Questions for Property Sourcers

**Context:** Originally drafted during Propreneur v1. Refined 6 May 2026 with Malike.

**Core thesis:** The bundle (£49/mo vs £135-380/mo piecemeal) gets them in the door. The questions reveal what to build inside it - so it's not just cheaper, it's better.

**Storage note:** Each set captures a different tier of depth. Use whichever fits the context.

---

## Set A - Onboarding / First Call (Recommended Starting Point)

*Versatile. Works in onboarding form, first discovery call, or lead magnet. ~8 min to answer.*

1. **What's the most annoying recurring task in your week as a sourcer?**
   *(Anchors to role + recurrence. Tells you the habit to break.)*

2. **What spreadsheet or calculator do you reach for most often, and what do you hate about it?**
   *(Adds "reach for most often" - tells you which tool is most used, not just most hated.)*

3. **What mistake did you make on a deal last year that cost you time or money - and how did you find out too late?**
   *(Binds mistake to moment of discovery. The "how you found out" is where the product opportunity lives.)*

4. **If an investor asked you to prove a deal was good, where do you go and how long does it take?**
   *(Uncovers the credibility/trust gap - sourcing isn't just finding deals, it's convincing people to fund them.)*

5. **If you could snap your fingers and have one part of your deal pipeline run itself, what would it be?**
   *(Narrower than a generic "magic wand" - keeps it in the property world.)*

---

## Set B - Short / Lead Magnet or Landing Page Quiz

*3 questions. Fast to answer. Still gives actionable intel.*

1. **Which spreadsheet or tool do you use most that you secretly hate?**

2. **What's the one thing about finding and pitching deals that makes you want to quit for the week?**

3. **If you could wave a wand and make one part of your business automatic, what's the first thing?**

---

## Set C - Deep / Discovery Call or Paid Advisory

*Designed for 1:1 conversations. Expect 20-30 min. These surface system-level gaps.*

1. **Walk me through your last deal from find to close. Where did the friction points show up?**

2. **What's the most expensive mistake you've made on a deal - and what system could have caught it?**

3. **What information do you wish you had before you made an offer that you only get after?**

4. **When an investor says no, what's the real reason? (Not what they tell you - what you suspect.)**

5. **If I told you Propreneur could automate everything on the KYC/AML, deal analysis, and investor reporting side - what would you still want to do yourself?**
   *(Key question. The bundle is about what to take off their plate. Stickiness comes from what they want to keep control of.)*

---

## Usage Notes

- **The originals** (v1) were: "What's the most annoying part of your week?", "What spreadsheet do you use that you hate?", "What do you wish would just happen automatically?", "What mistake did you make last year that cost you money?", "If you could wave a magic wand, what would software do for you?"
- **Problem with originals:** Too generic for any SaaS audience. Q3 and Q5 overlap. No anchoring to property sourcing or deal velocity.
- **Strategy:** Use the bundle price as the entry wedge. Use the questions as the product intelligence engine. Find the 20% of features that solve 80% of friction, then relentlessly automate that 20%.
- **Next step after collecting answers:** Pattern-match across responses. Whatever 2-3 themes emerge most often = next feature sprint.
