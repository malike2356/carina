I have been thinking about how to explain this without it sounding scattered.

The truth is, it all connects.

Since July last year, I have been working at the University of Portsmouth as a Services and Facilities Assistant under Residential Services, within the Estates Department. Housing operations, building compliance, maintenance cycles, tenant turnover. Every day.

I took that role because I wanted to understand property from the ground up.

Then I started building a system for myself. A single platform where I could manage deal pipeline, compliance, investor relationships, and property analysis - all in one place. Because I could not find one that did everything.

I called it Propreneur.

When I spoke to people already in property, I realised something. A typical property sourcer needs:

- GoHighLevel CRM: £77/month
- Property Filter: £99/month + VAT
- PropertyData: £14-£60/month
- Invoicing software: £12-£25/month
- Document storage: £10/month
- Email marketing: £13-£20/month
- KYC/AML: £1 per check
- Website hosting: £15-£20/month

Total: £240-£390+ per month in fragmented tools that do not talk to each other.

Propreneur bundles it all. One platform. £99/month. Or use the Community Edition for free.

I built it for myself first. I needed it for Xelox. But when I saw the gap, I decided to improve it and let others use it too.

And now the execution arm is here.

**I am launching Xelox Properties Ltd.**

Xelox is a property sourcing and guaranteed rent company focused on Portsmouth, Hampshire, and the Isle of Wight. We find below market value deals for investors. We offer guaranteed rent solutions for landlords who want fixed income without management headaches.

Propreneur runs Xelox. Xelox feeds back into Propreneur. One is the OS. The other is the real-world deal engine.
