## Why Testimonials Matter

Testimonials and reviews are the lifeline of a property sourcing business. They build trust with potential investors before you've even met them. A prospect reading a testimonial from a previous happy investor is worth more than anything you can say about yourself.

**The process:** Create customer → Create product → Sell product → Get customer happy → Get reviews

## How to Ask for Testimonials

When an investor is happy, ask immediately. Don't wait. Don't be scared.

### Ask by email
Send a simple request:
> "Would you be willing to give me a written testimonial about your experience working with me?"

### Ask for a video (best option)
> "Would you be okay meeting me at the property so we can film a walkthrough and you can tell me what you liked and didn't like?"

Most investors won't have time for a full filmed walkthrough, but many will be willing to pull out their phone and record a quick 10-15 second testimonial. Even a short clip is powerful.

### Displaying testimonials

- Display them prominently on your website
- Use review widgets that pop up in the bottom corner (like Trustpilot)
- Embed video testimonials on key pages
- Feature them in sales calls and pitch decks

## Trustpilot Strategy

- Aim for a 4.5+ star rating
- Each positive review is social proof for the next prospect
- Respond to every review - good or bad
- Link your Trustpilot profile on your website, email signature, and social media

## Key Principles

1. Treat customers well so they WANT to leave reviews
2. Ask for reviews immediately when they're happy - don't delay
3. Make it easy for them (send a link, offer to film it yourself)
4. Once you have testimonials, publicise them like crazy
5. Never be scared to ask
