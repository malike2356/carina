# Creating Property Marketing Graphics

**Source:** Property Sourcing Intensive (PSI) - Module 3: Marketing, Session 11
**Instructor:** the course instructor + marketing manager
**Extracted:** 14 May 2026

---

## Tool: Canva

The recommended tool for creating property marketing graphics. Available in two tiers:

| Version | Cost | Best For |
|---------|------|----------|
| Free | £0 | Basic social media posts |
| Pro | Subscription | Brand kits, premium templates, background remover |

Pro version recommended if you're creating graphics regularly. The free version is sufficient to start.

## Design Specifications

- **Instagram post size:** 1080 x 1080px
- **Format:** JPEG (preferred for social media)
- **Quality:** Good/8+ for clean images

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Open Canva** and select Instagram Post template (1080x1080)
2. **Search templates** - type "property" or "real estate" for relevant pre-made designs
3. **Select a template** that roughly matches what you need
4. **Replace images** - upload your own property photos to replace template placeholders
5. **Adjust text** - ensure it contrasts with the background (e.g., white text on dark backgrounds)
6. **Add your logo** - use a white format version so it shows clearly on dark/coloured backgrounds
7. **Add a call to action (CTA)** - phone number, website, email, "comment below for more info"
8. **Download** - click Share → Download → JPEG, 1080x1080, good quality

## Batch Production Strategy

- Create 5-7 designs in one sitting
- Schedule them on Instagram
- This saves time and ensures consistent posting

## Brand Consistency Rules

- Follow your logo colours
- Keep fonts consistent across all graphics
- Maintain the same style so followers recognise your content instantly
- Practise regularly - speed improves with repetition

## Free vs Paid

You don't need to invest heavily upfront. The free tool does the job. As you grow, upgrade to Pro for efficiency features like brand kits, magic resize, and premium templates.
