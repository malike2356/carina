# Creating a Five-Star Airbnb Guest Experience on the South Coast

The Airbnb guest experience determines your listing's success more than any other factor. The algorithm rewards listings that generate bookings and positive reviews. Every interaction a guest has with your property and your service feeds into that cycle.

On the South Coast, where competition includes coastal holiday lets, city centre apartments, and countryside retreats, the guest experience is your primary differentiator. This article covers the practical steps to delivering a memorable guest experience that generates repeat bookings and consistent five-star reviews.

## The Guest Journey Framework

Think of the guest experience as a journey with distinct stages. Each stage is an opportunity to impress or disappoint.

### Pre-Booking: The First Impression

Your listing page is the first interaction. The hero image makes the strongest impression. Invest in professional photography that shows your property at its best. Natural light, well-styled rooms, and clear shots of each space matter more than the number of photos.

Your listing description should answer every question a guest might have before they ask: how to check in, where to park, what is in the local area, and what amenities you provide. A comprehensive listing reduces pre-booking enquiries and signals professionalism.

### Booking to Check-In: Building Anticipation

Once a guest books, send a confirmation message within 24 hours. Include clear directions, parking information, a local recommendations guide, and your contact details.

A well-prepared welcome guide can be the difference between a five-star review and a four-star review. Include recommendations for local restaurants, takeaway options, nearby attractions, and public transport information. For Portsmouth properties, highlight the Historic Dockyard, Spinnaker Tower, and Gunwharf Quays. For Isle of Wight properties, include ferry schedules and popular beaches.

### Arrival: The First Physical Impression

First impressions of the physical property are formed within seconds. The entrance should be clean, well-lit, and welcoming. The key or lockbox should be easy to access with clear instructions.

Inside, the property should smell fresh, feel clean, and look exactly as it appeared in the photos. A welcome note, a small welcome gift, and clear instructions for appliances set a positive tone.

### During the Stay: Supporting Without Intruding

Guests want to know you are available if needed, but they do not want to be contacted unnecessarily. Strike the right balance by:

- Sending a message on the second day asking if everything is satisfactory
- Providing a guest manual with instructions for heating, appliances, and Wi-Fi
- Having a contact number available for urgent issues
- Responding to messages within 30 minutes during reasonable hours

### Check-Out: Ending on a High Note

The departure process should be simple. Provide clear check-out instructions in writing and in the property. A thank-you message on the morning of departure leaves a positive final impression.

After departure, send a follow-up message thanking the guest and asking them to leave a review. Do not pressure them, but a polite request is appropriate.

## What South Coast Guests Expect

South Coast guests fall into several categories, each with different expectations.

**Tourists** want proximity to attractions, comfortable accommodation, and local knowledge. They value recommendations for things to do and places to eat.

**Business travellers** want reliable Wi-Fi, a desk or workspace, and quiet surroundings. They value efficiency and clarity over charm.

**Families** want space, child-friendly amenities, and safety. They value high chairs, travel cots, and secure gardens.

**Couples** want privacy, comfort, and a touch of luxury. They value quality bedding, good shower pressure, and thoughtful extras such as coffee machines or bath robes.

Knowing your target guest and tailoring your experience to their needs will generate better reviews than a generic approach.

## The Essentials of a Five-Star Experience

Certain elements are non-negotiable for a five-star rating:

- Spotless cleanliness throughout the property
- Comfortable beds with good quality mattresses and bedding
- Reliable, fast Wi-Fi
- Good water pressure and consistent hot water
- Well-equipped kitchen with cooking basics (oil, salt, pepper, tea, coffee)
- Clear instructions for everything from the TV remote to the heating controls
- Immediate response to any issues during the stay

## Handling Problems

Problems will happen. A boiler stops working. A guest arrives to find the Wi-Fi is down. A neighbour complains about noise.

The difference between a five-star operator and an amateur is how the problem is handled. Respond immediately, apologise sincerely, and fix the issue as quickly as possible. Offer a goodwill gesture for significant inconvenience, such as a partial refund or a discount on a future booking.

Guests who experience excellent problem resolution often leave better reviews than those who had no problems at all, because the positive recovery experience exceeds their expectations.

## Using Reviews to Improve

Every review, good or bad, contains information you can use. Analyse recurring themes. If multiple guests mention the same issue, fix it. If guests consistently praise the same aspect, emphasise it in your listing and guest communication.

The guest experience is not a set of isolated actions. It is a system that delivers a consistent standard across every booking, every guest, and every property. Build the system, and the five-star reviews will follow.
