# Property Sourcing Reservation Agreement - Terms and Conditions

## Two Agreement Types

Property sourcing businesses use two variants:

1. **Standard Reservation Agreement** - used when a specific property has been found for an investor. Covers a named property, includes a 14-day due diligence period.
2. **Bespoke Sourcing Agreement** - used when an investor pays an upfront instruction fee for the firm to search specifically for them. No specific property named at outset.

### IT IS AGREED

**1. Payment of the Reservation Fee**

a. On the date of this agreement the buyer shall pay a reservation fee to Company Name as detailed above.

b. The buyer acknowledges that the reservation fee is not a part payment or deposit towards the purchase price of the property, but a fee paid to Company Name for their services in securing the property on the buyer's behalf.

c. The Reservation Fee is **Non-Refundable** after the 14-day Due Diligence period. No refunds will be authorised after 14 days.

d. Should a property fall through due to an issue with the vendor, Company Name will offer a replacement property of similar nature and value within **6 months** of the original transaction date.

e. If the buyer withdraws from the deal after the due diligence period, there will be no replacement offered or refund issued.

**2. Replacement Properties**

A replacement property will be offered only if:

a. The vendor withdraws from the sale
b. A survey report deems the property an unviable purchase
c. A legal issue with the property will result in additional expense or extensive delays
d. A RICS Property Valuation Report results in a down-value exceeding **10% of the agreed purchase price**

**3. Buyer's Obligations**

a. Complete the transaction in a timely manner
b. Provide all requested documentation without delay
c. Instruct chosen solicitor within **5 days**
d. Take independent mortgage advice (if applicable)
e. Satisfy themselves within the 14-day Due Diligence period that they are happy with the property
f. Understand that after the due diligence period Company Name will not refund any fees
g. Comply with all client due diligence requests from Company Name and any approved third-party suppliers
h. Confirm they have assessed their financial status and are able to obtain required funding
i. Pay the reservation fee in full on signing this agreement

**4. Company Name's Obligations**

a. Carry out suitable due diligence to ensure the property is a viable deal
b. View and carry out a full property inspection
c. Carry out due diligence on the property to ensure sufficient rental demand
d. Store and keep safe all personal information under the Data Protection Regulation
e. Provide full due diligence to the buyer upon request
f. Provide the buyer with full disclosure of the property promptly after the reservation fee is paid
g. Process any refunds requested within the due diligence period promptly once approved
h. Ensure the company remains and acts in a compliant manner at all times

**5. Termination**

a. The buyer may terminate this contract within 14 days of signing
b. A termination fee of £250 is deducted from all refunds of reservation fees
c. All termination requests must be made within 14 days and in writing to [Company Email]
d. All refunds will be made no later than **30 days** after being approved
e. All refunds will be made to the original source payment account

**6. Company Name's Position**

The buyer acknowledges that Company Name's capacity in relation to this agreement is solely to act as an **introducer** of the buyer to the vendor or agent selling the property.

**7. Costs**

a. The buyer agrees to pay all costs associated with the purchase of the property
b. Company Name is not liable for any costs associated with the purchase

**8. General**

a. This agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes any prior agreement
b. No party shall make any public announcements or broadcasts in relation to this agreement without prior written approval from Company Name (including social media)

**9. Third Party Rights**

No third party shall have any right to the terms of this agreement.

**10. Governing Law**

This agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Law of England and Wales.

**11. Jurisdiction**

The Courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this agreement.

**12. Money Laundering**

a. Under the Money Laundering Regulation 2017, Company Name is legally obliged to verify your identity and address
b. Company Name reserves the right to make such enquiries and may use a suitably qualified third-party supplier to conduct checks, which may include a credit check (soft footprint only)
c. Company Name is bound by the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 and is obliged to report any suspicions to the NCA

## Bespoke Sourcing Agreement - Key Differences

Used when an investor pays an upfront **instruction fee** for bespoke sourcing (no specific property at time of signing).

| Feature | Standard Reservation | Bespoke Sourcing |
|---|---|---|
| Property named at signing | Yes | No |
| Fee type | Reservation fee (£2,000 typical) | Instruction fee (£1,200 typical) |
| Fee refundable? | Yes, within 14 days (minus £250 admin) | Non-refundable under all circumstances |
| Replacement provision | Yes (vendor withdrawal, survey, legal issue, down-value >10%) | Company provides 6 opportunities matching buyer criteria |
| Contract termination | Yes, within 14 days | Not applicable (fee paid for search, not specific property) |
| Due diligence period | 14 days on the property | N/A |

### Bespoke Sourcing Key Clauses

- The instruction fee is non-refundable in any circumstance and does not constitute any part of the finders fee payable towards any deal presented
- Company Name will provide **6 opportunities** that match the criteria set out. If the buyer fails to take up any of these, the contract is deemed fulfilled and the business relationship terminates
- The buyer must assess financial status and confirm they can obtain funding before signing
- CDD must be completed before any deals are presented
- Buyer must instruct solicitor within 5 days of any deal presented

## Reservation Fee - Standard Benchmarks

| Business Stage | Typical Reservation Fee |
|---|---|
| New property sourcer | £1,500-£2,000 |
| Established sourcer | £2,000-£3,500 |
| Premium / high-value deals | £3,000-£5,000 |
| Bespoke instruction fee | £1,000-£2,000 (upfront, non-refundable) |

Note: Fees are separate from and in addition to the purchase price of the property. They do not form part of the purchase price or deposit.
