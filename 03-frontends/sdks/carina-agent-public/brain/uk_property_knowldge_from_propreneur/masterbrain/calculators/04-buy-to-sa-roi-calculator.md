# Buy-to-SA (Serviced Accommodation) ROI Calculator

## Overview

Serviced Accommodation (SA) operates on nightly rates rather than monthly tenancies. Revenue is highly variable based on occupancy rate, nightly rate, and seasonality. SA can be operated as:
- **Buy-to-SA** - purchase the property, operate as SA
- **BRR-to-SA** - buy, refurb to add value, refinance, then operate as SA
- **R2R-to-SA** - rent a property from a landlord and sublet as SA (lowest capital required)

---

## Core SA Formulas

### Monthly Revenue
```
Monthly Revenue = Average Nightly Rate × Nights Available × Occupancy Rate
```

### SA ROI (Self-Managed)
```
Monthly Cashflow = Monthly Revenue - Monthly Operating Costs
Annual Cashflow = Monthly Cashflow × 12
ROI (%) = Annual Cashflow / Total Investment × 100
```

### SA ROI (Managed - using SA management company)
```
Management Fee = Monthly Revenue × 15% (+ VAT where applicable)
Monthly Cashflow (Managed) = Monthly Revenue - Management Fee - Other Costs
```

---

## Occupancy Rate Framework

**Minimum viable occupancy:** 50% - never take on a property that does not break even at 50% occupancy.

| Occupancy % | Notes |
|---|---|
| 50% | Absolute minimum - break-even threshold |
| 60-65% | Conservative realistic target |
| 70-75% | Good performance |
| 80-85% | High performance |
| 90%+ | Exceptional - major tourist/contractor hotspot |

**SA Pricing Philosophy:**
- Weekday rate (20 days/month): typically lower to attract contractors/corporate
- Weekend rate (8-10 days/month): higher for leisure/tourist stays
- Blended nightly rate = weighted average across both

## R2R-to-SA Model

### Structure
- Agree fixed rent with landlord
- Pay bills, operate as SA
- Profit on the nightly rate margin

### R2R SA Example
```
Rent to Landlord:     £750/month
Startup Costs:
  Deposit (5 weeks):  £900

  Renovation:         £500
```

## SA Performance Benchmarks

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum break-even occupancy | 50% | Hard rule - walk away if below this |
| Target average occupancy | 65-75% | Realistic for most UK markets |
| Nightly rate: contractor market | £80-£175/night | Weekday focus |
| Nightly rate: tourist market | £100-£300/night | Weekend focus |
| Management fee: SA operator | 15-20% of revenue | Some charge per clean instead |
| Booking channel commission | 3-15% | Airbnb ~15%, direct bookings 0% |
| SA furnishing budget | £3,000-£8,000 | Per property depending on size/standard |
| Setup fee (management company) | £1,500-£2,500 | One-time |

## Key Decision Rules for SA

1. Check AirDNA for your target area before committing
2. Only proceed if break-even at 50% occupancy
3. Model both self-managed and managed scenarios
4. Factor SA setup costs: furnishing, professional photography, management onboarding
5. SA requires active management - it is NOT passive like a standard BTL
6. SA mortgage/insurance products differ from BTL - confirm with broker before purchasing
7. Councils in some areas are restricting SA via Article 4 directions - always check planning
