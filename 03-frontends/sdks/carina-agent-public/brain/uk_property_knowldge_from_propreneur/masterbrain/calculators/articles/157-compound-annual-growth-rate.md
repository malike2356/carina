# Compound Annual Growth Rate

Slug: compound-annual-growth-rate

If you are investing in UK property, you will come across Compound Annual Growth Rate sooner or later. It is one of those metrics that sounds technical but is actually straightforward once you break it down.

**The formula**

```
CAGR (%) = ((Current Value / Purchase Price)^(1 / Years Held) - 1) × 100
```

**Why this matters**

Understanding Compound Annual Growth Rate gives you a clearer picture of your investments. The investors who take the time to learn these calculations consistently outperform those who rely on gut feel.
