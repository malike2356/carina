# Portfolio LTV: Your Overall Borrowing Position

Published: Wed, 06 Oct 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: portfolio-ltv-your-overall-borrowing-position

---

Portfolio LTV is the total of all your mortgages divided by the total value of all your properties. It measures your overall leverage position and determines your ability to borrow more or refinance.\n\nIf your portfolio is worth £1.5 million with total mortgages of £900,000, your portfolio LTV is 60%. That is a comfortable position. Most lenders will extend further credit. If the portfolio LTV rises to 80%, you are over-leveraged and most lenders will decline further borrowing.\n\nPortfolio LTV is the number that determines whether you can grow. If your LTV is 70% or higher, you need to de-leverage before buying more properties. If it is below 50%, you have significant capacity to add leverage.\n
Monitor your portfolio LTV at least annually. If property values fall, your LTV rises. If you have properties with high LTV, consider whether you need to sell one to protect the rest of the portfolio.

**The formula**

```
Portfolio LTV (%) = (Total Mortgage Debt / Total Portfolio Value) × 100
```

**Why this matters**

Portfolio LTV of 50% to 65% gives you room to grow. Above 70%, you are over-leveraged. Monitor it annually and adjust your strategy based on the number.
