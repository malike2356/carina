# Discounted Payback Period: Payback That Accounts for the Time Value of Money

Published: Wed, 01 Sep 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: discounted-payback-period-payback-that-accounts-for-the-time-value-of-

---

Discounted payback period improves on standard payback by discounting future cashflows back to today's value. It tells you how long until the discounted cashflows equal your investment. Because discounted cashflows are smaller, the discounted payback period is always longer than the standard payback period.\n\nFor a BTL with £70,000 invested and £3,000 annual cashflow discounted at 8%, the first year's discounted cashflow is £2,778, not £3,000. The payback period at this rate is no longer 23.3 years. It becomes theoretically infinite because the discounted cashflows never reach the initial investment. The property never pays back in today's money.\n\nThis is a sobering insight. Many properties that look cashflow positive on a simple basis never pay back in discounted terms. The longer the payback period, the more the discounting erodes the value.\n
Discounted payback is a conservative filter. If a property does not pay back within 20 years on a discounted basis, the investment is heavily dependent on capital growth and terminal value. That is a high-risk position.

**The formula**

```
Find T: Σ[CF(t) / (1 + r)^t] = 0 for t = 0 to T
```

**Why this matters**

Discounted payback period is more realistic than standard payback. Many properties that appear cashflow positive never pay back in discounted terms. It is a conservative filter that reveals heavy dependence on capital growth.
