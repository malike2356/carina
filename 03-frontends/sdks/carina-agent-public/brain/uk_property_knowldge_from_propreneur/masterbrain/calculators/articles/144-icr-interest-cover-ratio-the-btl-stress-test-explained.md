# ICR: Interest Cover Ratio -- The BTL Stress Test Explained

Published: Wed, 29 Sep 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: icr-interest-cover-ratio-the-btl-stress-test-explained

---

ICR is similar to DSCR but specifically measures interest cover rather than total debt service. It is your net rental income divided by your annual interest payments. UK BTL lenders use ICR as their primary affordability test.\n\nFor a BTL with rent of £12,000 and annual interest of £9,000, the ICR is 1.33. That means the rent covers the interest 1.33 times. The lender's typical minimum is 1.25 for basic rate taxpayers and 1.45 for higher rate taxpayers.\n\nThe ICR test is applied at a stressed interest rate, typically 5.5% or the pay rate plus 1%. This means even if your actual rate is 4.5%, the lender tests at 5.5% to ensure you can afford the mortgage when the fix ends.\n
ICR is the biggest barrier to BTL lending in the current market. If your ICR is below the lender's minimum, your only options are a larger deposit, a cheaper property, or a lender with a lower stress rate.

**The formula**

```
ICR = Annual Rent / Annual Interest Charge
```

**Why this matters**

ICR is the primary BTL affordability test. Most lenders require 1.25 for basic rate taxpayers and 1.45 for higher rate. The test is applied at a stressed rate. Know your ICR before you apply for a mortgage.
