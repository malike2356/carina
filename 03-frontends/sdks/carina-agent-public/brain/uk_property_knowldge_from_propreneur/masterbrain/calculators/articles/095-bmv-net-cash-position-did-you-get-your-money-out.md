# BMV Net Cash Position: Did You Get Your Money Out?

Published: Mon, 12 Apr 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: bmv-net-cash-position-did-you-get-your-money-out

---

The net cash position is the final check on any BMV or no-money-down deal. It is the remortgage proceeds minus your total acquisition cost. A positive number means cash out. A negative number means cash left in.\n\nIf your total acquisition cost is £165,000 (£160,000 purchase plus £5,000 for stamp duty, legal, and fees) and your remortgage proceeds are £150,000 (75% LTV on £200,000), your net cash position is negative £15,000. You still have £15,000 of your cash in the deal.\n\nBut if your discount was 25% and you bought at £150,000 with costs of £5,000, your total is £155,000. Remortgage proceeds at 75% LTV on £200,000 are £150,000. Your net cash position is negative £5,000. Much better.\n
To achieve cash out, you need the remortgage proceeds to exceed the total cost. That means either a deep enough discount, a high enough LTV on the remortgage, or both.\n
Net cash position is the number that determines whether a deal is genuinely no-money-down or just a discount purchase with some equity. Know this number before you start the process.

**The formula**

```
Net Cash = Remortgage Proceeds - Purchase Price - Bridging Fee - Legal Fees - Valuation Fee - SDLT
```

**Why this matters**

Net cash position tells you if your BMV deal is truly no-money-down. If the remortgage proceeds do not fully cover your costs, you still have cash in the deal. Positive cash position is rare but achievable with deep discounts.
