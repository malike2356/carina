# RevPAR: Revenue Per Available Room-Night -- The Hotel Industry Standard for SA Performance

Published: Mon, 04 Jan 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: revpar-revenue-per-available-room-night-the-hotel-industry-standard-fo

---

RevPAR is the metric that hotels use and SA operators should use. It combines ADR and occupancy into a single number. It answers the question: for every night the property is available, how much revenue do I generate?

RevPAR is your total revenue divided by the total number of nights your property was available. If your annual revenue is £28,105 and the property was available for 365 nights (minus any nights you blocked for personal use), your RevPAR is £28,105 divided by 365, which equals £77.

Compare two scenarios. Scenario one: ADR of £120 with 60% occupancy gives a RevPAR of £72. Scenario two: ADR of £100 with 75% occupancy gives a RevPAR of £75. Despite the higher rate in scenario one, scenario two generates more total revenue because the higher occupancy more than compensates for the lower rate.

RevPAR helps you make this trade-off decision. If lowering your rate increases RevPAR, the discount is working. If lowering your rate decreases RevPAR, the discount is not worth it.

In Portsmouth, a good RevPAR for a well-run SA property is £65 to £110. Below £50, your property is significantly underperforming and you need to address either pricing, marketing, or the quality of the listing.

**The formula**

```
RevPAR = Total Revenue / Total Available Nights
       = ADR × Occupancy Rate
```

**Why this matters**

RevPAR is the single most important SA metric because it combines ADR and occupancy. A good RevPAR for Portsmouth is £65 to £110. Below £50, you have a problem that needs addressing.
