# Rent-to-Rent Net Margin: The Percentage That Tells You How Efficient Your Operation Is

Published: Mon, 14 Dec 2026 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: rent-to-rent-net-margin-the-percentage-that-tells-you-how-efficient-yo

---

Net margin is your annual profit divided by your annual tenant income, expressed as a percentage. It tells you how efficiently you run your R2R operation.

If your annual tenant income is £19,800 and your annual profit after all costs and voids is £3,825, your net margin is 19.3%. That means for every £1 your tenants pay, you keep roughly 19p.

A net margin of 15% to 25% is typical for a well-run R2R operation. Below 15%, the risk of making a loss with one bad month is too high. Above 25%, you are running a tight ship and probably deserve more properties.

The difference between a 15% margin and a 25% margin on the same portfolio is significant. On an R2R portfolio turning over £100,000 a year in tenant income, a 15% margin gives you £15,000 profit. A 25% margin gives you £25,000. The extra £10,000 is the difference between a side hustle and a serious business.

If your margin is below 15%, look at your costs. Utilities, management, and maintenance are the three biggest levers. Can you negotiate better utility rates? Can you reduce management by self-managing? Can you reduce maintenance through better tenant screening?

**The formula**

```
Net Margin (%) = (Annual Profit / Annual Subtenant Income) × 100
Annual Subtenant Income = g × 12
```

**Why this matters**

Rent-to-rent net margin should be 15% to 25%. Below 15%, the risk-reward ratio is unattractive. Above 25%, you have an efficient operation worth scaling.
