# Development Hold Exit: Refinancing Instead of Selling

Published: Wed, 26 May 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: development-hold-exit-refinancing-instead-of-selling

---

Instead of selling the completed development, you can refinance and hold the units as rental investments. This defers the tax, provides ongoing income, and lets you benefit from future capital growth.\n\nThe refinance is based on the GDV. At 75% LTV on a £560,000 GDV, you can refinance £420,000. Your total project cost was £480,802. The refinance proceeds of £420,000 leave you £60,802 still in the deal. But you now own two flats worth £560,000 with mortgages of £420,000. Your equity is £140,000.\n\nIf the flats generate rental income, the mortgage payments might be covered by the rent. Your cash left in of £60,802 starts generating rental returns. Over time, as rents increase and the mortgage balance reduces, your equity grows.\n\nThe hold exit avoids the sales costs (saving £16,600) and defers the tax. You only pay tax when you eventually sell. The trade-off is ongoing management, tenant risk, and capital that is tied up rather than recycled.\n\nFor developers who want to build a portfolio, the hold exit is often better than selling. For developers who need to recycle capital into the next project, selling is usually the right choice.

**The formula**

```
Hold Refinance LTV: 70% (stressed)
Hold Mortgage = GDV × 70%
Annual Mortgage Cost = Hold Mortgage × Stressed Rate (5.5% IO)
Net Annual Cashflow = (Gross Annual Rent × 0.80) - Annual Mortgage Cost
```

**Why this matters**

Development hold exit defers tax, saves sales costs, and provides ongoing rental income. But it ties up your capital. Compare the net returns of selling versus holding before you decide.
