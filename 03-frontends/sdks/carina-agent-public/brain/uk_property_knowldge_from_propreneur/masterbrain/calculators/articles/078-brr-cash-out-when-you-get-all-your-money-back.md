# BRR Cash Out: When You Get All Your Money Back

Published: Wed, 10 Feb 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: brr-cash-out-when-you-get-all-your-money-back

---

Cash out is the BRR dream. You invest £196,950, refinance at a valuation high enough that the bank gives you £196,950 or more, and your cash is fully returned. The property is now owned with none of your own money in it.\n\nTo achieve cash out, you need an ARV that supports refinance proceeds equal to or greater than your total cost. With total costs of £196,950 and a 75% LTV, you need an ARV of £262,600. That is a £112,600 uplift from a £150,000 purchase price.\n\nThat level of uplift is achievable but requires finding a property that is significantly undervalued in its current condition. Typically this means a property that needs substantial refurbishment, is in a location where refurb adds significant value, or is being sold by a motivated seller at below market price.\n\nCash out is rare but possible. A typical good BRR returns 70% to 90% of your cash. Cash out on your first deal should not be the target. Focus on reducing cash left in to a level that makes your ROE attractive.\n\nIf you can get cash left in down to 10% of ARV with a 10% net yield on the remaining cash, you have a strong BRR deal. The cash out comes later as the property appreciates and you refinance again.

**The formula**

```
Cash Out = Refinance Proceeds - Total Cost
```

**Why this matters**

Cash out is the BRR ideal but not the realistic target. A good BRR should return 70% to 90% of your cash. If you can get 90% back on your first deal, you are doing well.
