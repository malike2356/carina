# VAT on Commercial Property: When You Charge It and When You Do Not

Published: Wed, 05 Jan 2028 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: vat-on-commercial-property-when-you-charge-it-and-when-you-do-not

---

VAT on commercial property is more complex than residential because commercial properties can be elected for VAT. The election, known as opting to tax, makes the rent VAT-able. Commercial landlords can charge VAT on rent and recover VAT on their own costs.\n\nIf you buy a commercial property and opt to tax, you can reclaim the VAT on your purchase price, legal fees, and refurbishment costs. But you must charge VAT on the rent to your tenants. If your tenant is VAT-registered, they can reclaim the VAT, so it is neutral for them. If the tenant is not VAT-registered, the VAT adds 20% to their rent, which could make your property uncompetitive.\n\nThe decision to opt to tax depends on your tenant base. If your tenants are all VAT-registered businesses, opting to tax is usually beneficial because you can reclaim VAT on your costs. If your tenants include charities, schools, or other non-VAT-registered organisations, it might be better to not opt to tax.

**The formula**

```
VAT on Purchase = Purchase Price × 20% (if opted to tax)
```

**Why this matters**

VAT on commercial property depends on whether you have opted to tax. Opting lets you reclaim VAT on costs but must charge it on rent. It works best when all your tenants are VAT-registered businesses.
