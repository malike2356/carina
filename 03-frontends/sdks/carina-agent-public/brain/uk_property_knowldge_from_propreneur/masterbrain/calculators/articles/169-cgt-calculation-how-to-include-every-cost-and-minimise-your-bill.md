# CGT Calculation: How to Include Every Cost and Minimise Your Bill

Published: Mon, 27 Dec 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: cgt-calculation-how-to-include-every-cost-and-minimise-your-bill

---

The CGT calculation is straightforward but requires accurate records. The chargeable gain is your net sale proceeds minus your allowable base cost minus your allowable improvement costs. Every cost you can document potentially reduces your tax bill.\n\nYour allowable base cost includes the purchase price, SDLT, legal fees on purchase, survey fees, and mortgage arrangement fees. If you bought a property for £200,000 with £15,000 in costs, your base cost is £215,000.\n\nAllowable improvement costs are capital improvements that increase the property's value. A new kitchen, a loft conversion, or an extension counts. Painting and general maintenance does not. Keep receipts for every capital improvement. If you spend £30,000 on a loft conversion and sell five years later, that £30,000 reduces your CGT bill.\n
The best way to minimise CGT is to keep meticulous records from day one. When you eventually sell, every pound of documented cost is a pound of gain that is not taxable.

**The formula**

```
Allowable Base Cost = Purchase Price + SDLT + Legal Buy + Survey
Allowable Improvement Costs = Capital Improvements (not repairs)
Net Gain = Sale Price - Allowable Base Cost - Allowable Improvement Costs - Selling Costs
```

**Why this matters**

CGT is calculated on the gain after allowable costs. Keep records of every purchase cost and capital improvement. A well-documented cost base can reduce your CGT bill by thousands.
