# Expense Ratio: How Much of Your Rental Income Goes to Costs?

Published: Wed, 17 Nov 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: expense-ratio-how-much-of-your-rental-income-goes-to-costs

---

The expense ratio is your total operating expenses divided by your gross rental income. It tells you how efficiently your properties run. A low expense ratio means more income stays in your pocket. A high ratio means costs are eating your returns.\n\nFor a BTL with £12,000 gross rent and £3,600 in operating expenses, the expense ratio is 30%. For an HMO with higher costs, the ratio might be 45% to 55%. For a serviced accommodation property, it can be 55% to 70%.\n\nThe expense ratio is a useful efficiency benchmark. If your BTL expense ratio is above 40%, your costs are too high. Review your management fees, maintenance costs, and insurance. Can any of them be reduced?\n
Compare your expense ratio to local averages. If your ratio is significantly higher than comparable properties, look for the cause. It might be an inefficient letting agent, an older property requiring more maintenance, or simply bad budgeting.

**The formula**

```
Expense Ratio (%) = (Total Operating Expenses / Gross Rental Income) × 100
```

**Why this matters**

BTL expense ratio should be 25% to 35%. HMO and SA ratios are higher due to more costs. If your expense ratio exceeds 40% for a BTL, look for cost savings.
