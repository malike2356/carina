# HMO Effective Gross Rent: The Income After Room Voids

Published: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: hmo-effective-gross-rent-the-income-after-room-voids

---

Gross potential rent assumes every room is full every month. Effective gross rent adjusts for the reality that rooms will be empty between tenancies.

For a five-bed HMO with rooms at £600 each monthly, your gross potential rent is £3,000. But if each room is empty for two weeks between tenants, and the average tenancy lasts six months, you lose roughly 8% of your income to voids. Your effective gross rent is £2,760 a month, or £33,120 a year.

The difference of £240 a month is £2,880 a year. That is real money that needs to be in your budget. HMO voids are more predictable than single let voids because they happen more frequently but are shorter. You can model them accurately based on your local market.

In student areas of Portsmouth, the void pattern is predictable. Rooms are full from September to June, with a summer void as students leave. A well-managed HMO will pre-let rooms for September, keeping summer voids to two to three months on the full property, or less if you stagger lease dates.

For professional HMOs, voids are shorter but less predictable. A typical professional HMO room might be empty for two to four weeks between tenants. With gross rent of £600 per room, that is £150 to £300 in lost rent per void per room.

The key to HMO profitability is minimising void time. Pre-letting, good marketing, and responsive maintenance all reduce the time rooms sit empty.

**The formula**

```
Void Allowance = Rent per Room × Void Months per Year × Rooms
Effective Gross Rent = Gross Potential Rent - Void Allowance
```

**Why this matters**

HMO effective gross rent is your real income after room voids. Student HMOs have predictable summer voids. Professional HMOs have shorter but less predictable voids. The difference is significant and must be budgeted for.
