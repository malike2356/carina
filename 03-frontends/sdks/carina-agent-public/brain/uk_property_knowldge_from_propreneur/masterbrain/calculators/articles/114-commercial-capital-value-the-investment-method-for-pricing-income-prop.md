# Commercial Capital Value: The Investment Method for Pricing Income Property

Published: Wed, 16 Jun 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: commercial-capital-value-the-investment-method-for-pricing-income-prop

---

The investment method is the standard way to value commercial property. It values the property based on its income, not comparable sales. The formula is simple: income divided by yield equals capital value.\n\nIf a property generates £25,000 in net income and the market yield for similar properties is 7%, the capital value is £25,000 divided by 0.07, which equals £357,143. That is the investment method valuation.\n\nThis is fundamentally different from residential valuation, where comparable sales are the primary method. In commercial, the income drives the value. If the income changes, the value changes. If the market yield changes, the value changes.\n\nThe investment method is used for most commercial property types: retail, office, industrial, and leisure. It is less useful for specialised properties like hotels, care homes, and petrol stations, which use different valuation approaches.\n\nThe method works both ways. If an agent tells you a property is worth £500,000, you can work backwards to check the implied yield. If the net income is £30,000, the implied yield is 6%. Compare that to market yields for similar properties. If the market is 7%, the property is overpriced.

**The formula**

```
Capital Value = Net Income / Target Yield (as decimal)
```

**A worked example**

£50,000 NOI at 7.5% yield = £50,000 / 0.075 = **£666,667**

**Why this matters**

The investment method is the primary commercial valuation approach. Income divided by yield equals value. It lets you quickly assess whether any commercial property is fairly priced relative to the market.
