# Future Value: What Your Property Will Be Worth Tomorrow

Published: Mon, 13 Sep 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: future-value-what-your-property-will-be-worth-tomorrow

---

Future value is the opposite of present value. It tells you what an amount today will be worth in the future, assuming a specific growth rate. For property investors, it answers the question: what will my £250,000 property be worth in ten years if prices grow at 3% per year?\n\nThe calculation is the present value multiplied by one plus the growth rate to the power of the number of years. £250,000 times 1.03 to the power of 10 equals £250,000 times 1.344, which equals £336,000. That is the future value after ten years at 3% annual growth.\n\nFuture value is a critical assumption in every BTL investment. If properties grow at 2% instead of 3%, the future value drops to £304,750. That difference of £31,250 is significant.\n
When underwriting a property investment, model future value at different growth rates. If the investment only works at 4% or higher growth, it is a speculative bet, not a conservative investment. Assume 2% to 3% growth for South Coast property.

**The formula**

```
FV = PV × (1 + r)^n
```

**Why this matters**

Future value is a critical assumption in property investing. Small changes in growth rates create large changes in future value. Be conservative. Assume 2% to 3% growth for South Coast property.
