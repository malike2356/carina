# Present Value of an Annuity: Valuing a Fixed Income Stream

Published: Wed, 08 Sep 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: present-value-of-an-annuity-valuing-a-fixed-income-stream

---

An annuity is a fixed income stream that lasts for a specified period. In property, it represents the value of rental income over a fixed hold period. The present value of an annuity is the annual payment multiplied by a factor that depends on the discount rate and the number of years.\n\nFor ten years of £12,000 annual income discounted at 8%, the annuity factor is roughly 6.71. The present value is £12,000 times 6.71, which equals £80,520. That is what ten years of income is worth today at 8%.\n\nThe annuity formula is useful for valuing income streams with a fixed end date. If you plan to sell a BTL after ten years, the annuity value of the income plus the present value of the sale proceeds gives you the total investment value.\n
The annuity factor decreases as the discount rate increases and as the number of years decreases. A 5% discount rate over 15 years gives a factor of 10.38. An 8% discount rate over five years gives a factor of 3.99. The shorter the period or the higher the required return, the lower the present value.

**The formula**

```
PV = CF × [1 - (1 + r)^(-n)] / r
```

**Why this matters**

The annuity formula values a fixed income stream over a specific period. It is useful for BTL analysis where you plan to sell after a defined hold period. Combine it with terminal value for a full DCF.
