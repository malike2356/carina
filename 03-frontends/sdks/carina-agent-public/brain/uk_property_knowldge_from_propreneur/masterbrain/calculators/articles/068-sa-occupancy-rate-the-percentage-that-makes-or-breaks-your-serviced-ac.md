# SA Occupancy Rate: The Percentage That Makes or Breaks Your Serviced Accommodation

Published: Wed, 06 Jan 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: sa-occupancy-rate-the-percentage-that-makes-or-breaks-your-serviced-ac

---

Occupancy rate is the simplest metric in serviced accommodation and the most powerful. It is the number of nights you sold divided by the number of nights you were available.\n\nFor a Portsmouth SA with 256 booked nights out of 365 available, your occupancy rate is 70%. That is good. Below 60%, you are underperforming. Above 80%, you are in the top tier of SA operators.\n\nThe challenge with occupancy is that it is not independent of ADR. Lower your price and occupancy goes up. Raise your price and occupancy goes down. The sweet spot is where your total revenue is maximised, not where occupancy is highest.\n\nA common mistake is celebrating 90% occupancy without realising you could have achieved the same revenue at 70% occupancy with higher rates and less wear and tear. The goal is RevPAR, not occupancy.\n\nIn Portsmouth, occupancy varies significantly by season. Summer months can hit 90% to 95%. Winter months might fall to 40% to 50%. The annual average is what matters. Anything above 65% annual occupancy for a Portsmouth SA property is respectable.\n\nIf your occupancy is consistently below 60%, check your pricing, your listing quality, your photos, and your reviews. One of these is probably holding you back.

**The formula**

```
Occupancy Rate (%) = (Nights Booked / Total Available Nights) × 100
```

**Why this matters**

SA occupancy below 60% is underperforming. Above 80% is exceptional. But do not chase occupancy at the expense of ADR. Maximise RevPAR, not bookings.
