# BRR Gross Yield: What the Property Delivers After Your Improvements

Published: Wed, 24 Feb 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: brr-gross-yield-what-the-property-delivers-after-your-improvements

---

After you have refurbished and refinanced, the property should be producing income. The gross yield post-refurb tells you how well the finished product performs.\n\nWith an ARV of £220,000 and a post-refurb rent of £1,100 a month, your gross yield is 6%. That is decent for the South Coast. If you achieved a lower ARV or lower rent, the yield would be lower.\n\nThe BRR gross yield should be compared to the market for similar properties. If comparable properties in the area yield 5%, your 6% is good. But if the market yields 7%, your refurb has not added enough value relative to the price.\n\nThe yield also affects your refinance options. Lenders look at the yield when deciding whether to approve the remortgage. If the yield is below 5% in the current market, some lenders may decline because they see the property as overvalued relative to its income.\n\nA post-refurb gross yield of 5.5% to 7% is the sweet spot for South Coast BRR. Below 5%, the yield is too low to justify the refurb risk. Above 7%, you have found a special deal.

**The formula**

```
Gross Yield (%) = (Monthly Rent × 12 / Post-Refurb Value) × 100
```

**Why this matters**

BRR post-refurb gross yield of 5.5% to 7% is the sweet spot on the South Coast. Below 5%, the yield is too low. Above 7%, you have a strong deal. Lenders also use yield in their approval decision.
