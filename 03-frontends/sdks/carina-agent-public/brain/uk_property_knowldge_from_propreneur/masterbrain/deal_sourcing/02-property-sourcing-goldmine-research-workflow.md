# Property Sourcing: Goldmine Patch & Deal Discovery

**Source:** TENX Property Sourcing Manual Module 5
**Purpose:** How to research and identify target investment areas and find viable property deals
**Audience:** Carina can guide a sourcer through area research, deal types, and the 5-minute due diligence protocol

---

## The Goldmine Patch

Pick an area approximately 1 hour from where you live (you'll visit it often). Use PropertyData.co.uk for research:

**Research workflow:**
1. Go to PropertyData.co.uk -> "Research" tab -> "Local Data"
2. Enter target postcode
3. Expand search to include 2,000-3,000 data points for statistical significance
4. Metrics to pull: population density, property ownership rates, rents (average + per room), average purchase price, demographics, crime stats, education levels, average price per square metre, average yields, area growth, property value growth, number of transactions, average time to sell, average time to rent, political alliance, average household income, age profile
5. Download branded PDFs from PropertyData to share with investors
6. Confirm rental values independently by calling local letting agents

## Types of Deals to Find

**BRRR (Buy Refurbish Refinance Rent):** Most popular. Price range £80k-£200k. Below £80k = not enough profit. Above £200k = fewer buyers. Look for the ugliest, dirtiest, smelliest properties (opposite of BTL search). Signs: multiple coloured wallpapers, poor condition.

**HMO (House in Multiple Occupation):**
- Minimum £100k (easier to mortgage). Prefer under £300k.
- Check SpareRoom.co.uk for supply/demand in target area.
- Check council website for Article 4 direction (restricts new HMO conversions).
- Google "HMO rules [area]" for local council requirements.
- Lenders value bricks-and-mortar only (not rental income potential). Watch for vendors pricing rental income into the asking price.
- Minimum room sizes: bedroom minimums enforced by council (e.g. 70 sqft).
- Fire doors required in most/all interior doors.

**BTL (Buy to Let):**
- Safe but "boring" compared to BRRR/HMO.
- Minimum 15% ROI target.
- 3-bed houses preferred (flats have high service charges and harder to mortgage).
- Price range: £50k-£150k.
- Look for good condition properties that will rent easily.

## Finding a Deal (Rightmove Workflow)

1. Rightmove: search 3 miles from target area.
2. Filter: min £50k, max £150k (or appropriate range), 3 beds, houses only.
3. Look for good condition (BTL) or poor condition (BRRR).
4. Check location map for amenities: hospitals, schools, industry, shopping, transport.
5. Check Sold House Prices nearby for comparables.
6. Find postcode -> PropertyData "Valuation" section.
7. Confirm rental value on Rightmove, Zoopla, OpenRent.
8. Calculate ROI: (monthly rent minus monthly costs) x 12 / total invested x 100 = ROI%.
9. Create shortlist -> phone agent -> if available, book viewing.

## Five-Minute Due Diligence Protocol (Before Viewing)

Before viewing, do ONLY this quick analysis:
1. Know area metrics from Goldmine research (you should already have this).
2. Rough refurb cost estimate from memory (see below).
3. Check if property is viable ON PAPER only.
4. Phone agent to confirm availability.
5. Book viewing. Don't spend hours on deep DD before viewing.

**Rough refurb costs (3-bed properties):**
- Carpets: £1,200-£1,500
- Painting (whole house, 3-4 bed): £1,000-£1,500
- Standard interior doors (fitted): ~£150 each
- Fire doors (fitted): ~£300 each
- Always overestimate costs.

## Valuing a Property

RICS valuation is the only recognised standard for lenders. Use Red Book approach:
- Compare like-for-like: same structure, size, location, amenities.
- Tools: PropertyData, Land Insight, Rightmove Sold House Prices.
- Call local estate agents (say you're BUYING, not selling -- they inflate valuations when selling).
- Present FACTS, not opinion. Download branded PDFs as evidence.

## Good Deal Example -- Bedford HMO

- Purchase price: £210,000 (from £220k asking, offered £180k rejected)
- Initial quote from housing association contractor: £29,805 + VAT (over-inflated)
- Actual repair cost: under £5,000 (experience identified the inflated quote -- 43 other viewers walked away)
- Total refurb: £68,000 (over budget due to material cost increases + builder went into liquidation)
- Total investment: £280,025
- Refinance valuation: £400,000 (3 local agents agreed)
- Refinance at 75% LTV: ~£260,000 after fees
- HMO income: 5 rooms @ £650/room = £3,250 gross, ~£2,000/month profit after costs
- Stamp duty: £8,000 (reclaimed 100% -- property was uninhabitable at purchase)
- Bridging finance: 0.89%/month. Private lender: £45,000 at 1%/month
