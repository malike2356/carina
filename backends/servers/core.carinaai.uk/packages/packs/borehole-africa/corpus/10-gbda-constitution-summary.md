# GBDA Constitution Summary

Ghana Borehole Drillers Association (GBDA) governs professional conduct for licensed drillers.

## Membership

Open to registered drilling companies and individual drillers operating in Ghana.
Membership dues required for active status. Lapsed members lose voting rights.

## Meetings and Quorum

- General meeting quorum: minimum 10 members for valid votes
- Committee meeting quorum: minimum 3 committee members
- AGM requires 14 days written notice before scheduled date

## Elections

- Officer term: 2 years; non-renewable without election
- Nomination requires signatures from 2 existing members
- No candidate may contest two positions in the same election

## Disciplinary Rules

- Expulsion requires majority vote at a general meeting (not committee alone)
- Committee may suspend pending general meeting decision
- Expelled members lose GBDA badge and marketplace trust signal on ABBIS

## Pricing Governance

GBDA publishes regional drilling rate benchmarks (see corpus/07-drilling-prices-by-region.md).
2022 Greater Accra benchmark: GHS 4,200 for 90m (GHS 46.67/m).
Rates are guidance; members should not undercut below sustainable industry levels.

Tier 4 Association subscribers manage GBDA registry through ABBIS association module.
GBDA member badge displays on verified member marketplace profiles.
