## Part 1: Business Structures and Tax

### The "Why Before Structure" Principle

The single most common mistake property investors make is choosing a business structure based on short-term tax efficiency without first defining their long-term goals.

Choosing a structure is like choosing a turn at a roundabout - you cannot decide which way to turn until you know where you are going. The structure should serve the long-term destination, not the other way around.

**Questions to answer before choosing a structure:**
1. Why are you doing this? What is your actual goal?
2. What does your portfolio look like in 10-20 years?
3. Do you want to pass wealth to children or grandchildren?
4. Will you stay UK resident indefinitely?
5. How many strategies will you pursue (investment only, or also development, deal sourcing, flipping)?
6. Do you need the income now, or are you building for the future?

### Tax Bracket Context

For a basic rate taxpayer with modest income who needs rental income to supplement a pension: buying in personal ownership is often right. Rental income is taxed at 20%, no need to pay corporation tax then dividend tax to extract money from a company.

For a high earner (£200k+/year) building a portfolio to retire on passive income: personal ownership may again be right for the investment period, because when they retire and income drops, they will be a basic rate taxpayer anyway.

The structure is always subordinate to the goal.

### Group Structure Design Thinking

When you will eventually want a group structure (holding company + subsidiaries), design your first company now to look like what the holding company will eventually look like:
- Choose the right SIC codes
- Draft articles of association that mirror the intended group structure
- When creating subsidiaries later, restructuring work is minimal

The cost of designing backward is high - capital gains tax, stamp duty, and legal fees on corporate restructuring can run to tens of thousands.

### Family Investment Companies (FICs)

The FIC is legitimate tax planning used for generations by wealthy families. HMRC investigated it exhaustively and confirmed it in 2021. It is not a scheme.

**But:**
- Setup costs are £20,000-£25,000 in specialist legal fees. Bespoke articles, shareholder agreements, and director service contracts required
- Standard templates are unsuitable and create problems
- One case: £10,000 poorly drafted FIC required £100,000 in corrective work
- Wait until your portfolio is worth £2-3M before establishing a FIC, but plan for it from the start

### Compliance Environment

HMRC is not going to become more relaxed. The trend is toward more transparency and enforcement:
- HMRC Connect links Land Registry, bank accounts, credit cards, letting agent reports, and social media
- Making Tax Digital will eventually allow HMRC to pull data directly from accounting software
- Cheap compliance is not compliance - 97% of accountants file returns; 3% advise proactively
- HMRC investigations can look back 20 years in deliberate non-disclosure cases. Penalties can be 100% of tax owed

### Company Articles of Association

Sole directors using model articles (government template) face a legal risk: a 2022 case found a sole director had acted ultra vires - making decisions they were not legally empowered to make. At least one lender has refused to proceed with a mortgage shortly before completion because the borrower used unmodified model articles. Get articles reviewed and amended.

## Part 2: Property Development

### The CEO Mindset

The most valuable mental shift in property development: you are the CEO, not the builder.

You find the deal. You assemble the professional team. You manage the project manager. You make strategic decisions. You do not lay foundations or wire electrics.

Transferable skills that matter in development: organisation, management, decision-making, people skills. An IT manager, nurse, teacher, or lorry driver can all become property developers using the same skills they already have.

### The Eight Pillars of Business (Richie Clapton's framework)

1. Strategy
2. Business and Structure
3. Marketing
4. Sales
5. Customer Service
6. Operations
7. People and Resources
8. Finance and Profitability

Every pillar must be covered. If one underperforms, it drags all others down.

### Development Funding - It Is About the Deal

Development finance lenders assess:
1. The deal (primary) - does GDV, costs, and margin stack up?
2. Professional team experience
3. Two exit strategies (minimum)
4. Developer experience - for new developers, this is covered by the team's track record
5. Developer equity and assets/liabilities

Your personal income, payslips, or employment status are largely irrelevant. A first-time developer who has never owned a property can access development finance if the deal stacks and the team is credible.

Use a specialist commercial funding broker - not a high street bank.

### Two Exits Minimum - Always

Before starting any development, have at least two exits planned:

**Exit 1 - Sell:** Put completed units on the market. Target the need market (1-3 bedroom units). There is always demand.

**Exit 2 - Hold and Refinance:** If the sales market is soft, refinance onto BTL/commercial mortgages. Rent until conditions improve, then sell or continue holding.

**Exit 3 - Supported Housing:** Work with a specialist supported housing provider. Agree specification before build starts. Sign a 10-20 year FRI lease before groundworks begin. Requires longer lead time but genuinely guaranteed.

### The Need Market vs the Want Market

**Need market:** 1, 2, and 3 bedroom homes. People need somewhere to live. Demand is structural and persistent.

**Want market:** 4+ bedroom homes, luxury units, large houses. Discretionary demand, highly sensitive to economic conditions.

Build for the need market in uncertain conditions. You will sell or rent faster and have a wider buyer/tenant pool.

### Fenovo Case Study - IT Manager to Developer

- IT manager enrolled on Property CEO programme
- Met mentor Richie Clapton at a PIN meeting in Swindon
- First project: old Gospel Hall complex in Swindon - 7 residential units. Found a 3-bed apartment in roof space others had dismissed. Refinanced and holds as SA.
- Second project: old antique shop - larger conversion. Projected profit £400k+
- Third project: 15-16 residential + 4-5 commercial units
- Combined profit across first two projects: £650k+
- Still works full-time in IT
- Timeline from first meeting to third project started: under 2.5 years

Lesson: follow a proven framework consistently. Have a mentor on site. Do not improvise.

### Permitted Development Rights (PDR) Opportunity

Many commercial buildings (shops, banks, gyms, offices, cafes) can be converted to residential under PDR (Class MA, Class Q, Class P) without full planning permission. Prior Approval is faster, cheaper, and more certain than full planning.

Empty commercial buildings are everywhere - former banks, redundant shops, unused gyms, empty offices. Check for Article 4 Directions (council restrictions) before assuming PDR applies.

### Mentor Value in Development

A mentor who has done hundreds of projects can see what you cannot see. Richie identified a 3-bed apartment in a roof space the original architect said was impossible. That extra unit changed the economics of the entire deal. Find experienced mentors - the cost of mentorship is always less than the cost of mistakes they help you avoid.

## Recurring Themes (All Speakers)

1. **Treat property as a business, not a hobby.** Section 24, HMRC Connect, and increasing compliance requirements push casual landlords toward professional structures.

2. **Know where you are going before you choose your tools.** Structure, strategy, portfolio size, income requirements - all depend on the destination.

3. **The professional team does the work; you direct it.** Find the best specialists and let them do what they are good at.

4. **Cheap advice is expensive.** A poorly drafted FIC costs £100k to fix. Unreviewed company articles cost you a mortgage at the last minute.

5. **Community and mentorship accelerate everything.** PIN meetings, masterminds, and training connect investors with deals, professionals, and knowledge most cannot build independently.

6. **Every great strategy has a framework.** All have proven systems. Find the framework that fits your goal and follow it consistently.
