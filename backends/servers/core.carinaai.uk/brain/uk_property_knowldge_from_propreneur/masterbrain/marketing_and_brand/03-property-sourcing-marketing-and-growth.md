# Property sourcing: marketing funnel and growth

**Updated:** 2026-05-03  
**Kind:** External practice guide (Carina RAG).  
**Source notes:** Property Sourcing Manual (compiled).

## 1. M.A.R.K.E.T.I.N.G. framework (mnemonic)

- **Marketplace:** Understand customers, competitors, and your position.
- **Authority:** Become known as an expert (content, podcast, book, brand).
- **Reputation:** Central asset; easy to damage quickly.
- **Know your business:** Clear offer and market fit.
- **Engaging:** Content must earn attention to grow brand, list, and sales.
- **Trust:** Know, like, trust before purchase; serve before you sell.
- **Interesting:** Authenticity beats polish-only; honest setbacks can build trust.
- **No-brainer:** Offers should be so valuable the purchase decision feels easy.

## 2. Authority and reputation

- Position as an expert through consistent content and visible proof.
- Reputation is reinforced by doing what you promise: punctuality, clear contracts, honouring refunds when agreed.
- Poor reviews can outweigh years of goodwill; treat complaints seriously.

## 3. Engagement cadence and channels

- **Frequency:** Roughly two to three meaningful touches per week; too many risks unsubscribes, too few risks being forgotten.
- **Channels:** Email as primary; social and SMS as secondary.
- **Rule of thumb:** Drive traffic back to owned web properties to capture leads on your database.

## 4. Marketing and sales funnel

**Stage 1: top of funnel (lead magnet)**  
Free value (ebook, PDF, short video series) in exchange for contact details.

**Stage 2: know, like, trust (example 10-day email arc)**  
- Day 1: welcome.  
- Days 3 to 4: case study of a completed deal (numbers, photos, short narrative).  
- Days 7 to 8: compliance and credentials.  
- Day 10: consultation call invite framed mostly as value (example split cited: 75% value, 25% offer).

**Stage 3: sale**  
Consultation leads to a structured sales conversation and fee agreement.

## 5. Social media strategy

- Be where your audience already spends time.
- Keep branding consistent (logo, colours, tone).
- Mix business content (success stories, events, testimonials, motivation) with limited personal content if it supports authenticity.
- Training material notes Instagram as a strong interaction channel for some sourcers (platform effectiveness changes over time).

## 6. Testimonials and reviews

- Written and video testimonials support conversion.
- Third-party review platforms (example Trustpilot) cited; aim for strong average rating through service quality, not manipulation.

## 7. Funnel metrics (illustrative)

- **Email opens:** rough industry band cited 5% to 8%; well-trained lists cited up to roughly 22% to 35%+ in some training examples.
- **VIP list economics (example):** 119 members at £24.99/month cited as about £2,973.81 monthly recurring illustration (verify your own pricing and VAT treatment).

## 8. Training investors (process control)

Set written rules to filter time-wasters.

- **Qualification:** Questionnaire before list access (capital, strategies, geography).
- **Reservations:** Example rule: reservations by SMS only; return calls in message order.
- **Address disclosure:** Do not release full address or postcode until finder fee agreement and terms are signed (reduces circumvention risk).
