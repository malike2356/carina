# Property Sourcing: Social Media Post Strategy

**Source:** Professional Mentorship -- Social Media Engagement Training
**Purpose:** How to create engaging social media posts that drive interaction and build your property sourcing business
**Audience:** Carina can guide sourcers on post structure, engagement tactics, and algorithm strategy

---

## The Core Principle: Talk With, Not At

Social media posts should be a conversation, not a lecture. Think of speakers on stage -- if they just dictate at you, you switch off. The best speakers are engaging. Bring that engagement into your posts.

## The Question Strategy

The easiest way to drive engagement: **ask questions.**

Examples:
- "What has been your best refurb?"
- "What has been your biggest breakthrough?"
- "What has been your biggest obstacle?"
- "What's the biggest profit margin you've made on a refurb?"

Why questions work:
- Some people want to show off (big numbers).
- Some people are honest and realistic.
- Some people share small wins.
- ALL of it is interaction. Every comment drives the algorithm.

## Post Structure: 30-50 Words

The most effective social media posts are 30-50 words long. Longer than a one-liner but short enough to scan.

**Structure:**
1. **Hook** -- Grab attention in the first line.
2. **Context** -- A little bit of background on what you're talking about.
3. **Call to action** -- What you want them to do (comment, DM, click link, register).

**Example (video promoting your mailing list):**
"Example: "Hey everyone, I run a property sourcing business finding great deals. If you're looking for great deals, click the link below and I'll send them to you.""

**Written post version:**
Elaborate on what you do. Show a testimonial or example. End with a call to action.

## WIIFM (What's In It For Me)

Consumers only care about what you can do for them. They don't care about you or your business -- they care about what your business can give them.

Every post must answer: **What does the reader/viewer get out of this?**

## The Algorithm Hack: Give Love to Get Love

Before you post, spend 10-15 minutes being active on the platform:
1. Find people doing similar things to you.
2. Like their posts.
3. Leave thoughtful comments.
4. Interact with others in the comments.
5. THEN post your content.

Why it works: Algorithms favour creators over consumers. Most people are consumers. By being interactive before you post, you signal to the algorithm that you're a creator, not just a consumer. This boosts your post's reach.

## Engagement Rules

- When people comment on your posts, **comment back**. Close the loop.
- Every interaction drives more reach.
- Use prompts: "DM me for more info," "Comment below," "Click below to register."
- Anything that requires action drives engagement.
