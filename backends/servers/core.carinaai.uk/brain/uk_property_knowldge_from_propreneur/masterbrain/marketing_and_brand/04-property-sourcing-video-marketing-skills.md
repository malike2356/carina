# Property Sourcing: Video Marketing Skills

**Source:** Professional Mentorship -- Video Creation Training
**Purpose:** How to create effective property sourcing videos using just a phone
**Audience:** Carina can guide sourcers through video creation, framing, delivery, and content strategy

---

## Why Video Marketing Matters

Video is the most effective form of advertising for property sourcing because:
- Text alone can't convey expression, tonality, or body language.
- Video builds connection and trust with potential investors.
- Investors spend thousands in a single transaction -- they need to know, like, and trust you.
- Videos have generated hundreds of thousands (if not millions) for the business.

## Getting Started: Equipment

You do NOT need fancy equipment. Just a modern phone with a decent camera (iPhone or Samsung equivalent).

## Before You Record: Define the Outcome

Know what type of video you're making:

1. **Informational video** -- Pure information, no call to action. Example: explaining what property sourcing is.
2. **Call-to-action video** -- Specific outcome. Click this link, register, buy this deal. Think clearly about what you want the viewer to do.
3. **Friendly video** -- "This is me, this is what I do." Builds personal connection.

## Recording Technique

**Camera positioning:**
- Hold the phone at arm's length.
- Not too high, not too low. Eye level or slightly below.
- The camera lens is at the end of the phone (not the middle for many models).

**THE CRITICAL RULE: Look into the LENS, not at yourself.**
- If you look at yourself on the screen, the viewer sees you looking away from them.
- TRICK: Cover the screen with your hand so you can't see yourself. Forces you to look at the lens.

**Delivery:**
- Confident, commanding, direct, to-the-point, friendly.
- People need to like you to do business with you.
- Make one continuous take. Do NOT edit mid-video (creates jump cuts with different positioning/hair/background).
- Only crop the beginning and end.
- "Done is the new perfect." Don't aim for perfection. Just post it.

**Example video script:**
"Hey, my name is [Name] and I'm finding great property investment deals all over the UK. These deals are minimum 20% ROI. If you're looking to invest in property, make positive cash flow, and have great deals where we do all the work -- click the link below, get registered for my database. I'll be sending you deals every Tuesday at 9:00 AM."

## Why Imperfect Videos Work

- Real-life videos build more trust than polished productions.
- People want to get to know you as a person.
- Property sourcing involves high-value transactions (thousands of pounds per deal).
- Most businesses sell low-value products with high volume. Property sourcing is the opposite -- low volume, high value. Trust is everything.
- Best way to build know/like/trust: get on camera, often. Have fun with it. Show them who you are and why they should do business with you.
