# Property Sourcing: Instagram Reels for Property Marketing

**Source:** Professional Mentorship -- Instagram Reels Training
**Purpose:** How to create and optimise Instagram Reels for a property sourcing business
**Audience:** Carina can guide sourcers through reel creation, posting strategy, and algorithm optimisation

---

## Why Instagram Reels

As of 2022, Instagram Reels are the platform's most favoured format. They rank higher, get better reach, and generate more engagement than standard posts. The algorithm actively promotes Reels to non-followers, making them the best organic growth tool.

## Creating a Reel

**Step 1: Open Instagram -> Plus icon -> Select "Reel"**

**Step 2: Choose a pre-recorded video**
- Use videos you've already recorded for your business.
- Trim to approximately **20 seconds** -- short enough to hold attention, long enough to deliver value.
- Example video script: "My property business is making £35-40,000 every single month. I've done that by systemisation. If you want the top three things for systemisation, click the link below and follow me."

**Step 3: Edit**
- Trim the beginning and end to keep it tight.
- One continuous take is fine. Don't over-edit.

**Step 4: Write the caption**
- Engaging, benefit-driven caption.
- Example: "How I created a systemised property sourcing business generating £40k profit every single month. If you want to take your income to the next level, make sure you're liking and following for more great content."

**Step 5: Add hashtags**
- Relevant to property and business: #property #business #winning
- Don't overdo it -- a few targeted hashtags work better than a long list.

**Step 6: Tag accounts**
- Tag relevant business pages or collaborators.

**Step 7: Share**

## Posting Strategy

**Consistency is everything.** The algorithm rewards regular posting:
- Post at the **same time every single day** for 30 days.
- After a month, Instagram will start showing your Reels to non-followers.
- Test and iterate: try 30 days of daily posting at the same time and measure results.

## Measuring Performance

- Check views, likes, shares, and saves after each post.
- **Attention rate:** track where viewers drop off. If they drop at 25 seconds, keep videos to 25 seconds. If they stay longer, increase length.
- Some videos will get low views, others will get thousands. Work out what works for YOUR business.
- Example performance range: 98 views (low) to 17,000+ views (high) for active pages.

## Key Rules

- The more you do them, the better the algorithm picks you up.
- Don't let fear of being on camera stop you. You've seen how easy it is.
- Don't take yourself out of the game because you're scared. Just do it.
- Instagram rewards consistency above all else.
