### Describe your company in 10 words or less

VERLOX LTD builds software platforms; Propreneur is our flagship SaaS product

### Overview of your idea (problem & solution)

The Problem:
Property entrepreneurs pay for and juggle multiple separate software subscriptions. A CRM, an accounting tool, an HR system, a compliance tracker, a deal pipeline, a calculator, a tenant management system, a booking platform, a community tool. Each one costs money, takes time to learn, and doesn't talk to the others. The fragmentation is expensive, inefficient, and exhausting.

The Solution:
Propreneur is the all-in-one operating system that replaces every software tool a property business needs; in a single platform where every module connects to every other.

Modules include:
Deal pipeline, 11 property calculators, compliance tracker, investor CRM, general CRM, HR management, accounting, tenant and landlord management, mentoring OS(LMS) (courses, bookings, community), Carina AI, and data integrations interface for Rightmove, Zoopla, Xero, Open Banking, and more.

Deals feed into accounting. Tenants link to compliance. Investors connect to deals. One platform. One learning curve. One subscription; instead of eight.

The financial case is simple:
it's not just about what you earn, it's about what you keep. Propreneur cuts the cost stack of running a property business significantly.

The platform is fully built, live at propreneur.uk, and currently being trialled by an active UK property mentor on the Mentor Network tier.

## Funding spend (text)

What will you spend the funding on if successful?

The £5,000 grant will be spent entirely on marketing and customer acquisition, the single constraint currently limiting growth. The Propreneur platform is fully built and operational (live application: https://propreneur.uk). VERLOX LTD will use the funding to accelerate conversion of existing trading activity into paying subscribers.

Breakdown:

1) Paid advertising - Meta & Google (£2,200)  
Targeted campaigns aimed at property investors, HMO operators, deal sourcers, and rent-to-rent operators. Budget allocated to testing creative, audiences, and messaging to identify the lowest cost-per-trial and cost-per-conversion.

2) Property network sponsorships (£1,500)  
Sponsorship of 3-5 UK property investor network events and meetups. The property community is referral-driven; visibility and credibility at these events supports direct subscriber acquisition and word-of-mouth beyond the event itself.

3) SEO content production (£900)  
Commissioning 8-10 high-intent articles (e.g. "BRR calculator UK", "HMO compliance checklist", plus broader portfolio/operations topics) designed to attract qualified traffic into trials; with calculators delivered inside the Propreneur OS/MIS, not as a separate product. SEO content is a permanent asset that continues generating organic traffic and trial signups after the grant period.

4) Creative and ad assets (£400)  
Professional creative including a platform walkthrough video and static feature-highlight ads for paid channels.

Governance: no grant funds will be used for wages, salary, or office space. The applicant operates from a home base; the founder also earns income from employment at the University of Portsmouth, separate from this grant allocation.

## Impact

What impact will gaining the funding have on your business/startup?

This grant represents the bridge between a trading, pre-revenue business and a self-sustaining one.

Propreneur is already operational.  
The platform is live, VERLOX LTD is trading, £698/month in operational costs are being met personally by the founder from employment income. The missing piece is marketing budget. This grant directly funds that gap.

Projected impact (illustrative):  
A conservative target of 10-20 new paying subscribers within three months of campaign launch. At a £60/month illustrative blended ARPU, that is £600-£1,200 new monthly recurring revenue (£7,200-£14,400 annualised). At the upper end of the range, this begins to cover operating costs and reduces reliance on founder subsidy, freeing cash to reinvest in growth.

The SEO content investment compounds over time. The articles targeting high-intent property search terms will continue generating free trial signups months and years after publication. The network sponsorships will build brand recognition in a referral-driven community where trust drives purchasing decisions.

Beyond the direct financial return, this grant will provide validation noting support from a respected institution. That alone will accelerates credibility with prospective users, particularly property training businesses and mentors considering the £299/month Mentor Network tier.

In short: the business is already moving; this grant shifts acquisition into a higher gear and supplements the founder's personal subsidy as the primary growth constraint.

Expected ROI on the grant:  
£7,200-£14,400 annualised new recurring revenue on a £5,000 grant is approximately 1.4-2.9× first-year ARR relative to grant size; before long-tail SEO and referral effects.
