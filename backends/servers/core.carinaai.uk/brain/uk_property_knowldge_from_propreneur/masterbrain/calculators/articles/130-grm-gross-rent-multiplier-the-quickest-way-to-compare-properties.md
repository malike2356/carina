# GRM: Gross Rent Multiplier -- The Quickest Way to Compare Properties

Published: Wed, 11 Aug 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: grm-gross-rent-multiplier-the-quickest-way-to-compare-properties

---

GRM is the simplest valuation tool in property. It is the purchase price divided by the annual gross rent. A lower GRM means better value relative to income. It is a quick filter that lets you compare dozens of properties in minutes.\n\nA property with a price of £250,000 and annual rent of £12,000 has a GRM of 20.8. A similar property at £220,000 with the same rent has a GRM of 18.3. The lower GRM property is better value from an income perspective.\n\nGRM is useful because it is fast. You can calculate it from any property listing. But it ignores operating costs, mortgage costs, and capital growth. It is a filter, not a decision tool.\n\nTypical GRM ranges for UK property vary by region and type. In London, GRMs of 20 to 30 are common, reflecting higher prices and lower yields. In the North, GRMs of 10 to 16 are typical, reflecting better value. On the South Coast, GRMs of 14 to 20 are typical.\n
Use GRM as your first filter. If the GRM is above 20 on the South Coast, the gross yield is below 5% and the property is unlikely to cashflow. If the GRM is below 14, the gross yield is above 7% and the property deserves a closer look.

**The formula**

```
GRM = Purchase Price / Annual Gross Rent
```

**Why this matters**

GRM is the fastest way to compare properties. A lower GRM means better income value. On the South Coast, GRMs of 14 to 20 are typical. Use it as a first filter, not a final decision.
