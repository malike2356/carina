# Rental Yield vs Mortgage Rate Spread: The Number That Determines Whether You Make or Lose Money

Here is the most important number in BTL that most investors never calculate: the spread between your rental yield and your mortgage rate.

If your gross yield is 5.3% and your mortgage rate is 5%, the spread is 0.3%. That is dangerously thin. After costs, that spread disappears entirely and you are losing money.

A positive spread means the property earns more than it costs to finance. A negative spread means the opposite. The bigger the positive spread, the more cushion you have against rising costs and interest rates.

In the current market, a spread of at least 1% between gross yield and mortgage rate is healthy. At 1% or below, your property is a bet on capital growth.

How do you achieve a positive spread? You can buy in higher-yielding areas, negotiate a lower purchase price, add a larger deposit, or find a lower mortgage rate. Each of these actions widens your spread.

On the South Coast, where yields are lower than the national average, the spread is often negative or marginal. This is why many investors buy in the North and Midlands, where yields of 7% to 9% create a significant positive spread even at current mortgage rates.

**The formula**

```
Yield Spread = Gross Yield - Mortgage Rate
```
