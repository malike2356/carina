# Development Arrangement Fee: The Cost of Setting Up Your Finance

Published: Mon, 10 May 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: development-arrangement-fee-the-cost-of-setting-up-your-finance

---

Development lenders charge an arrangement fee for setting up the loan. It is typically 1.5% to 2.5% of the total facility. On a £334,875 facility, that is £5,023 to £8,372.\n\nThe arrangement fee is payable on completion of the loan paperwork, usually deducted from the first draw. Some lenders allow it to be added to the loan, but that increases your interest costs.\n\nThere are other fees to watch for. Monitoring surveyor fees at each draw stage, typically £500 to £1,000 per visit. Legal fees for the lender, typically £1,500 to £3,000. Exit fees if you repay early. Each of these adds to your total finance cost.\n\nThe total finance cost including arrangement fee, interest, and ancillary fees should be calculated as a percentage of the loan amount. If the total is more than 10% of the loan for an 18-month project, the finance is expensive and you should shop around.\n
Development finance is one of the most expensive ways to borrow. But it is the only option for most developers. The key is to keep the term as short as possible to minimise the total cost.

**The formula**

```
Arrangement Fee = Development Loan × Arrangement Fee % (typically 1.5-2%)
```

**Why this matters**

Arrangement fees of 1.5% to 2.5% plus monitoring fees and legal costs add up. Total finance cost should be calculated upfront. Keep the development term as short as possible to minimise interest.
