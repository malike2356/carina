# BRR Refinance Proceeds: How Much the Bank Will Give You After Refurb

Published: Wed, 03 Feb 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: brr-refinance-proceeds-how-much-the-bank-will-give-you-after-refurb

---

Refinance proceeds are how much a lender will give you based on the after-refurb value. This is the cash that pays back your bridging loan and returns your capital.\n\nAt 75% LTV on an ARV of £220,000, your refinance proceeds are £165,000. At 80% LTV, they would be £176,000. The difference between these two numbers is significant.\n\nYour total cost was £196,950. At 75% LTV, your refinance proceeds of £165,000 leave you £31,950 short. That is cash left in the deal. At 80% LTV, you get £176,000, which leaves you £20,950 short. Still cash left in.\n\nTo get all your cash out, you need an ARV that supports enough refinance proceeds to cover your total cost. With a total cost of £196,950 and a 75% LTV target, you need an ARV of at least £196,950 divided by 0.75, which equals £262,600. That is a significant uplift from your purchase price of £150,000.\n\nThe higher your LTV on refinance, the less equity you need. But higher LTV also means a larger mortgage, which affects your cashflow. There is a trade-off between capital recycling and monthly cashflow.

**The formula**

```
Refinance Proceeds = Post-Refurb Value × Refinance LTV
```

**Why this matters**

Refinance proceeds determine how much cash you get back. At 75% LTV on a £220,000 ARV, you get £165,000. Compare that to your total cost. The gap is your cash left in. Aim to minimise it.
