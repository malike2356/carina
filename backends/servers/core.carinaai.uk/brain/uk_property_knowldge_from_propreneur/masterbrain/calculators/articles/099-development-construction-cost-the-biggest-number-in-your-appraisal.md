# Development Construction Cost: The Biggest Number in Your Appraisal

Published: Mon, 26 Apr 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: development-construction-cost-the-biggest-number-in-your-appraisal

---

After GDV, construction cost is the most important number in development. It is also the hardest to estimate accurately because there are so many variables.\n\nConstruction cost is typically quoted in pounds per square metre. For a basic residential refurb in Portsmouth, expect £1,200 to £1,700 per square metre. For a new build or complex conversion, expect £1,700 to £2,500 per square metre. For a high-spec finish, the cost can exceed £3,000 per square metre.\n\nFor two flats of 70 square metres each, the construction cost at £1,500 per square metre is £210,000. That is before contingency, professional fees, and finance. The total development cost will be significantly higher.\n\nThe biggest mistake developers make is underestimating construction costs. A rule of thumb is to add 15% to 20% to your initial estimate. If the contractor quotes £210,000, budget £240,000 to £250,000. The difference could be your profit margin.\n\nGet multiple quotes. A well-specced tender document from three contractors will give you a realistic market price. If all three come in above your budget, your budget is wrong, not the contractors.

**The formula**

```
Construction Cost = Build Cost per Unit (£/m² or per unit) × Number of Units
```
