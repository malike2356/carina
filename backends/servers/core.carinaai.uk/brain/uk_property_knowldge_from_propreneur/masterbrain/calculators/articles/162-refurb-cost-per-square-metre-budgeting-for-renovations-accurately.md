# Refurb Cost per Square Metre: Budgeting for Renovations Accurately

Refurb cost per square metre is the standard way to budget for renovation work. It standardises costs across different property sizes. A 100 square metre flat costing £25,000 to refurb has a cost of £250 per square metre.\n\nTypical refurb costs in the South Coast market range from £200 to £400 per square metre for a cosmetic refurb, £400 to £700 for a medium refurb including kitchen and bathroom, and £700 to £1,200 for a full structural refurb.\n
Getting refurb cost per square metre right is essential for BRR, flipping, and development. If you underestimate by £100 per square metre on a 100 square metre property, your budget is £10,000 short. That is your profit margin gone. Get multiple quotes and add a 15% to 20% contingency.

**The formula**

```
Refurb Cost PSM = Total Refurb Cost / Gross Internal Area (m²)
```

**Why this matters**
