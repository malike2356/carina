# NRCGT: Tax on UK Property for Non-Resident Sellers

Published: Wed, 12 Jan 2028 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: nrcgt-tax-on-uk-property-for-non-resident-sellers

---

Non-residents who sell UK residential property must report and pay Capital Gains Tax within 60 days of completion. The rates are the same as for residents: 18% for basic rate and 24% for higher rate. The 60-day reporting window is strict. Missing it incurs penalties.\n\nNRCGT applies to any non-resident individual or company selling UK residential property. It does not apply to non-residents selling commercial property, although commercial property gains may still be taxable under other rules.\n
If you are a non-resident landlord or planning to move abroad, understand NRCGT before you sell. The 60-day reporting requirement means you need to have your records ready and your tax position calculated before completion.

**Why this matters**
