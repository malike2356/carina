# SA Breakeven Occupancy: The Minimum Nights You Must Book to Break Even

Published: Wed, 20 Jan 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: sa-breakeven-occupancy-the-minimum-nights-you-must-book-to-break-even

---

Every SA operator should know their breakeven occupancy. It is the occupancy level at which total revenue equals total costs. Below this number, you are losing money. Above it, you are making profit.\n\nUsing our Portsmouth SA with £28,105 annual revenue at 70% occupancy, total costs of £18,937, and a fixed cost base of roughly £12,000 (mortgage, insurance, platform minimums), you need enough revenue to cover those fixed costs.\n\nYour breakeven occupancy is your fixed costs divided by (ADR minus variable cost per night). With fixed costs of £12,000, ADR of £110, and variable costs per night of £40, each booked night contributes £70. You need £12,000 divided by £70, which equals 171 booked nights. That is a breakeven occupancy of 47%.\n\nAnything above 47% contributes to your profit. At your target 70% occupancy, you have 256 booked nights, which is 85 nights above breakeven. Those 85 nights at £70 contribution each give £5,950 in profit.\n\nA breakeven occupancy below 50% is healthy. Between 50% and 65%, the numbers are acceptable but tight. Above 65%, the property is high risk because a seasonal downturn could put you underwater.

**The formula**

```
Breakeven Occupancy (%) = Annual Fixed Costs / (Nightly Rate × 365) × 100
```

**Why this matters**

SA breakeven occupancy should be below 50%. If you need more than 60% occupancy to break even, the property is too expensive to run as SA and might work better as a standard let.
