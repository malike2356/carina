# BRR Capital Recycled: What Percentage of Your Investment Did You Get Back?

Published: Mon, 15 Feb 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: brr-capital-recycled-what-percentage-of-your-investment-did-you-get-ba

---

Capital recycled measures how much of your original cash investment you recovered through refinancing. It is the refinance proceeds divided by your total cost, expressed as a percentage.\n\nIf your total cost is £196,950 and your refinance proceeds are £165,000, your capital recycled is £165,000 divided by £196,950, which is 83.8%. That means you recycled 84% of your capital.\n\nThe recycled capital can now be used for your next deal. If you had one pot of £200,000 and you recycle 84% into each BRR deal, your pot grows as you complete each deal. After three BRRs, you have recycled your original £200,000 into properties worth £600,000+ with a growing cash balance.\n\nCapital recycling is the engine of BRR portfolio growth. A 70% to 80% recycling rate builds a strong portfolio. Below 60%, the recycling is too slow and you will run out of capital after a few deals. Above 90%, you are building wealth rapidly.\n\nThe formula for portfolio growth: number of deals before capital runs out is 1 divided by (1 minus recycled fraction). At 84% recycling, you can do roughly six deals before your capital is exhausted. At 70%, you can do roughly three deals. The difference is exponential.

**The formula**

```
Capital Recycled (%) = min(100, (Refinance Proceeds / Total Cost) × 100)
```

**Why this matters**

Capital recycled is the engine of BRR growth. Aim for 80% or higher. Each percentage point of recycled capital exponentially increases the number of deals your capital can support.
