# Annual Capital Growth: How Fast Is Your Portfolio Appreciating?

Published: Wed, 10 Nov 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: annual-capital-growth-how-fast-is-your-portfolio-appreciating

---

Annual capital growth is the percentage increase in your property values over a year. It is calculated as the current value minus the previous year's value, divided by the previous year's value.\n\nFor the South Coast, long-term average capital growth is roughly 3% to 5% per year. Some years are higher, some are lower. A well-chosen property in a strong location should track or exceed this average.\n\nCapital growth is the biggest driver of portfolio wealth for most South Coast investors. A portfolio worth £1 million growing at 4% a year adds £40,000 in equity annually. That is more than most BTL cashflows.\n
But capital growth is not guaranteed. It depends on market conditions, interest rates, and local demand. If you are relying on growth to achieve your returns, make sure you have stress-tested your portfolio against periods of zero or negative growth.

**The formula**

```
Capital Growth (%) = ((Current Value - Purchase Price) / Purchase Price) × 100
```
