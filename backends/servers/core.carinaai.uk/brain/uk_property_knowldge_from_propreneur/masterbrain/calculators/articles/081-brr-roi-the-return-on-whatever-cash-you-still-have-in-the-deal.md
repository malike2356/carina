# BRR ROI: The Return on Whatever Cash You Still Have in the Deal

Published: Mon, 22 Feb 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: brr-roi-the-return-on-whatever-cash-you-still-have-in-the-deal

---

ROI on a standard BTL measures return on your total cash invested. BRR ROI measures return on the cash you have left in after refinancing. And because that number is smaller, the ROI is typically much higher.\n\nIf your cash left in is £31,950 and the property generates £3,000 a year in net cashflow, your BRR ROI is £3,000 divided by £31,950, which equals 9.4%. That is excellent. A standard BTL with £70,000 cash invested and £1,200 cashflow has an ROI of 1.7%.\n\nThe BRR ROI is higher because you have less cash tied up in the deal. This is the power of capital recycling. You get most of your cash back and still own an asset that generates income.\n\nIf you achieve cash out (zero cash left in), your ROI is technically infinite because you have no cash invested. But that is theoretical. In practice, you should always measure ROI against the cash you could have earned elsewhere. If the BRR property generates £3,000 a year and you have £32,000 left in, you are earning 9.4% on that cash. That beats most investment options.\n\nBRR ROI of 8% to 15% on cash left in is a strong result. Below 5%, the risk of the refurb and refinance might not be justified.

**The formula**

```
BRR ROI (%) = (Annual Net Cashflow / Cash Left In) × 100
```

**Why this matters**

BRR ROI on cash left in of 8% to 15% is strong. Below 5%, the risk might not justify the strategy. The power of BRR is earning good returns on a fraction of the capital a standard BTL would require.
