# Flip Selling Costs: The Fees That Cut Into Your Profit

Published: Wed, 10 Mar 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: flip-selling-costs-the-fees-that-cut-into-your-profit

---

If acquisition costs are the invisible erosion of your profit, selling costs are the visible exit fee. Together, they can consume 10% to 15% of your sale price.\n\nFor a flip selling at £220,000, your selling costs include: estate agent fees at 1.5% plus VAT = £3,960, legal fees on sale at £1,200, EPC certificate at £120, and any seller concessions or guarantees. Total selling costs: roughly £5,280.\n\nAdd your acquisition costs of £10,100 and your total transaction costs are £15,380. On a £220,000 sale, that is 7% of the sale price consumed by costs. If your gross profit before costs was £70,000, you have already lost £15,380 without doing any work.\n\nThe ratio of total costs to sale price is your cost burden. A flip with a cost burden above 10% is hard to make work. Below 7%, you have room to make a decent profit.\n\nThe best flippers minimise both sides. Buy directly from motivated sellers to reduce acquisition costs. Sell privately or through investor networks to reduce selling costs. Every pound saved on either side is a pound of profit.

**The formula**

```
Selling Costs = Sale Price × Agent Fee % + Legal Sell Fee
```

**Why this matters**

Selling costs add 2% to 4% to your exit costs. Combined with acquisition costs, total transaction costs can consume 7% to 12% of your sale price. Minimise both sides.
