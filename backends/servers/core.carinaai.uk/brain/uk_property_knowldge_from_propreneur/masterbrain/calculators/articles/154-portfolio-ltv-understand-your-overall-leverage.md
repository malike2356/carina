# Portfolio LTV: Understand Your Overall Leverage

Published: Wed, 03 Nov 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: portfolio-ltv-understand-your-overall-leverage

---

Weighted average portfolio LTV is total mortgages divided by total property values. It gives you your overall leverage position. If your portfolio LTV is 60%, you are comfortably leveraged. If it is 75% or above, you are pushing the limits of what lenders will accept.\n\nPortfolio LTV varies with market conditions. If property prices rise, your LTV falls without you doing anything. If prices fall, your LTV rises. This is why it is important to keep LTV below 65% during strong markets, giving you a buffer for market downturns.\n
A portfolio LTV above 70% is risky because a 10% market correction takes you to 78%, which is above most lenders' maximum. You could be forced to sell or unable to refinance.

**The formula**

```
Portfolio LTV (%) = (Total Debt / Total Portfolio Value) × 100
```

**Why this matters**

Portfolio LTV of 50% to 65% is a comfortable range. Above 70% is risky. Keep headroom so a market correction does not push you above lender limits.
