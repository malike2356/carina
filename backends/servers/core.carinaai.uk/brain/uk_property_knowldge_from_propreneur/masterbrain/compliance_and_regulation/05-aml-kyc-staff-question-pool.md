### Section 1: Single Response Questions

**Q1. What is Money Laundering?**
A) It means bringing money earned into a laundromat to be thoroughly cleaned.
B) It means to keep money "clean" from any suspicious criminal activity.
C) It means bringing money earned from crime into the financial system and making it look legitimate.
> **Answer: C**

**Q2. When should a financial institution implement Customer Due Diligence processes?**
A) They should always implement these processes at any given time.
B) At the closing of a bank account to make sure there was no suspicious activity reported.
C) They doubt that the customer's identification data is truthful.
> **Answer: C** - CDD must be carried out in specific circumstances, including when you have doubts about previously obtained identification data.

**Q6. What is the key international body in anti-money laundering legislation called?**
A) The Money Task Force (MTF)
B) Financial Action Task Force (FATF)
C) Anti-Money Laundering Group (AMLG)
> **Answer: B**

**Q7. What does Customer Due Diligence mean?**
A) Verifying who your customers are.
B) Give your customers the service they deserve.
C) Treating the customer with care.
> **Answer: A**

**Q8. What should a Money Laundering Reporting Officer make sure about a business?**
A) Ensure that the money is regularly cleaned to avoid contamination.
B) Ensure that the business complies with the regulations.
C) Ensure that the business is making profit.
> **Answer: B**

**Q9. What is Money Laundering?**
A) It means bringing money earned from crime into the financial system and making it look legitimate.
B) It means to keep money "clean" from any suspicious criminal activity.
C) It means bringing money earned into a laundromat to be thoroughly cleaned.
> **Answer: A**

**Q11. The Second Money Laundering Directive regulations apply to what type of businesses?**
A) Clinics
B) Supermarkets
C) Hospitals
D) Lawyers and Accountants in some circumstances
> **Answer: D**

**Q15. \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ means exchanging the money in a foreign currency or hiding it in another person's bank account.**
A) Converting
B) Transferring
C) Concealing
D) Disguising
> **Answer: A**

**Q20. A suspicious transaction is when a customer...?**
A) Deposits in cash and receives direct deposits.
B) Opens more accounts than necessary and transfers money between them.
C) Deposits large amounts of money and uses their banking services.
D) Opens one account and transfers money to others.
> **Answer: B**

**Q21. An example of good practice in the workplace regarding anti-money laundering regulations is what?**
A) Training should be the same for all positions.
B) Only staff in high-risk positions should be trained.
C) Training should be practical, such as looking at real case studies.
> **Answer: C**

**Q23. What is an example of a way that criminals use to make income appear legitimate?**
A) Casinos
B) Government Offices
C) Supermarkets
D) Bank
> **Answer: A**

**Q24. In a court, the prosecution would have to prove what in order to find a person guilty of money laundering?**
A) Bring in large bags of money into the court, along with certified bank statements.
B) The defendant knew about the origin of the money.
C) The location of the money and if it is in cash or in a bank account.
> **Answer: B**

**Q26. What type of records have to be kept by a company or a financial institution?**
A) Automotive information & insurance
B) Living conditions
C) Internet service provider information
D) Transactions and customer information
> **Answer: D** - Under AML regs, you must keep customer verification documents (CDD) and transaction records for five years after the relationship ends.

**Q27. What does POCA stand for?**
A) Procedures Of Criminals in Action
B) Proceeds Of Crime Act
C) Processes of Criminal Accountability
> **Answer: B**

**Q29. What does RBA stand for?**
A) Risk-Based Approach
B) Renewing Benefits Association
C) Replaceable Base Approach
> **Answer: A**

**Q30. What is an example of a business that criminals use to make income appear legitimate?**
A) Casinos
B) Government Offices
C) Bank
D) Supermarkets
> **Answer: A**

### Section 2: Fill In The Blank Questions

**Q3. The FCA suggest that companies ensure their employees' \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ should be reviewed regularly.**
> **Answer: competence**

**Q5. Financial institutions keep records of transactions in several formats. They are: credit and debit slips, cheques, and \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.**
> **Answer: electronic records**

**Q28. The 3 Stages of Money Laundering are: placement, \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, and integration.**
> **Answer: layering**

### Section 3: True / False Questions

**Q10. Financial institutions use software to identify suspicious activity.**
> **Answer: True**

**Q13. The MLRO should usually hold a senior management position.**
> **Answer: True**

**Q16. If criminals use a business to launder money or finance terrorism, the institution is automatically pardoned because they did not commit crimes to obtain the illegal income.**
> **Answer: False**

**Q18. An authorised disclosure is a legal defence against arranging or using criminal property.**
> **Answer: True** - An authorised disclosure to the NCA (before the prohibited act) with appropriate consent provides a statutory defence under POCA.

**Q19. A money laundering reporting officer (MLRO) is responsible for prosecuting and presenting evidence to the court.**
> **Answer: False**

### Section 4: Multiple Response Questions

**Q4. There are seven elements of a Customer Due Diligence Program. What are 3 of them?**
A) Communication
B) Monitoring
C) Customer Identification
D) Customer Service
E) Risk Rating
F) Customer Care
> **Answer: B, C, E** - Monitoring, Customer Identification, Risk Rating

**Q12. Which of the following are NOT the responsibilities of a Money Laundering Reporting Officer?**
A) Producing many reports a year
B) Monitoring policies and procedures
C) Dealing with internal reports of suspicious activity
D) Personally giving anti-money laundering training to staff
E) Informing the appropriate authorities
> **Answer: A, D** - Producing many reports a year is not a requirement, and the MLRO is not expected to personally deliver AML training to all staff.

**Q14. A customer may be considered suspicious when...?**
A) They provide documents that are easily verified.
B) They want to open an account without references, a local address or adequate identification.
C) They provide financial statements from similar businesses.
D) They refuse to provide necessary information to use banking services.
> **Answer: B, D**

**Q22. The stage that criminals are likely to be caught is during the placement stage. What 2 types of methods do the police use to identify suspicious activity?**
A) Questioning the Business Owner
B) Vertical Analysis
C) Cross-Border Declaration Rules
D) Watching the business from a patrol car everyday
> **Answer: B, C**

**Q25. Approved managers have the responsibility of assessing risks at both types of which levels?**
A) Entity Level
B) Entry Level
C) Client Level
D) Intern Level
> **Answer: A, C** - Entity Level and Client Level
