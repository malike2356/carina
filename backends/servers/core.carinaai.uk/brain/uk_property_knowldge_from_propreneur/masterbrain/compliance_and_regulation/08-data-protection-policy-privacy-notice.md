**Xelox Properties Ltd**
**Version 1.0 - May 2026**

## Part A: Internal Data Protection Policy

### 1. Introduction

Xelox Properties Ltd is committed to protecting the privacy and security of personal data. This policy sets out how we collect, use, store, and protect personal data in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

### 2. Scope

This policy applies to all personal data processed by Xelox Properties Ltd, including data relating to:

- Investors and potential investors
- Landlords and property owners
- Tenants and prospective tenants
- Suppliers and contractors
- Website visitors
- Employees and job applicants

### 3. Data Protection Principles

Xelox Properties Ltd will ensure that personal data is:

a) Processed lawfully, fairly, and in a transparent manner
b) Collected only for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes
c) Adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary
d) Accurate and kept up to date
e) Kept for no longer than necessary
f) Processed securely, with appropriate technical and organisational measures

### 4. What Personal Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

- Full name and title
- Residential address
- Email address and telephone numbers
- Proof of identity documents (passport, driving licence)
- Proof of address documents
- Financial information including bank details, source of funds, and transaction history
- Property ownership details
- Communication records including emails and call notes
- Website usage data via cookies and analytics

### 5. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal data under the following lawful bases:

- **Contract:** To fulfil our obligations under sourcing agreements, rent to rent agreements, and other contracts
- **Legal obligation:** To comply with AML regulations, right to rent checks, and other legal requirements
- **Legitimate interests:** To operate our business effectively, including marketing our services to potential clients
- **Consent:** Where we rely on consent, we will obtain it explicitly and document it

### 6. How We Use Personal Data

We use personal data for:

- Assessing and presenting property deals to investors
- Managing rent to rent and guaranteed rent agreements
- Conducting anti-money laundering checks
- Communicating with clients about properties, deals, and services
- Maintaining accurate business records
- Complying with legal and regulatory obligations
- Improving our services and website

### 7. Data Storage and Security

We implement the following security measures:

- All personal data is stored on secure, password-protected systems
- Access to personal data is restricted to staff who need it for their role
- Electronic data is encrypted where practical
- Physical documents are stored in locked cabinets
- Staff receive data protection training
- Breach response procedures are in place

### 8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary:

- Client records: Five years after the end of our business relationship
- Financial records: Six years as required by HMRC
- AML records: Five years after the last transaction
- Marketing data: Until the individual opts out or withdraws consent

After the retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

### 9. Data Subject Rights

Individuals have the following rights under UK GDPR:

- Right to be informed about how their data is used
- Right of access to their personal data
- Right to rectification of inaccurate data
- Right to erasure (subject to legal obligations)
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing
- Rights relating to automated decision making

Requests to exercise these rights should be directed to the Data Protection Officer at Xelox Properties Ltd. We will respond within one month.

### 10. Data Breach Procedure

In the event of a data breach, Xelox Properties Ltd will:

- Immediately contain the breach
- Assess the risk to affected individuals
- Notify the ICO within 72 hours where there is a risk to individuals' rights and freedoms
- Notify affected individuals where appropriate
- Document the breach and remedial actions taken

### 11. Third Party Processors

Where we engage third parties to process personal data (such as CRM providers, email services, or accounting software), we ensure they have appropriate data protection measures in place through written contracts.

### 12. Training

All staff handling personal data receive data protection training. Training is refreshed annually or when regulations change.

### 13. Review

This policy is reviewed annually or when regulatory changes occur, whichever is sooner.
