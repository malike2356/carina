# Deal due diligence pack: structure and contents

**Updated:** 2026-05-03  
**Kind:** External practice guide (Carina RAG).  
**Source notes:** DD pack templates and worked examples. Redact third-party contact details when sharing externally.

## Section 1: Property information

- Full address when appropriate stage reached.
- Link to listing.
- Agreed or guide price.
- Strategy bullets (value-add angle, planning angle, unique features).

## Section 2: Sold comparables

**Rules:**

- Like-for-like on size and type where possible.
- Prefer same street or tight radius (example **within one mile**), training-dependent.
- Status: sold, under offer, or on market with transparent asking if clearly labelled.
- Match beds, baths, reception count, and broad condition band.

**Table shape:** address, size and room count, distance, sold price.

- Attach listing screenshots for traceability.

## Section 3: Rental comparables

- Sources often include portal data, OpenRent, Spareroom room market.
- HMO: match room type (standard double vs ensuite).
- BTL: match property size and quality band.

**Table shape:** address, type, distance, rent (pcm per property or per room as relevant).

## Section 4: Council tax and EPC

- Band, annual liability, monthly accrual.
- EPC rating and expiry where relevant to letting rules.

## Section 5: Location narrative

**A. Strategy fit:** regeneration story, employment anchors, student demand, HMO demand ratio examples (always recompute for your patch), Article 4 presence and implications for new conversions vs grandfathered stock.

**B. Amenities:** stations, city centre drive times, hospitals, universities, retail, food.

**C. Crime, development, growth:** five-year price tables if defensible; planning application evidence.

## Section 6: Agent and operator calls

Log calls to sales and lettings agents or HMO operators:

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Firm and role | Traceability |
| Article 4 view | Planning reality check |
| Area recommendation | Subjective but recorded |
| Room rent benchmarks | Underwriting |
| Occupancy view | Risk |
| Fees | Management, setup, tenant find |

## Section 7: Selling agent file

- Named contacts, email thread presence, written offer acceptance evidence.
- Screenshots or PDF exports stored with the pack.

## Section 8: Letting agent file

- Same pattern as section 7 plus rental opinions and fee percent (example **10% to 12%**).

## Section 9: Floor plans

- As-is layout.
- Proposed layout for conversions (example three-bed to five-bed with ensuites) with planning caveat.

## Section 10: Refurbishment costs

- Line items from your refurb database (see `49-property-investment-roi-and-refurb-calculators.md`).
- Split mandatory licensing works vs cosmetic.
- Include misc and contingency lines with stated percentages.

## Section 11: Return on investment

- Attach or summarise models: BRR to HMO, BRR to single let, vanilla multi-let, rent-to-rent/SA at **50% to 90%** occupancy bands.
- Self-managed vs managed cost variants where material.

## Section 12: Bricks-and-mortar sense check (example methodology)

- Land Registry or title-aware area for comparables per square metre.
- House value estimate = internal area × selected £/m² benchmark.
- Add garden or land bands only with defensible comparables (large error risk).

Training spreadsheet example (illustrative only, not a live instruction to buy):

- Built form about **117 m²** at about **£2,255/m²** ≈ **£263,856** headline.
- Adjoining land about **200 m²** at same £/m² (often wrong without land comps) ≈ **£451,036** headline.
- Combined headline **~£715k** vs purchase **£232k** shown as deep discount story in source material (method is contestable; professional valuation always wins for lending).

## Section 13: Media

- Internal video: each room, consumer unit, boiler, damp suspects, ceilings.
- External: elevations, roofline, gardens, outbuildings, basements, extra plots.

## Section 14: HMO compliance checklist (themes from DD pack spec)

- Minimum bedroom size example cited: **6.51 m²** for one person aged over ten (national and local rules interact; verify licence and council standards, for example **`52-manchester-hmo-licensing-standards-and-conditions.md`** for Manchester ratios).
- Minimum kitchen sizes by occupier count where the council publishes a table.
- Bathroom and WC provision by occupier count.
- Kitchen appliances and worktop rules by occupier count for licensable HMOs.
- Article 4 status, expected planning timeline, and grandfathering questions for existing stock.
- Mandatory licence fee examples (council-specific, example **£985** cited in source material).
