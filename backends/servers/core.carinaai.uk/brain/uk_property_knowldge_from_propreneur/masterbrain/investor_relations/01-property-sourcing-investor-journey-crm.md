# Property Sourcing: Investor Journey, CRM & Database

**Source:** TENX Property Sourcing Manual Module 4
**Purpose:** Building and managing an investor database, CRM setup, Know-Like-Trust campaign
**Audience:** Carina can guide users through investor acquisition and CRM workflows

---

## Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

Target investors matching:
- Age: 30-50
- Gender: predominantly male
- Occupation: busy professionals (doctors, senior management, corporate executives)
- Salary: £60k+
- Nationality: UK-based
- Hobbies: expensive hobbies (indicates disposable income)

The deeper the ICP analysis, the more targeted and effective the marketing.

## Market Dominating Position (MDP)

A defining statement for your business. Example:
"Helping busy professionals find the best property deals for hassle-free investing."

MDP must include buzzwords investors relate to. Write your MDP before doing any marketing or networking. It separates you from amateurs.

## Networking Events

Google "networking events [location]". Outcome: "How can I serve?" not "I want to sell."

Rules:
- Add value. Solve problems.
- Make it about them. Ask questions.
- Dress: business casual. Not tracksuits.
- High energy. Smile. Be present. No phones.
- Everything you do is watched -- it reflects your real self.
- Have a 20-second pitch ready. NO WAFFLE. Clear, concise, direct, practised.
- Your pitch speaks 1-to-many. Make it count.
- Body language: open and welcoming.
- Adapt pitch to audience by reading the room.

## Investor Journey (Website Funnel)

Five-stage website flow:

**Stage 1 -- Landing Page:**
Distraction-free homepage. Easy to navigate. One main call to action.

**Stage 2 -- Free Deal Alerts Signup:**
- Short video explaining what you do.
- Signup form: name, email, phone number.
- Phone number collected so virtual PA can call the database to generate leads.

**Stage 3 -- Pre-Qualification Questions:**
Must answer before joining database. Filters serious investors from time wasters.
Questions to ask:
- "What type of properties are you most interested in?"
- "How much capital do you have available?"
- "What would you buy if the perfect house came on the market tomorrow?"
- Don't make it too easy -- high barrier = higher quality.

**Stage 4 -- VIP Offer:**
£24.99/month. Get deals sent 24 hours before general email.
Presented immediately after signup. Video explaining what VIP includes.
No obvious "X" button to close -- designed to push conversion.
~125 VIP subscribers = £3,123/month passive income.

**Stage 5 -- Thank You Page:**
Returns to main website. Enters the Know-Like-Trust campaign.

## CRM System

Recommended: **Keap** (formerly Infusionsoft). Cost: ~£259/month for up to 9,000+ contacts.
Budget alternative: **AllClients** (~£30/month, includes landing pages).

Keap is NOT just a mail blaster like Mailchimp -- it's a fully managed, integrated CRM with automation, tagging, and campaign sequencing.

Example performance: 9,530 investor database. Open rate: 22-36% (industry average is 5-8%). 119 VIP members at £24.99 = £2,973/month.

## Know-Like-Trust (KLT) Campaign

Automated email sequence triggered when someone joins the database:

| Day | Email Content |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Welcome email. Thank you, explain what the next few days look like. |
| Day 3-4 | Example of a sold deal. Short story, figures, photos. Show how you've helped an investor. |
| Day 5-6 | Your buying process. Explain how buying from you works. Your rules. |
| Day 7-8 | Your compliance details. EPC, company, insurance, ICO, AML -- builds trust. |
| Day 10 | Free Zoom consultation offer. 75% value, 25% offer. Don't sell until you've helped them. |

## Train Your Investor

You set the rules. Investors must follow them:
- Deals reserved by TEXT only (not calls, not email).
- You call back in text order.
- No full address or postcode before finder's fee paid + T&Cs signed.
- Don't reward investors who break your rules.
- Never give full property details before payment. NDA is not worth the paper it's written on -- just don't give the address.

## Email Engagement Schedule

Minimum 2-3 emails per week:
- Monday 2:00 PM: VIP investors email
- Tuesday 9:00 AM: All investors email (even if no deals to share)
- Thursday/Friday: Additional engagement email

Social media postings alongside emails.

Protocol: When an investor reaches out directly, respond: "Thank you. All deals go through my website. Please register there." This feeds the funnel.
