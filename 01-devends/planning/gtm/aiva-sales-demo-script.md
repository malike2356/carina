# Aiva sales demo script (5 minutes)

**Audience:** Owner-operator or ops manager at a regulated UK SMB (property management, IFA, accountancy, legal).

**Goal:** Show that Aiva is governed AI labour, not another ChatGPT wrapper. Viewer should leave able to say: "It does the admin, asks before it sends, and logs everything."

**Prerequisites:** Demo workspace with one AI Employee (Mia, Property Coordinator), approval workflow enabled on `email_send`, Scout connected (green status).

---

## Minute 0-1: Frame the problem

Say:

> "You have looked at Copilot or ChatGPT. Your compliance officer said no because there is no audit trail and nothing stops it sending the wrong email. Aiva is the opposite: named AI employees that do the work, ask before they send, and log every step to Scout."

Show the Aiva landing page hero or compliance one-pager (`/aiva/compliance/`).

---

## Minute 1-3: Client onboarding demo (core)

1. Open the approval inbox (or trigger a test inbound email to the employee mailbox).
2. Show inbound: *"New tenant application received: David Chen, 14 Maple Court."*
3. Show Mia's draft welcome email in the approval card.
4. Point out: **nothing has sent yet**. The employee waited.
5. Click **Approve and send** (or show a pre-approved example).
6. Open Scout dashboard: show the event line (`email_send`, employee, approver, hash, timestamp).

Say:

> "Your compliance officer gets the answer they need. Yes, you can use AI here, because you can prove exactly what it did."

---

## Minute 3-4: Kill switch and human backup (30 seconds each)

**Kill switch:** Open Scout or Aiva governance panel. Show suspend on an employee. Note pending triggers queue; nothing drops.

**Human backup:** Show the Human handover tab on the marketing page or a live billing-dispute thread where VERLOX VA James resolved an issue in the same email thread.

Say:

> "When judgment matters, a real person steps in. When policy matters, you stop the employee from one screen."

---

## Minute 4-5: Pricing anchor and close

Say:

> "A part-time UK admin costs £240 to £360 a month. Aiva Starter is £149 and includes Scout Govern. You are not buying software. You are buying governed admin capacity with an audit trail."

CTA: 15-day trial, no card. Offer compliance one-pager PDF for their legal team.

---

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "We already use Copilot." | Copilot drafts text in M365. It does not run persistent employees, require approval before sends, or give an AI-specific audit trail. |
| "AI will make mistakes." | That is why approval is required on sends and updates. You see the draft before it goes out. |
| "Our compliance team is strict." | Give them `/aiva/compliance/`. Kill switch, DPA, UK residency, Scout export. |
| "We are too small." | Solo freelancers become their own approval bottleneck. Aiva fits 5 to 100 employees with at least one approver. |

---

## Sector variants

- **Property (default):** Tenant onboarding email. Use `/aiva/property/` talking points.
- **IFA / accountancy:** Client onboarding pack and annual review scheduling. Emphasise FCA-adjacent record-keeping.
- **Legal:** Intake triage and appointment scheduling. Emphasise SRA confidentiality and approval on all outbound client email.

---

## Acceptance check

After the demo, the prospect can answer in one sentence:

> "Aiva replaces part-time admin work with AI employees that ask before they send and log everything for compliance."

If they cannot, repeat the approval step and Scout log. That is the wedge.
