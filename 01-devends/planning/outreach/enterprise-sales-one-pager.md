## Elevator pitch (30 seconds)

Carina is the free MIT kernel for AI agents (tools, memory, channels, packs). Carina Enterprise is VERLOX paid support for ISVs and regulated teams shipping **products on the kernel**, not a license fee. Think Red Hat for Linux: software stays open; you buy SLAs, custom packs, dedicated hosting, and training.

## Ideal customer profile

1. **ISV** building vertical SaaS (property, logistics, healthcare copilot)
2. **Regulated** UK/EU team needing DSPT or GDPR documentation support
3. **Platform team** at mid-size org standardising on one agent kernel
4. **Partner** graduating from "Built on Carina" to paid support (see [partner program](https://carinaai.uk/partners))

Disqualifiers: wants only a chatbot wrapper with no kernel adoption; expects MIT kernel to become proprietary.

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Why pay if MIT is free?" | You pay for response times, dedicated instances, and VERLOX building your vertical pack, not for the kernel binary. |
| "Is this the same as Scout?" | No. Scout is security governance. Enterprise is builder support. Often sold together. |
| "Is this Carina Ops?" | Ops is hosted SaaS tiers. Enterprise adds SLAs, custom packs, and dedicated hosting beyond standard Cloud. |
| "Will you change our license?" | No. MSA covers services. Kernel stays MIT. |
