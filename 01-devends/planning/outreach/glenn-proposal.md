## Internal: What Glenn Gets

Propreneur -- the operating system for property entrepreneurs. Deal pipeline,
CRM, compliance tracking, property calculators, document storage.

Aiva -- a named AI Employee powered by Carina, governed by Scout, backed by
real human VAs. Glenn's Aiva will:

1. **Monitor his inbox.** Reads incoming messages from property managers and
   his accountant. Extracts what matters: rent received, maintenance updates,
   invoice details, compliance deadlines.

2. **Auto-enter data into Propreneur.** Incoming information lands in the
   right place: deal updates, tenant records, compliance logs. No manual entry.

3. **Daily briefings by Telegram.** Every morning Glenn gets a summary: what
   came in yesterday, what needs attention today, upcoming deadlines. Text
   and audio.

4. **Answer questions about his portfolio.** "What's the rent status on Flat
   4?" "When does the EPC expire on Unit 12?" Aiva knows because Aiva reads
   everything that comes in.

5. **Human backup included.** When Aiva encounters something it cannot handle,
   a real human VA from VERLOX steps in. Glenn doesn't see the handoff.
