Tue's tips

Starting points for CX and contact center leaders

1

Start with one task you hate

Not your most strategic work. The thing you do out of obligation every week. Hand that to an AI worker first. You'll learn more from that single delegation than from any webinar about AI.

2

Don't expect perfection on day one

The first output will be 80% right. Give feedback. It learns. In my experience, workers are at full working speed within a week.

3

The ROI question you should ask

What is one hour of your time worth? Multiply that by the hours you'll reclaim. That's your business case. No spreadsheet required.

4

The team conversation you need to have

AI workers don't replace your people. In most; if not all; cases, they absorb the work your people resent doing. That's a retention argument, not just an efficiency one.
