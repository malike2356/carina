The Anatomy of an AI Colleague

Not just another chatbot. Not just another workflow tool.

Spinnable workers combine multiple cutting-edge technologies into something that actually feels like a colleague — one that grows with your team, works across your tools, and gets better over time.

The Brain

Intelligence That Plans, Not Just Responds

An AI worker isn’t powered by a single model — it’s orchestrated by multiple state-of-the-art models working together. When you assign a task, it breaks it down, delegates sub-tasks to specialized models, and combines their outputs into one cohesive result.

Think of it as a team of experts in one.

Memory

Memory That Grows Over Time

Every worker remembers — your company goals, the people you work with, your preferences, and past decisions. Workers build a consistent personality and knowledge base that deepens over time.

Just like a real colleague who’s been with the team for months.

Tools & Skills

Connected to Every Tool You Use

Your worker connects to the tools your company already uses — Notion, Google Calendar, Gmail, Slack, and dozens more. Credentials are stored securely so your worker can access multiple tools and accounts on your behalf.

Plus, every worker comes with powerful built-in skills:

  • Deep research across the web
  • Website scanning and browsing
  • Code writing and execution
  • File and document generation
  • Image and presentation creation

Time Awareness

Understands Time Like You Do

Tell your worker “Send me a report every Friday at 4pm” or “Follow up with this client tomorrow morning.” Workers understand scheduling, recurring tasks, and deadlines — and they’ll execute autonomously, even when you’re offline.

Multi-Channel

Works Where You Work

Talk to your worker over WhatsApp, email, Slack, or the Spinnable interface. Workers meet you in your existing workflows — no new apps to learn, no new habits to build.

More Than the Sum of Its Parts

A brain. Memory. Tools. Time awareness. Multi-channel communication. Each is impressive on its own — but the real magic is how they all work together.

The result isn’t a tool you operate. It’s a colleague you collaborate with. One that doesn’t need technical setup, doesn’t require you to build workflows, and gets better the longer you work together.

Security & Trust

Built for Trust

Spinnable is designed to keep your data safe and give you full control over how your workers operate.

Isolated Memory

Each worker has their own memory. When you teach a worker something or share confidential information, it’s never exposed to any other worker.

Need-to-Know Basis

Like in a real company, you can give each worker access only to the knowledge they need — nothing more.

Secure Credentials

We use trusted partners and OAuth to handle authentication, so your tool credentials are never exposed or stored insecurely.

Smart Tool Usage

Workers only access tools when they need to complete a task. They don’t scrape data or store information just for the sake of it.

You Control the Exposure

Internal workers can have inbound emails and WhatsApp disabled for safety. Customer-facing workers can have read-only tool access. Because Spinnable lets you build a team of workers, each one can have different security levels — just like a real company.
