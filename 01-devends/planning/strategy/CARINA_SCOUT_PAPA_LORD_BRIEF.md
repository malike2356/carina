## Why This Matters; And Why You Should Care

Every business today faces four accelerating threats. They are getting worse, not better. And no existing product addresses all of them.

### The Pain: Security Is a Tax Companies Cannot Afford to Pay

A proper security audit costs five to twenty thousand pounds. It takes two to six weeks. It produces a PDF that is outdated the day it is delivered. Meanwhile, the company continues operating with vulnerabilities they do not know about. They are exposed for weeks between audits. The auditor leaves, and nothing has changed except a report no one reads.

Small and medium companies cannot afford this at all. They skip security entirely. They hope nothing bad happens. Sometimes it does not. Sometimes it does; and they lose customer data, reputation, and in some cases, the entire business.

### The Fear: AI Agents Are Being Deployed With Zero Oversight

Companies are giving AI agents access to code repositories, customer databases, cloud servers, and internal systems. These agents can delete files, send emails, modify databases, push code to production. And no one is watching.

An AI agent that misunderstands an instruction can delete a production database in seconds. An agent that gets confused can email confidential data to the wrong person. An agent running at 3 AM with no human in the loop can cascade a small error into a full outage before anyone wakes up.

When the board asks "is our AI secure?" the answer today is "we don't really know." When an auditor asks "prove your AI systems are governed," the answer is silence. When a regulator asks "show us your AI audit trail," there is nothing to show.

This is not hypothetical. Companies are deploying AI agents right now with no kill switch, no monitoring, no audit trail, and no compliance evidence. They are flying blind. They know it. They are afraid of it. And they have no solution.

### The Cost: Companies Pay For Too Many AI Tools That Do Not Work Together

A typical growing company uses: one AI tool for writing code, another for drafting documents, another for analyzing data, another for customer support, another for research. Each tool has its own subscription, its own login, its own learning curve, its own data silo. None of them talk to each other. None of them are monitored. The company pays hundreds of pounds a month for fragmented tools that create more management overhead than they save in productivity.

### The Risk: Compliance Is No Longer Optional

SOC2. ISO 27001. GDPR. The EU AI Act. Regulations that were once "nice to have" are now mandatory for any company handling customer data or deploying AI. The cost of non-compliance is not just a fine. It is losing enterprise contracts because you cannot pass a security review. It is being blocked from government procurement because you lack the certifications. It is investors walking away because your compliance posture is undocumented.

The compliance automation market is worth fifteen billion dollars and growing because companies are desperate for help. But existing solutions are built for large enterprises with dedicated compliance teams. Small and medium companies are left to figure it out themselves; or ignore it and hope no one asks.

### The Solution: One Platform That Does Everything; Securely

Carina replaces all those fragmented AI tools with one platform. One subscription. One interface. One place where all AI work happens.

Scout ensures that everything Carina does is monitored, logged, and auditable. One dashboard shows everything your AI systems are doing. One button stops everything if something goes wrong. One click generates a complete compliance evidence package ready for your auditor.

This combination; a universal AI agent with built-in security governance; does not exist anywhere else.

## How Carina Works; Real Examples

You give Carina a task in plain English. No programming. No commands. No technical knowledge required.

**Everyday business:** "Draft a tenancy renewal letter for Flat 4, 106 Chichester Road, new rent £950, effective August 1st." Carina drafts the letter, formats it, attaches the current agreement. Three minutes. Done.

**Specialist professional:** "Review these patient discharge summaries. Flag any mentioning heart conditions. Create a seven-day follow-up schedule and draft the notification letters." Carina reads, identifies, schedules, drafts. No specialist software needed.

**Complex multi-step:** "I need a business plan for a compliance software product. Research the market, identify competitors, draft the executive summary, create a financial projection." Carina researches online, analyzes competitors, writes the document, builds the spreadsheet. Twenty minutes. What takes a consultant two weeks.

**Security; same platform, same interface:** "Scan our website for security issues." Carina discovers every subdomain, scans every port, checks 200,000 known vulnerabilities, tests for web attacks, checks certificates, produces a ranked report with fixes. Scout monitors every step. Every action is logged. The results are auditor-ready.

The user does not switch tools. They do not learn new interfaces. They ask for what they need. Carina delivers.
