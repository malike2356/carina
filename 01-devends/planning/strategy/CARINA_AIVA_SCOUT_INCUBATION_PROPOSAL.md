## The Problem

Companies are adopting AI agents faster than they can secure them.

A recent survey found that 75% of enterprises are piloting AI agents. But these agents have no governance. They operate without monitoring. They make decisions without audit trails. They access systems without permission boundaries.

The result: every CISO is terrified. Procurement is blocking adoption. Legal is demanding compliance documentation that doesn't exist. The AI revolution is happening, but the trust layer is missing.

At the same time, businesses want AI workers. They want to hire an AI employee who handles email, manages calendars, processes invoices, and answers questions. But the current options are either:

- Pure SaaS AI workers with no governance and no human backup; hope nothing breaks
- Enterprise AI platforms that cost six figures and take months to deploy

Neither works for the small and medium business that just wants an AI employee who shows up and works.

## The Solution

**Three products. One platform.**

### Carina; The Universal AI Platform

Carina is an AI agent operating system. It orchestrates multiple AI models behind the scenes. It gives businesses a single interface to deploy AI agents for any task in any industry.

Think of Carina like an operating system. Windows provides the foundation; developers build applications on top. Carina provides the AI foundation; businesses build their specific workflows on top.

Key capabilities:
- Self-hosting; deploy on your own servers, full data control
- White-labelling; rebrand Carina as your own AI platform
- Multi-model; use any AI model (OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, local models)
- Memory; per-agent memory that grows over time
- Skills; pre-built and custom skills for any workflow
- Channels; email, WhatsApp, Slack, Telegram, web chat
- Extensions; plugin system for custom tools and integrations

### Aiva; The AI Employee

Aiva is the commercial product built on Carina. Businesses hire a named AI employee; Sarah, your Property Manager Aiva; who works 24/7 across email, WhatsApp, Slack, and web.

What makes Aiva different from every other AI employee platform:

1. **Built on a real VA business.** VERLOX already operates a human VA company. Aiva is trained on real workflows from real clients. When the AI cannot handle something, a human VA takes over; in the same channel, invisible to the client. No one else in the market can offer this.

2. **Scout-governed from day one.** Every action Aiva takes is monitored, logged, and auditable. Clients get compliance reporting that no other AI employee platform provides.

3. **Three minutes to hire.** Describe what you need. Aiva is ready. No technical setup. No API keys. No developer required.

Pre-built roles: Property Manager, Outreach Coordinator, Financial Analyst, Content Assistant, Calendar Manager, Customer Support. Custom roles on request.

### Scout; The Trust Layer

Scout is the governance engine that monitors every AI agent action. It provides:

- Full audit trail; every message sent, every action taken, every escalation logged
- Kill switch; instant agent pause or termination
- Compliance reporting; SOC 2 ready, GDPR compliant
- Permission boundaries; agents cannot act outside their defined scope
- Human override; human VAs can step in at any moment

Scout is sold standalone as a monitoring layer for other AI platforms, and embedded in every Carina deployment and every Aiva. Two revenue streams from one technology.
