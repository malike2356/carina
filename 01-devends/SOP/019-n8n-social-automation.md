# n8n: Personal Social Media Automation

**Status:** Documented, not implemented
**Date:** 24 June 2026
**Purpose:** Personal brand growth, not Propreneur promotion

---

## Use Cases

### 1. Engagement Alerts (do this first)

**Trigger:** New LinkedIn comment on your post
**Action:** Forward comment text to you on Telegram instantly

Why: LinkedIn rewards fast replies. Reply within minutes instead of hours. Impressions double.
n8n watches for comment notifications, parses the text, and sends to you via Telegram.

---

### 2. Content Recycling

**Trigger:** Scheduled (monthly)
**Action:** Identify top 3 performing posts from archive, reformat, reschedule 6-8 weeks later

Why: Your best content works twice. No new writing required. Top posts already proven (653 impressions, 23 likes).
n8n scans your post history, ranks by engagement, and queues top performers for republishing.

---

### 3. Event Live-Posting

**Trigger:** Scheduled during event time window
**Action:** Publish pre-written posts on schedule while you are in the room

Why: You attend IPN, Central London Property Network, and similar events. You look present online while networking offline.
Write 3-4 posts before the event. n8n publishes during the event. No phone in hand required.

---

### 4. Industry Signal Monitoring

**Trigger:** New post from monitored accounts (Salim Miah, Brendan Quinn, key property investors)
**Action:** Send Telegram summary when they post

Why: Engage within minutes. They notice. This is how relationships compound.
n8n watches LinkedIn or RSS for key accounts, summarizes, and alerts you.

---

### 5. Weekly Performance Report

**Trigger:** Every Sunday
**Action:** Pull LinkedIn analytics, send 5-line summary to Telegram

Why: Learn what works without logging into LinkedIn analytics. Over a month you know exactly which topics and formats to repeat.
n8n aggregates impressions, likes, comments, new followers, formats the report, and delivers it.

---

## Priority Order

1. Engagement alerts: highest leverage, simplest setup
2. Industry signal monitoring: compound relationships
3. Weekly performance report: data-driven content
4. Content recycling: multiply existing assets
5. Event live-posting: situational, high impact when used
