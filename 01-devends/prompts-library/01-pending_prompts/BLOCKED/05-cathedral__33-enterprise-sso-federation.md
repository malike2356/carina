# Prompt 33: Enterprise SSO Federation (Phase 05)

**Phase:** 05 - The Cathedral
**Priority:** P1
**Depends on:** Phase 04
**Target time:** 2-3 weeks
**Platform:** Identity Broker Service, SAML 2.0 / OIDC

## What This Builds

Federated Identity Broker service enabling seamless cross-organization single sign-on across NHS Trusts, private hospitals, and regional healthcare systems.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Identity broker federates SAML 2.0, NHS CIS, and Entra ID authentication.
- [ ] Clinicians access multi-trust organizations with a single unified identity.
- [ ] JIT (Just-In-Time) provisioning creates clinician accounts dynamically upon verified login.
