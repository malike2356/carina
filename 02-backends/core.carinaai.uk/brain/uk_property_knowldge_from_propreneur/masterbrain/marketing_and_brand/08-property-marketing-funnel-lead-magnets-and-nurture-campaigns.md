# Property Marketing Funnel, Lead Magnets, and Nurture Campaigns

## The Marketing Funnel

The property marketing funnel flows top-to-bottom through distinct stages, each building on the last. The lifetime value of a client through the full funnel is approximately **£18K+**.

```
Ideal Client (Top)
    |
Lead Magnet (Value)
    |
Nurture Campaign (KLT - Know Like Trust)
    |
Low-Ticket Offer (£24.99 VIP Investor List)
    |
Mid-Ticket Offer (Discovery Day - £3K to £10K)
    |
High-Ticket Offer (12-month coaching - £12K+)
```

### Funnel Stages

- **Lead Magnet (Value):** Free resource offered in exchange for contact details. Entry point for all new leads.
- **Nurture Campaign (KLT):** 52-week automated email sequence building Know, Like, Trust.
- **Low-Ticket Offer:** £24.99/month VIP Investor List. Low barrier, "forgettable amount" pricing.
- **Mid-Ticket Offer:** Discovery Day at £3K-£10K. One-to-one engagement.
- **High-Ticket Offer:** 12-month coaching programme at £12K+. Full transformation.

## Know Like Trust (KLT) Framework

Three stages of relationship-building before a prospect buys.

### Know (Awareness)
- Lead magnet
- Paid ads
- Social media presence
- SEO blog content
- Goal: Prospect becomes aware you exist and you solve property problems.

### Like (Positive Feeling)
- Value content (education, market insights, deal breakdowns)
- Authentic, consistent presence
- Helpful without asking for anything
- Goal: Prospect feels positively about you and your brand.

### Trust (Belief in Delivery)
- Testimonials
- Case studies with real numbers
- Documented results
- Social proof
- Goal: Prospect believes you can deliver what you promise.

### 52-Week Nurture Campaign

- One email per week, same time, same day
- Total duration: 52 weeks (one full year)
- Fully AI-automated possible
- Content themes: property education, local market insights, inspiration
- Goal: Move prospect to book a sales call
- AI can write all 52 emails in a single session

#### Content Rotation Schedule

1. Welcome + value
2. Case study
3. Market insight
4. Education
5. Social proof
6. Soft offer
7. Repeat cycle

## Lead Magnet Types for Property Sourcing

### Lead Magnet Ideas

- **Local intelligence reports:** e.g. "Warrington Buying Hotspot Maps 2026"
- **Deal analysis tools:** Calculators, ROI sheets, deal analysers
- **Case studies:** Deal breakdowns with real numbers
- **Free reports / ebooks:** Downloadable education
- **Deal alert subscriptions:** Be first to know about new deals
- **Area guides and market reports:** Local market deep dives
- **Viewing checklists:** What to look for when viewing a property
- **Joint venture partnership guides:** How to structure JV deals
- **Refurbishment cost guides:** Typical costs per property type
- **Tenant demand reports:** What tenants want, where

### Lead Magnet Creation via AI

- Use AI to generate 20 different lead magnet ideas
- Test with paid ads at **£10/day** for **5 days**
- Analyse which lead magnet converts best
- Scale ad spend on the winning lead magnets
- **Cost per lead:** Approximately **£12/lead** at £10/day ad spend

## Nurture Automation Workflow

### Trigger-to-Notification Pipeline

```
Investor fills in landing page form
    |
Tag added: "New Investor Lead"
    |
Auto-SMS sent immediately
    |
Wait 12 hours
    |
Auto-email with content from nurture sequence
    |
Tag updated
    |
Repeat weekly for 52 weeks
    |
When SMS responded to:
    |-> AI reads and crafts reply
    |-> Notification: "Call this lead now"
```

### Workflow Steps (Detailed)

1. **Trigger:** Investor completes landing page form.
2. **Tag:** Lead tagged "New Investor Lead" in CRM.
3. **SMS:** Automated SMS sent immediately after form submission.
4. **Wait:** 12-hour delay before first email.
5. **Email:** Automated email with content from the 52-week nurture sequence.
6. **Tag:** Lead tag updated to reflect position in nurture sequence.
7. **Repeat:** Weekly for 52 weeks.
8. **SMS Response Handling:** When lead responds to SMS, AI reads the message and crafts a human-quality reply.
9. **Notification:** System notifies the team: "Call this lead now."

## 52-Week Email Campaign Architecture

### Delivery Schedule
- Same day every week
- Same time every week
- One email per week
- Total: 52 emails (one full year)

### Content Rotation
1. **Welcome + Value:** Introduction, what to expect, free resource.
2. **Case Study:** Real deal breakdown with numbers, before/after.
3. **Market Insight:** Local property market data, trends, analysis.
4. **Education:** How-to content, strategy, methodology.
5. **Social Proof:** Testimonial, client success story, results.
6. **Soft Offer:** Low-pressure invitation to book a call or join VIP list.
7. Repeat from step 1.

### AI Automation
- AI can write all 52 emails in one session
- Fully automated send schedule
- Personalisation via CRM tags and lead data

## SEO Blog Content

### Strategy
- Use AI to generate **20+ SEO-optimised blog posts** at once
- Target local keywords (e.g. "buying property in [city name]", "property investment [area name]")
- Publish on the website blog section
- Google crawls and ranks content over **3-6 months**

### Purpose
- Blog does not need direct readership
- Primary function: SEO ranking for Google searches
- Secondary function: Credibility when someone Googles your name and finds expert content

### Key Numbers
- 20+ posts minimum for initial SEO push
- 3-6 months for Google ranking
- Local keyword targeting essential for property sourcing
