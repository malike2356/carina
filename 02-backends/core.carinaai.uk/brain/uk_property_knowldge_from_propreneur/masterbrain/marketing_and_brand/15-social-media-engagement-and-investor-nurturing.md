# Social Media Engagement and Investor Nurturing

## The Core Problem: Engaging Without Selling

Most property sourcers make the same mistake: they ignore their database until they have something to sell, then bombard investors with deals. This destroys trust.

**The rule:** Prospects only become clients when you add enough value, engage with them on a regular basis, and they know you, like you, and trust you enough to pay you money.

A prospect is NOT a client until they have paid you. Everything before that is relationship-building.

---

## The 7-Interaction Rule

People need **7 meaningful interactions** with you before they will trust you enough to buy from you - especially for high-value transactions like property deals (£2,000-£5,000+ fees).

**Implications:**
- One email will not close a deal
- Consistent, value-first engagement is required
- Engagement must happen BEFORE you have something to sell

---

## Engagement Frequency Framework

### VIP Database (premium tier - paying monthly subscribers)
- Monday at 2:00 PM - deals emailed to VIP list 24 hours before general release
- Goal: reward loyalty, create urgency and exclusivity

### General Database (all investors)
- Tuesday at 9:00 AM - deals released to full database

### Mid-week Value Content (all)
- 1-2 times per week: value-add emails (NOT selling)
- Examples: property market update, tip from a recent viewing, area research insight, regulatory news

### Key Principle
Train your investors to expect your emails. Consistency at a fixed time creates anticipation. You want investors sitting by their computer at Tuesday 9am waiting for your deal email.

---

## Content Mix Formula

| Content Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Deal alerts | Weekly (fixed day/time) | Revenue driver |
| Value content (tips, insights, news) | 2-3x per week | Trust building |
| Personal/behind the scenes | Occasionally | Likability |
| Success stories / student wins | Regularly | Social proof |
| Motivational / mindset | Occasionally | Engagement |

**Never:** Constant selling without value. People will unsubscribe or disengage.

**Rule:** Value comes before the sale. Always.

## Social Media Strategy for Property Sourcers

### Why You Must Be on Social Media

Social media is where prospects verify you are who you say you are. Before an investor sends you £3,000+, they will check your social media. If it's empty or unprofessional, you lose them.

The first thing most people do in the morning is check social media. You need to be where your clients are.

### Platforms to Prioritise

| Platform | Primary Strength | Content Type |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Highest engagement for property business | Reels, posts, stories, carousels |
| Facebook | Relationship building, groups, events | Posts, live videos, groups |
| TikTok | Growing reach, younger investor demographic | Short-form video |
| YouTube | Long-form authority content | Viewings, educational content |
| LinkedIn | Corporate/professional investors | Articles, professional updates |

**Most interactive platform for property business:** Instagram (as of recent experience).

### Social Media Branding Rules

1. **Consistent branding across all platforms** - same logo, same colours, same profile image
2. **Professional headshot** - no silly images, nothing unrelated to your business
3. **Business-focused content** - property viewings, refurbs, deal analysis, area research
4. **Social proof** - share investor wins, student successes, completed deals
5. **Some personal content** - keeps it human; events, behind-the-scenes, hobbies (sparingly)

**Key:** People buy from people. Be real, be authentic. Avoid fake "Lamborghini lifestyle" content - it destroys trust with serious investors.

## Multi-Channel Engagement System

Engage clients on multiple fronts simultaneously:

1. **Email database** - weekly deals, mid-week value
2. **Social media** - Instagram, Facebook, TikTok
3. **YouTube** - property viewing tours, area research vlogs
4. **WhatsApp group** - VIP investors (fast deal alerts)
5. **In-person events** - networking events, educational evenings
6. **One-to-one calls** - high-value investor relationships

Each touchpoint builds another layer of the know-like-trust relationship.

---

## Engagement Agenda - Always Have One

Every time you contact a prospect or client, have an agenda. Don't go in to sell every time. Options for your agenda:

- Add value: share a tip, insight, or market update
- Ask a question: get feedback on the type of deals they want
- Share a win: a deal you've just closed, a happy investor review
- Invite: to an event, webinar, or property tour
- Sell: present a deal (maximum 1x per week in formal deal email)

---

## Database Segmentation for Engagement

Segment your investor database by:

| Segment | Characteristics | Engagement Approach |
|---|---|---|
| VIP / Active investors | Paid fee, actively buying | Priority deal alerts, personal calls |
| Warm leads | Engaged, not yet purchased | Nurture with value, move to VIP |
| Cold / new | Recently joined database | Welcome sequence, educational content |
| Past clients | Have bought before | Re-engagement campaigns, upsell |

## Engagement Content Ideas for Social Media

### Property Business Content
- "Day in the life" of a property sourcer
- Property viewing tour (video walkthrough)
- Before and after refurbishment
- Area research trip - what you found
- Deal breakdown (anonymised numbers)
- Common investor mistakes to avoid
- Finance explained in simple terms

### Educational Content
- How to calculate ROI (quick explainer)
- What to look for in an HMO area
- How to spot a money pit on a viewing
- Top 5 things to check before buying
- What is R2R and how does it work?

### Social Proof
- Investor testimonials (written, video)
- Student deal wins
- Completed property transformations
- Event highlights

### Polls and Engagement Posts
- "What strategy are you investing in?" (poll)
- "What's your biggest challenge right now?" (question)
- "Which of these deals would you buy?" (A/B comparison)

## The Know-Like-Trust Framework Applied to Sourcing

**Know:** They've seen your content, visited your social media, read your emails.
**Like:** They enjoy your personality, relate to your story, find your content helpful.
**Trust:** They believe you know what you're doing and won't waste their money.

Building all three takes time. You cannot shortcut it. Every engagement moves someone forward on the KLT scale.

**Target:** Get every prospect to 7+ meaningful interactions before attempting a deal close.
