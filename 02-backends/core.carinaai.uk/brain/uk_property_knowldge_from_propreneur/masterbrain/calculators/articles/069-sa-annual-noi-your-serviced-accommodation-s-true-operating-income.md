# SA Annual NOI: Your Serviced Accommodation's True Operating Income

Published: Mon, 11 Jan 2027 09:00:00 +0000

Slug: sa-annual-noi-your-serviced-accommodation-s-true-operating-income

Revenue is vanity. NOI is sanity. For serviced accommodation, the gap between them is wider than any other property strategy because the operating costs are so high.\n\nUsing the same Portsmouth SA with £28,105 annual revenue, your costs might be: platform fees at 15% = £4,216, cleaning at £40 per stay with an average 3-night stay and 85 bookings = £3,400, management at 20% = £5,621, utilities = £2,400, maintenance = £1,500, insurance = £600, supplies and consumables = £1,200. Total costs: £18,937. Your NOI is £9,168.\n\nCompare that to a BTL on the same property. The BTL NOI was roughly £7,850. The SA delivers more income but the margin is tighter when you account for all the costs.\n\nThe SA NOI margin is your NOI divided by revenue. In this case, £9,168 divided by £28,105 is 32.6%. A healthy SA operation has an NOI margin of 30% to 45%. Below 30%, your costs are too high or your revenue is too low.\n\nTrack your SA NOI monthly. If the margin drops below 30%, review your costs. Platform fees are hard to reduce, but cleaning and management can often be optimised.

**The formula**

```
Operating Costs = Cleaning + Platform Commission + Management + Utilities + Channel Manager + Insurance + Maintenance

NOI = Annual Revenue - Annual Operating Costs
```

**Why this matters**

SA NOI margin should be 30% to 45%. Below 30%, your costs are eating too much of your revenue. SA delivers higher top-line income than BTL but the margin is thinner relative to turnover.
