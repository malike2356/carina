# Portfolio Equity: How Much of Your Portfolio You Actually Own

Published: Mon, 25 Oct 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: portfolio-equity-how-much-of-your-portfolio-you-actually-own

---

Portfolio equity is your portfolio value minus your total mortgage debt. It is the amount you would walk away with if you sold everything tomorrow. It is your true net worth in property.\n\nIf your portfolio is worth £1.5 million and your total mortgages are £900,000, your portfolio equity is £600,000. That is your equity. But selling everything would trigger capital gains tax and selling costs, so your net equity after all costs would be lower.\n\nPortfolio equity grows through three mechanisms: capital appreciation, mortgage repayment, and retained cashflow. A well-managed portfolio should see equity grow faster than the market because you are paying down debt and reinvesting income.\n
Track your portfolio equity annually. If it is growing faster than the market, you are actively creating value. If it is growing slower, your properties are underperforming or your costs are too high.

**The formula**

```
Portfolio Equity = Total Portfolio Value - Total Outstanding Mortgage Debt
```

**Why this matters**

Portfolio equity is your true net worth in property. Track it annually. It should grow faster than the market through mortgage repayment and retained income. If it is not, something is wrong.
