# Refurb Payback Period: How Long Until Your Renovation Pays for Itself?

Published: Mon, 06 Dec 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: refurb-payback-period-how-long-until-your-renovation-pays-for-itself

---

Refurb payback period measures how long it takes for the increased rent from your refurbishment to recover the cost. If you spend £10,000 on a kitchen refurb that increases rent by £100 a month, the payback period is 100 months, or 8.3 years. That is a long time.\n\nA good refurb investment has a payback period of 12 to 36 months. A new bathroom costing £4,000 that adds £200 a month in rent pays back in 20 months. That is excellent. A full kitchen costing £10,000 that adds £200 a month pays back in 50 months, which is marginal.\n\nThe payback period helps you prioritise refurbishment spending. Focus on the improvements with the shortest payback period. A fresh coat of paint and new flooring costing £2,000 that adds £50 a month pays back in 40 months. That is better than a full kitchen at 50 months.\n
Calculate the payback period before you spend any money on refurbishment. If the payback period exceeds your expected holding period, the refurb is not worth doing.

**The formula**

```
Payback (months) = Refurb Cost / (Uplift in Monthly Rent or Cashflow)
```

**Why this matters**

Refurb payback period of 12 to 36 months is good. Above 60 months, the investment is unlikely to pay back before you sell. Prioritise renovations with the shortest payback.
