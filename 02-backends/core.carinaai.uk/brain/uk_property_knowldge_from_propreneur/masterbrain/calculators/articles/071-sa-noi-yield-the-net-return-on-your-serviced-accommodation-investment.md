# SA NOI Yield: The Net Return on Your Serviced Accommodation Investment

Published: Mon, 18 Jan 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: sa-noi-yield-the-net-return-on-your-serviced-accommodation-investment

---

If gross yield is what agents quote, NOI yield is what you earn. For SA, this is the number that matters because the cost structure is so different from traditional letting.\n\nSA NOI yield is your annual NOI divided by the property value. Using our example: £9,168 divided by £250,000 equals 3.7%. A BTL on the same property delivers roughly 3.4%. The SA premium is 0.3%.\n\nThat 0.3% premium represents the reward for running a hospitality business instead of a rental business. For many investors, that is not enough. But there are ways to improve it.\n\nIf you achieve 80% occupancy instead of 70%, your NOI jumps to roughly £12,500 and your NOI yield rises to 5%. Now the premium over BTL is 1.6%, which starts to look attractive.\n\nIf you self-manage and save the 20% management fee, your NOI jumps further. The best SA operators achieve NOI yields of 6% to 8% through a combination of high occupancy, premium pricing, and efficient operations.\n\nThe question is whether you have the skills and time to operate at that level. If not, SA might underperform a simple BTL.

**The formula**

```
NOI Yield (%) = (Annual NOI / Purchase Price) × 100
```

**Why this matters**

SA NOI yield is typically 3% to 5% for managed properties and 5% to 8% for self-managed. If you cannot achieve at least 4%, a standard BTL might be a better use of your capital.
