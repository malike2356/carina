# DSCR: The Ratio That Determines Whether Your Property Can Afford Its Debt

Published: Mon, 27 Sep 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: dscr-the-ratio-that-determines-whether-your-property-can-afford-its-de

---

DSCR is your Net Operating Income divided by your total annual debt payments. It tells the lender whether you can comfortably make your mortgage payments. A DSCR of 1.3 means your property generates 30% more income than needed to cover the mortgage.\n\nFor a UK BTL, lenders typically require a DSCR of 1.25 to 1.45 depending on whether you are a basic or higher rate taxpayer. For commercial property, the minimum is typically 1.3 to 1.5.\n\nIf your DSCR is below 1.0, the property cannot afford its own mortgage. You are subsidising it from other income. If the DSCR is between 1.0 and 1.25, the property is barely covering its debt and any increase in costs or interest rates could push it into the danger zone.\n
Track DSCR quarterly on every property. If any property's DSCR drops below 1.2, it needs attention. Either increase the rent, reduce costs, or consider selling.

**The formula**

```
DSCR = NOI / Annual Debt Service (P + I)
```

**Why this matters**

DSCR above 1.25 is the lender's minimum. Below 1.0, the property cannot afford its mortgage. Track DSCR quarterly on every property. A dropping DSCR is an early warning sign.
