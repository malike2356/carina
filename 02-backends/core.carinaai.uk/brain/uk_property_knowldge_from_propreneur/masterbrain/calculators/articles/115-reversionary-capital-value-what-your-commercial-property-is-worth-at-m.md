# Reversionary Capital Value: What Your Commercial Property Is Worth at Market Rent

Published: Mon, 21 Jun 2027 09:00:00 +0000

Slug: reversionary-capital-value-what-your-commercial-property-is-worth-at-m

---

Reversionary capital value answers the question: what would this property be worth if the rent were at the full market rate? It is the ERV divided by the market yield.\n\nIf the estimated rental value is £35,000 and the market yield is 7%, the reversionary capital value is £35,000 divided by 0.07, which equals £500,000. The current capital value, based on current rent of £25,000, is £357,143. The difference is £142,857. That is the upside from raising the rent.\n\nThis upside is why investors pay a premium for properties with reversionary potential. The current yield might be low, but the ability to increase the rent creates hidden value. Professional investors underwrite the reversionary value, not just the current value.\n\nThe risk is that the ERV never materialises. If the market declines or the tenant leaves, the reversionary value might be lower than expected. Conservative investors assume a discount on the ERV to account for this uncertainty.

**The formula**

```
Reversionary Capital Value = ERV / Target Yield
```

**Why this matters**

Reversionary capital value shows the full potential of a commercial property. The gap between current value and reversionary value represents the value you can unlock. Be conservative with ERV assumptions.
