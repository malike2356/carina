# SA Annual Revenue: Projecting Income for Your Serviced Accommodation

Published: Wed, 23 Dec 2026 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: sa-annual-revenue-projecting-income-for-your-serviced-accommodation

---

Serviced accommodation is a hospitality business. Your income is not monthly rent. It is nightly rates times the number of nights you are booked.

Annual revenue for an SA property is your average nightly rate multiplied by the number of nights you expect to be occupied. If your average rate is £110 and you achieve 70% occupancy, your annual revenue is £110 times 365 times 0.7, which equals £28,105.

Compare that to the same property as a BTL. A £250,000 property renting for £1,100 a month gives you £13,200 a year. The SA version delivers more than double the gross income.

But that extra income comes with extra costs. Platform fees on Airbnb or Booking.com are 10% to 18%. Cleaning costs are higher. Utility costs are included and usage is higher. Management is more intensive.

The key metric in SA is not annual revenue. It is NOI after all costs. A property that generates £28,000 in revenue but costs £20,000 to run is no better than a BTL that generates £13,000 and costs £3,000 to run.

In Portsmouth, SA annual revenue ranges from £20,000 to £50,000 depending on location, size, and quality. City centre properties near the dockyard, university, and tourist attractions perform best.

**The formula**

```
Annual Revenue = Nightly Rate × Occupancy Rate × 365
```

**Why this matters**

SA annual revenue is driven by ADR and occupancy. A well-run SA can generate two to three times the gross income of a BTL. But the costs are proportionally higher. Track NOI, not revenue.
