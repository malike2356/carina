# Residual Land Value: What a Piece of Land Is Actually Worth for Development

Published: Mon, 31 May 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: residual-land-value-what-a-piece-of-land-is-actually-worth-for-develop

---

RLV is the most important number for buying development land. It works backwards from the GDV to tell you the maximum you can pay for the land while still achieving your target profit.\n\nThe calculation starts with GDV. Subtract all development costs, finance costs, contingency, and your target profit. The remainder is what the land is worth to you.\n\nFor our £560,000 GDV project with total costs of £390,000 (excluding land), finance of £30,000, contingency of £21,000, and a target profit of £85,000 (15% of GDV), the RLV is £560,000 minus £390,000 minus £30,000 minus £21,000 minus £85,000, which equals £34,000. That is what the land is worth to you.\n\nIf the seller wants £150,000 for the land, the numbers do not work. You cannot achieve your target profit at that price. You either negotiate the land price down, find a way to increase GDV or reduce costs, or walk away.\n\nRLV protects you from paying too much for land. If you pay more than the RLV, your profit falls below your target. If you pay significantly more, the project becomes loss-making.

**The formula**

```
RLV = GDV - Build Costs - Contingency - Professional Fees - Finance Costs - Profit Requirement
```

**Why this matters**

RLV tells you the maximum you can pay for land and still make your target profit. If the seller wants more than the RLV, walk away. Paying too much for land is the most common mistake in development.
