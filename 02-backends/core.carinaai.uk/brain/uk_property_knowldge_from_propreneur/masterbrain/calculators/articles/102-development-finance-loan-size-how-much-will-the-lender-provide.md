# Development Finance Loan Size: How Much Will the Lender Provide?

Published: Wed, 05 May 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: development-finance-loan-size-how-much-will-the-lender-provide

---

Development finance is different from a standard mortgage. The lender provides the funds in stages as the build progresses. The loan size is determined by two numbers: Loan to Cost and Loan to GDV.\n\nThe maximum loan is the lower of two calculations. Loan to Cost: typically 70% to 80% of your total development cost. Loan to GDV: typically 60% to 65% of your GDV. The lender takes the smaller number to ensure the loan is covered by the completed value.\n\nFor our £446,500 GDC and £560,000 GDV project, the maximum loan by LTC at 75% is £334,875. The maximum by LTV-GDV at 65% is £364,000. The lender will offer £334,875, the lower of the two.\n\nThe remaining capital must come from your own equity. If the loan covers £334,875 and your GDC is £446,500, you need £111,625 of your own cash. That is your equity requirement.\n\nThe loan is drawn in stages. Typically 30% on acquisition, 30% after foundations, 20% after watertight, 10% after first fix, and 10% on completion. Each draw requires a valuation or surveyor sign-off.

**The formula**

```
Max by LTC = GDC × Max LTC (typically 80%)
Max by LTV-GDV = GDV × Max LTV-GDV (typically 65%)
Development Loan = min(Max by LTC, Max by LTV-GDV)
```

**Why this matters**

Development finance is the lower of 70% to 80% LTC and 60% to 65% LTV-GDV. The remaining equity must come from your own cash. The loan draws in stages against valuation milestones.
