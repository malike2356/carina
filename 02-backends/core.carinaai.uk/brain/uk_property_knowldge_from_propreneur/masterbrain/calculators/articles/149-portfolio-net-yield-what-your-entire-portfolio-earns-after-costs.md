# Portfolio Net Yield: What Your Entire Portfolio Earns After Costs

Published: Mon, 18 Oct 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: portfolio-net-yield-what-your-entire-portfolio-earns-after-costs

---

Portfolio net yield is your total net operating income across all properties divided by their total value. It is a more honest measure than gross yield because it accounts for costs.\n\nIf your total NOI across four properties is £32,000 and the total value is £1 million, your portfolio net yield is 3.2%. This is the real income return your portfolio delivers after management, maintenance, insurance, and voids.\n\nPortfolio net yield should be higher than your average mortgage rate for positive leverage. If your weighted average mortgage rate is 4.5% and your portfolio net yield is 3.2%, you have negative leverage across the portfolio. This is common on the South Coast but worth knowing.\n
If the portfolio net yield is below 3%, you are heavily dependent on capital growth. That is a valid strategy but a high-risk one. Consider whether selling lower-performing properties and reinvesting in higher-yielding ones would improve the portfolio yield.

**The formula**

```
Portfolio Net Yield (%) = ((Total Annual Rent - Total Operating Costs) / Total Portfolio Value) × 100
```

**Why this matters**

Portfolio net yield should be above 3% for a healthy income position. Below 3%, you are dependent on capital growth. Compare it to your weighted average mortgage rate to understand your leverage position.
