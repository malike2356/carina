# BMV Maximum Purchase Price: How Much Can You Pay and Still Pull Cash Out?

The maximum purchase price for no-money-down is the highest price you can pay and still achieve your target cash posit ion. It is the purchase price that puts your net cash position exactly where you want it.\n\nIf you want zero cash left in and you have a remortgage LTV of 75% on a market value of £200,000, your maximum purchase price is the remortgage amount (£150,000) minus your purchase costs. If purchase costs are roughly 3%, your maximum purchase price is £145,600.\n
If you are willing to leave £10,000 in the deal, your maximum purchase price is £15,000 higher. That is £159,600. Now your discount is only 20% instead of 27%. The trade-off between discount and cash left in is a personal decision based on your capital position.\n
Experienced BMV investors have a maximum purchase price for every deal. They calculate it before they negotiate. If the seller will not come down to their maximum, they walk away. That discipline is what makes them successful.

**The formula**

```
Max Purchase (NMD) = Market Value - (Breakeven Discount % / 100 × Market Value)
```

**Why this matters**

Calculate your maximum purchase price before you negotiate. If the seller cannot meet it, walk away. The discipline of walking away from a deal that does not meet your numbers is what separates successful investors.
