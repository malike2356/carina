# Occupancy Rate: The Metric That Drives SA and HMO Returns

Published: Mon, 29 Nov 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: occupancy-rate-the-metric-that-drives-sa-and-hmo-returns

---

Occupancy rate is the inverse of void rate. It is the percentage of time or rooms that are occupied and generating income. For HMOs and SA, occupancy rate is the most important operational metric because it directly drives revenue.\n\nFor an HMO with five rooms, occupancy of four rooms is 80%. For an SA property, occupancy of 250 nights out of 365 is 68.5%. The occupancy rate determines whether the higher costs of these strategies are justified.\n\nA single percentage point of occupancy can be worth hundreds or thousands of pounds. For a five-bed HMO with rooms at £600 each, every 1% increase in occupancy adds roughly £360 a year to revenue. For an SA with a £110 ADR, every 1% adds roughly £400 a year.\n
Track occupancy monthly. If it drops below your target for three consecutive months, take action. Adjust pricing, improve marketing, review your listing quality, or increase your minimum stay requirements.

**The formula**

```
Occupancy (%) = (Occupied Nights or Occupied Room-Nights / Total Available Nights) × 100
```
