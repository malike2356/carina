# Portfolio Gross Yield: The Overall Income Return Across All Your Properties

Published: Wed, 13 Oct 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: portfolio-gross-yield-the-overall-income-return-across-all-your-proper

---

Portfolio gross yield is the total annual rent from all your properties divided by their total market value. It gives you the headline income return of your entire portfolio.\n\nIf you own four properties worth a combined £1 million with total rent of £52,000, your portfolio gross yield is 5.2%. That is the starting point for understanding your portfolio performance.\n\nPortfolio gross yield trends tell you whether your portfolio is getting better or worse over time. If the yield is declining, either your rents are not keeping up with property values or you are adding lower-yielding properties. Both need attention.\n
A declining portfolio gross yield is not necessarily bad if the capital growth is strong. But it is a warning sign that you should investigate. Are your properties keeping up with inflation? Are you buying in lower-yielding areas? The trend tells the story.

**The formula**

```
Portfolio Gross Yield (%) = (Total Annual Rent / Total Portfolio Value) × 100
```

**Why this matters**

Portfolio gross yield gives you the headline income picture. A declining yield needs investigation. It could mean strong capital growth or underperforming rents.
