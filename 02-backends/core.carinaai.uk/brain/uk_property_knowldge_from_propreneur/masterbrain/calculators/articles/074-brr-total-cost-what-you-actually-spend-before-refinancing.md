# BRR Total Cost: What You Actually Spend Before Refinancing

Buy, refurbish, refinance. BRR is the strategy that lets you recycle capital and build a portfolio faster than traditional BTL. But the total cost calculation is critical because it determines whether the refinance leaves you with cash in or cash out.\n\nYour total cost is everything you spend before you refinance. Purchase price. Stamp duty. Legal fees. Survey fees. Refurbishment costs. Project management. Bridging finance interest. Holding costs like council tax and utilities during the works.\n\nFor a typical BRR in Portsmouth, the numbers might look like this. Purchase price: £150,000. Stamp duty on second home: £6,250. Legal: £1,500. Survey: £500. Refurb: £30,000. Project management: £3,000. Bridging interest for six months: £4,500. Council tax and utilities during works: £1,200. Total cost: £196,950.\n\nThe total cost is the number you compare against your after-refurb value (ARV). If your ARV is £220,000, your equity is £23,050 before refinance costs. That equity determines how much cash you can pull out.\n\nIf the total cost exceeds the ARV, the deal does not work. You cannot refinance enough to get your cash back. That is why BRR investors spend so much time getting the total cost right before they buy.

**The formula**

```
Total Cost = Purchase Price + SDLT + Legal Fees + Survey Fees + Refurb Cost + Finance Costs + Contingency
```

**Why this matters**

BRR total cost is the sum of everything you spend before refinancing. If it exceeds the after-refurb value, the deal is dead. Get this number right before you commit.
