# BTL Management Fee: What Letting Agents Actually Charge

Published: Wed, 21 Oct 2026 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: btl-management-fee-what-letting-agents-actually-charge

---

Most BTL investors use a letting agent at some point. The standard fee for full management is 8% to 12% of gross rent. For a £1,100-a-month property, that is £88 to £132 a month, or £1,056 to £1,584 a year.

Full management typically covers tenant finding, referencing, rent collection, and managing repairs. Some agents also cover inventory checks and deposit protection. The more they cover, the higher the fee.

There are cheaper options. Tenant find-only services cost 50% to 100% of the first month's rent and nothing after that. This can be better value if you are comfortable managing the property yourself once the tenant is in place.

For a £1,100-a-month property, find-only costs roughly £550 to £1,100 upfront. Full management costs £1,100 to £1,600 every year. If the average tenancy lasts 18 months, find-only works out cheaper in most cases.

On the South Coast, management fees tend to be at the lower end of the range because there is competition among agents. Shop around. A difference of 2% in management fees on a £1 million portfolio is £20,000 a year. That is real money.

**The formula**

```
Annual Management Fee = Annual Effective Rent × Management %
```

**Why this matters**

Management fees range from 8% to 12% of rent for full management. Tenant find-only is cheaper if you can manage the property yourself. Even 1% in fee difference is significant across a portfolio.
