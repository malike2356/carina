# LTV: The Debt Ratio That Governs How Much You Can Borrow

Published: Mon, 20 Sep 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: ltv-the-debt-ratio-that-governs-how-much-you-can-borrow

---

Loan to Value in a leverage context is the same calculation as in mortgage maths. It is your total borrowing divided by your total property value, expressed as a percentage. But when applied to a portfolio of properties, LTV takes on broader significance.\n\nYour portfolio LTV is the sum of all your mortgages divided by the sum of all your property values. If you own three properties worth a combined £750,000 with total mortgages of £500,000, your portfolio LTV is 66.7%.\n\nPortfolio LTV is the number lenders look at when assessing your overall position. If it rises above 75%, you are over-leveraged and vulnerable to interest rate rises or market downturns. If it is below 50%, you are conservatively financed but not making full use of leverage.\n
The sweet spot for portfolio LTV is 50% to 70%. Below 50%, your capital is not working hard enough. Above 70%, you are over-exposed to market movements and most lenders will not extend further credit.

**The formula**

```
LTV (%) = (Outstanding Loan / Property Value) × 100
```

**Why this matters**

Portfolio LTV of 50% to 70% is the sweet spot. Below 50%, you are not leveraging enough. Above 70%, you are over-exposed. Monitor your portfolio LTV at least annually.
