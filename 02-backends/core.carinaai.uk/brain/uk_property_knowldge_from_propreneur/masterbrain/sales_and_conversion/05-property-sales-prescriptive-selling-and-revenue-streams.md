# 101: Property Sales - Prescriptive Selling & Revenue Streams

## Overview

This knowledge file covers the end-to-end approach to selling property sourcing services, from the prescriptive selling methodology to the full revenue stack of a property sourcing business. Extracted from an intensive property entrepreneurship bootcamp.

---

## 1. Prescriptive Selling (The Doctor Approach)

### Core Philosophy

Do not list products. Diagnose needs first - like a doctor.

> "Ask them what they need and what they want, then give them the solution."

You don't show a patient a list of medicines and let them pick. You ask what hurts, run tests, then prescribe. Same applies to property services.

### The Process

1. **Ask diagnostic questions:**
   - What are you looking to achieve?
   - What budget are you working with?
   - What area are you targeting?
   - What is your timeline?
   - What is your investment experience level?
   - Are you investing personally or through a vehicle (Ltd company, SIPP, etc.)?

2. **Listen fully** before offering anything.

3. **Prescribe the specific solution** based on their answers - not your stock offer.

4. **Create products on the spot if needed.** If the client's needs don't match an existing package, build one that does. The offering bends to the client, not the other way around.

### Why It Works

- Builds trust - you're advising, not pitching
- Unearths real needs the client might not articulate
- Positions you as the expert (doctor-patient dynamic)
- Reduces objections because the solution was built for them

## 2. Sales Call Structure

### Step 1: Qualification Call (5-10 minutes)

**Purpose:** Determine mutual fit quickly. No deep selling.

**Opening line:** "This call is just to see if we can help each other."

**What to establish:**
- Are they a serious investor or just browsing?
- Do they have budget/finance in place?
- What are they looking for (property type, price range, area)?
- Can you realistically deliver what they need?

**Gate decision:** If they don't pass qualification, politely end the call. Do not waste time on unqualified leads.

### Step 2: Discovery Call (30-45 minutes)

**Purpose:** Deep dive into problems, present solution, eliminate objections.

**Structure:**
1. **Rapport & context** - recap what you know from the qualification call
2. **Deep needs analysis** - explore goals, fears, past experiences, timelines, numbers
3. **Present the solution** - prescribe the specific product/service package that matches their diagnosis
4. **Pre-empt objections** - address likely concerns before they raise them (see Section 3)
5. **Test the temperature** - "How does that sound to you so far?"

### Step 3: The Close

**Purpose:** Ask for the decision directly. Do not dance around it.

**Key principle:** "It's not been sold until you've seen the gold" - payment must be received.

**Close questions:**
- "Are you ready to move forward with this?"
- "Shall I send you the payment link?"
- "If we can get this started by [date], does that work for you?"

**Do not leave without a yes/no.** A "maybe" is a no. Push for clarity.

**Payment rule:** The deal is not closed until funds have cleared. Verbal agreement is not a sale.

## 3. Handling Objections

### Core Mindset

An objection is not rejection - it is a question in disguise. The client is asking for more information, reassurance, or proof of value.

### Three Most Common Objections and Responses

#### 1. "The fee is too high."

**Root cause:** Value has not yet been proven to the client.

**Response strategy:**
- Reframe around value, not cost
- Break down what they get for the fee (number of deals, access, expertise, time saved)
- Compare against the cost of doing it alone (mistakes, lost opportunities, delay)
- Use social proof: "Other investors found this valuable because..."

**Never discount.** Discounting signals that your price was inflated to begin with.

#### 2. "I want to think about it."

**Root cause:** The client is too scared to say no directly, or doesn't have enough confidence.

**Response strategy:**
- Gently probe: "What specifically would you like to think about?"
- Identify the hidden objection behind the stall
- Address the real concern, not the surface statement
- Offer a low-risk next step (e.g., "Let's book a second call. If you're not convinced by the end, no pressure.")

#### 3. "I need to speak to my partner."

**Root cause:** Genuine - they don't want to make a unilateral financial decision.

**Response strategy:**
- **Bring them on the call.** Do NOT let the client relay the information second-hand.
- Say: "That makes perfect sense. Let's get them on a call together - when people learn together, they earn together. I don't want you to have to explain everything to them; let me do it."
- This ensures both parties hear the same pitch and address objections together.

### Objection Handling Protocol (Step by Step)

1. **Listen fully** - do not interrupt. Let them finish their concern entirely.
2. **Acknowledge** - "I understand why you'd feel that way," or "That's a fair concern."
3. **Reframe / answer** - address the substance directly with facts, examples, or logic.
4. **Confirm** - "Does that make sense?" or "Does that address your concern?"

### Pre-Emptive Objection Elimination

Address common concerns **in your marketing and early conversations** before the client raises them.

**Example:** "Just so you know, our sourcing fee is £3,000 with a 14-day cooling-off period. Are you comfortable with that?"

If the answer is no, **do not book the call.** Save everyone's time.

## 4. Multiple Revenue Streams for a Sourcing Business

A well-structured sourcing business has multiple revenue tiers. Do not rely on a single income source.

| Tier | Product | Price | Type |
|------|---------|-------|------|
| **Low** | VIP Investor Database | £24.99/month | Passive, recurring |
| **Mid** | Sourcing Service Retainer | £1,000 upfront (4 deal opps over 3 months) | One-time / fixed term |
| **Mid** | Sourcing Fee Per Deal | £3,000-£5,000 | Per-transaction |
| **Mid** | Discovery Day | £3,000-£10,000 | One-time (full day) |
| **Mid** | Staging Service | Markup on cost (typically 2x materials) | Per-project |
| **Mid** | Project Management | 10% of builder quote | Per-project |
| **High** | Property Management | 10-15% of monthly rent | Ongoing, recurring |
| **High** | 12-Month Coaching Programme | £12,000+ | Upfront / payment plan |
| **High** | Custom Partnership | £25,000+ (bespoke weekly calls, strategy) | Bespoke |

### 4.1 VIP Investor Database (£24.99/month)

- Passive recurring revenue
- Monthly subscription for curated deal opportunities
- Low barrier to entry - captures bottom of funnel
- High volume potential

### 4.2 Sourcing Service Retainer (£1,000 upfront)

- Client pays £1,000 upfront
- You provide **4 deal opportunities over 3 months**
- If the client completes a purchase within that period, you also earn the per-deal sourcing fee
- This retainer covers your time and filters commitment

### 4.3 Sourcing Fee Per Deal (£3,000-£5,000)

- Charged upon successful property purchase through your sourced deal
- Typically paid at exchange or completion
- This is the core transaction revenue of a sourcing business
- 4 deals × £3,000-£5,000 = £12,000-£20,000 in sourcing fees alone

### 4.4 Discovery Day (£3,000-£10,000)

- Full day of property viewings organised for an investor
- Client gets **first refusal** on all deals shown that day
- Price varies by market density and investor profile

**Case study:** Alistar charged £10,000 for one Discovery Day. He took an investor from Bahrain around HMOs in the local area. The investor purchased 3 deals that same day. £10,000 for a single day's work.

**How to run a Discovery Day:**
1. Select 5-8 strong deal opportunities in the investor's target area/type
2. Arrange a full-day itinerary with staggered viewing times
3. Prepare deal packs for each property (purchase price, refurb costs, projected rent, ROI)
4. Accompany the investor to every viewing - you're the expert guide
5. Have contracts ready to move on the day
6. Collect the Discovery Day fee upfront (not contingent on purchase)

### 4.5 Staging Service

- You furnish and dress properties before sale or rental
- Charge a markup on materials - typically **2x the cost of materials**
- The investor benefits from higher sale price or faster rental
- You earn the staging fee plus sourcing fee on the same property

### 4.6 Project Management (10% of builder quote)

- Manage refurbishment or renovation projects for investors
- Charge **10% of the builder's quote** as your PM fee
- Example: £50,000 builder quote = £5,000 PM fee
- You oversee timelines, quality, and contractor coordination

### 4.7 Property Management (10-15% of monthly rent)

- Ongoing monthly revenue while the property is tenanted
- Charge **10-15% of the monthly rent**
- Example: £1,000/month rent = £100-£150/month ongoing
- Creates long-term client relationship
- Can be scaled with a portfolio of managed properties

### 4.8 12-Month Coaching Programme (£12,000+)

- One-to-one coaching for aspiring property investors
- Monthly calls, deal review, strategy, accountability
- Price point filters for serious clients only
- Content: sourcing, due diligence, financing, legal, portfolio building
- Can be delivered as group or 1:1

### 4.9 Custom Partnership (£25,000+)

- Highest tier - for serious investors or overseas capital
- Bespoke weekly calls, dedicated strategy, deal flow, joint ventures
- Essentially a fractional partnership
- Price point ensures high commitment from both sides

## 5. Low-Ticket to High-Ticket Product Stack

### The Funnel Logic

Every product tier naturally qualifies and filters clients:

| Step | Product | Price | Purpose |
|------|---------|-------|---------|
| **Top of funnel** | VIP Database | £24.99/month | Capture interest, prove value |
| **Middle** | Sourcing Retainer + Deals | £1K-£5K | Serious investors, deal-based |
| **Middle** | Discovery Day | £3K-£10K | High-intent, time-pressed investors |
| **Top tier** | Coaching / Partnership | £12K-£25K+ | High-net-worth, committed |

### Lifetime Value (LTV) of a Client

A client who moves through the full funnel is worth approximately **£18,000+** in total revenue:

- VIP Database: £24.99 × 12 months = £300
- Sourcing (retainer + fee): £1,000 + £4,000 = £5,000
- Property Management (if applicable): £1,200-£1,800/year ongoing
- Coaching/Partnership: £12,000+

**Do not treat low-ticket clients as less valuable.** They are potential high-ticket clients who haven't yet experienced your value.
