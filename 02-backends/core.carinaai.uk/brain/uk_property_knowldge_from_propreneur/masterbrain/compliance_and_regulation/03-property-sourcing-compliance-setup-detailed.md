## LTD Company Formation

Use Companies Made Simple (companiesmadesimple.com). Recommended package: £39.99 + VAT (includes registered office address at 22 Wenlock Road, London N1 7GU -- keeps personal address off public register).

**Step-by-step:**
1. Choose company name -- AVOID "property sourcing" in the name. Estate agents are hostile to property sourcers. Use abbreviated names like "APS Limited" instead of "Example Property Sourcing."
2. Check name availability on Companies House.
3. Select £39.99 option (includes registered office, confirmation statement filing at year 1). This is the best value.
4. Add fraud protection (£4.99) and review service (£5.99).
5. Skip VAT registration (£64.99) -- do it yourself on HMRC for free.
6. Use their registered office address (included in package). Keeps personal home address off public record.
7. SIC code: 68310 (Real Estate Agency). Can add up to 4 SIC codes.
8. Identity check required via text (upload ID, proof of address, selfie). Takes 15 min to 3 hours.
9. Shares: 1,000 ordinary shares at £1 par value.
10. Name yourself as shareholder + PSC (Person of Significant Control).
11. Skip bank account affiliate links -- use a high street bank instead.
12. Company appears on Companies House within ~24 hours.

## Bank Accounts

Three accounts recommended:

1. **Day-to-day business account** -- High street bank (NatWest, Barclays, HSBC). Use only for business transactions. Never mix personal expenses.
2. **Client holding account** -- For holding investor funds before they're paid to third parties. Legally required if handling client money. Money is not yours -- don't spend it. Transfer to day-to-day account after cooling-off period expires.
3. **Charge card (optional)** -- Pleo (~£20/month). Great for tracking expenses. Keeps out-and-about costs separate for accountant.

**Invoice payment flow:** Investor pays finder's fee -> goes into client account during cooling-off period -> after refund period expires -> transfer to day-to-day account.

## Insurances

Use Duncan Clark Insurance Brokers (mention "the course instructor" for the bespoke property sourcing policy, underwritten by AXA).

Required policies:
1. **Professional Indemnity** -- Minimum £100,000 cover. Increase for higher-value properties (e.g. £1.5M cover for properties up to £1M).
2. **Public Liability** -- Covers you when showing investors around properties. If an investor falls and claims against you.
3. **Employers' Liability** -- Only if you employ staff or contractors.
4. **Business car insurance** -- Business use added to personal car insurance.

Annual cost: approximately £450/year (can be paid monthly).

## Data Protection (ICO Registration)

Website: ico.org.uk

Steps:
1. Register for a new data protection licence.
2. Select "Limited Company" as organisation type.
3. Wait until company appears on Companies House (need company number).
4. Answer questions about your company.
5. Select "Estate Agency" as business type.
6. Cost: £40 annual fee.
7. Store ICO registration number -- display on website.

## Property Redress Scheme

Must join one of two schemes:
- **The Property Ombudsman** (tpos.co.uk)
- **Property Redress Scheme** (theprs.co.uk)

Joining fee: approximately £200. Provides mediation if disputes arise. Investors recognise and expect this.

## Anti-Money Laundering Registration

Through HMRC business tax account. Need: Government Gateway number, company number, UTR (Unique Tax Reference Number, received by post after company formation).

**Process:**
1. Log in to HMRC business tax account.
2. Select "anti-money laundering supervision."
3. Complete application.
4. Make payment -- receive payment reference number (save this -- proves you've applied during the 6-12 week approval window).
5. You CAN start trading during the approval window using the payment reference as proof.

## Ongoing AML Training

Cheap online courses with certificates:
- **High Speed Training** -- £25, takes 2-3 hours, certificate next working day.
- **Anti-Money Laundering Learn On Demand** -- £12 with certificate.

Do once a year. Keep certificates on file. Display on website. HMRC may ask for proof.

## KYC -- Manual Verification

Two methods: manual (slow) or online (recommended).

Manual requires:
1. **Proof of Identity** -- Passport or in-date driving licence. Certified by solicitor, postmaster, or estate agent.
2. **Proof of Address** -- Letter dated within 3 months (bank statement, council tax bill, utility bill). Cannot reuse the same document as ID. Driving licence address is usually not valid.
3. **Proof of Funding** -- See separate section.

## KYC -- Online Verification (Credas)

Website: credas.co.uk

**Process:**
1. Register for account, buy credits (300 credits for £567, or smaller batches via phone).
2. Create "new invite" -> "remote" (investor is remote).
3. Always select **Enhanced AML checks** (not standard or ID-only).
4. Enhanced checks include: identity verification, name/address/DOB against credit header, mortality register (checks if person is alive), international sanctions, PEP (Politically Exposed Persons), bank account verification.
5. Optional: credit status check (bankruptcy, insolvency, CCJs, directorships). Must inform investor. Leaves footprint on credit report.
6. Optional: ongoing PEP monitoring (1 credit/week).
7. Invite by SMS. Investor receives text with code.
8. Investor opens link, enters code, confirms details, takes selfie, uploads ID document (passport recommended -- reads RFID chip).
9. Investor provides bank account details for verification.
10. Credas runs checks, returns pass/refer/fail.
11. If issues (e.g. bank account not in investor's name), contact investor for clarification.
12. Document in AML record log. Investor now cleared.

Cost: approximately £1 per check per person, ~£5 average cost per set of checks.

## Invoicing

Professional invoicing system required. Recommended: Xero (~£24.99/month). Can link Stripe for card payments. Alternatives: QuickBooks, Sage, Invoice2go.

Must track: invoices sent, paid, awaiting payment. Required for AML compliance and year-end accounting.

## Proof of Funding Verification

Required before every deal purchase. Must verify both proof AND source of funding.

**Cash buyers:**
- Request bank statements (2-6 months depending on situation).
- Look for consistency. Check the story matches the statements.
- If spikes/inconsistencies appear (e.g. £2,000 balance for 2 months then suddenly £150,000), ask: "Where has this money come from?"
- Document the question and verification.
- Source types: savings, inheritance (solicitor letter), re-mortgage, business income, loans between companies (loan agreement required).

**Mortgage/finance buyers:**
- Need TWO parts of proof:
  1. Proof of lending (mortgage offer, Decision in Principle, solicitor letter confirming funds from sale/re-mortgage).
  2. Proof of cash element (deposit) -- same process as cash buyers above.
