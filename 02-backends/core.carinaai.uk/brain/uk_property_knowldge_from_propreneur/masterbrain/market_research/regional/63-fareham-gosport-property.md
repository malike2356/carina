# Fareham and Gosport: A Tale of Two Property Markets on the Solent

Fareham and Gosport sit side by side on the Hampshire coast, separated by a short stretch of water. Despite their proximity, they offer distinctly different property investment profiles. Understanding the difference matters because a strategy that works in one could underperform in the other.

This article compares both markets for investors considering the western Solent area.

## Fareham: the balanced market

Fareham is a well-established market town with a population of roughly 120,000. It has its own commuter rail station on the West Coastway line, a growing town centre, and good road access via the M27.

Average house prices in Fareham sit around GBP 340,000. A three-bedroom semi-detached home typically costs GBP 320,000 to GBP 380,000. Two-bedroom flats and terraced houses range from GBP 200,000 to GBP 260,000.

Gross rental yields typically fall between 4% and 5.5% for standard buy-to-let properties. This is not a high-yield market, but tenant demand is consistent and tenant quality is good. The tenant base includes commuters who work in Southampton or Portsmouth, professionals at Fareham's business parks, and families who want access to good local schools.

Fareham's rental market is dominated by two and three-bedroom houses. There is demand for one-bedroom flats from young professionals, but the market is smaller. HMOs are less common and yields are generally lower than in Portsmouth due to higher purchase prices.

The key advantage of Fareham is stability. Voids are low, tenant stays are long, and the local economy is diverse enough to weather regional downturns. For investors who prioritise low-risk income over maximum yield, Fareham is a solid choice.

## Gosport: the value opportunity

Gosport sits on a peninsula south of Fareham, accessible by road via the A32 or by the Gosport Ferry from Portsmouth Harbour. It has a very different feel and a very different property market.

Average house prices in Gosport are significantly lower than Fareham, sitting at around GBP 260,000. Two-bedroom terraces can be found for GBP 180,000 to GBP 220,000, and small flats for under GBP 150,000. This makes Gosport one of the most affordable markets in South East Hampshire.

Gross yields are correspondingly higher, typically 5.5% to 7.5% for standard lets. Converted HMOs in the right locations can achieve yields above 10%.

The tenant base in Gosport is more diverse than many investors assume. There is strong demand from naval personnel based at HMS Sultan and other local establishments, families priced out of Fareham and Portsmouth, and a growing number of young professionals attracted by the lower cost of living. The Stokes Bay and Alverstoke areas also attract premium tenants.

The main challenge in Gosport is its transport connectivity. There is no direct rail link into Gosport town centre. Residents must either drive to Fareham station, use the ferry to Portsmouth Harbour for rail connections, or rely on bus services. This limits the commuter market compared to Fareham.

| Average house price (3-bed) | GBP 340,000 | GBP 260,000 |

| Tenant quality | High | Good to high |
| Capital growth potential | Steady | Moderate |
| Risk profile | Lower yield, lower risk | Higher yield, moderate risk |
| Rail connectivity | Good | Limited |

## Which market suits your strategy

Choose Fareham if you want lower-risk, long-term lets with professional tenants and stable capital growth. The lower yields are balanced by strong tenant retention and consistent demand.

Choose Gosport if you are yield-focused and willing to accept slower capital growth in return for better cash flow. The lower entry prices also make Gosport more accessible for newer investors with smaller budgets.

## The combined strategy

Some experienced investors work both markets together. They buy in Fareham for portfolio stability and capital growth, and in Gosport for cash flow. The proximity of the two towns makes management efficient, and the different risk profiles create natural portfolio diversification.

Whether you focus on one or combine both, Fareham and Gosport each offer genuine opportunities for the right investor.
