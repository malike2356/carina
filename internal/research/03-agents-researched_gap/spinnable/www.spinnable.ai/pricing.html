What is an AI Worker?

Think of each worker as a colleague — not a chatbot

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about hiring AI workers

What is Spinnable?

Spinnable lets you hire AI workers that join your team as autonomous colleagues. They communicate via WhatsApp, Email, and Slack, use your existing tools, and handle real work without constant supervision.

How autonomous are the workers?

Very. Once you describe a role and share context, your worker takes initiative — scheduling tasks, sending follow-ups, flagging issues, and completing workflows without you having to prompt them every time.

What counts as one worker?

Each worker is a dedicated AI colleague with its own role, context, and memory. Think of it like hiring one person for one job — but it works 24/7 and costs a fraction of a human hire.

What tools do workers connect to?

Google Workspace, Outlook, Notion, Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, and many more. Your workers take action directly in these tools — they don't just answer questions.

Do I need technical expertise?

Not at all. Hiring a worker takes 60 seconds. You describe the role in plain language, and the worker is ready. No coding, no complex setup, no IT team required.

What happens when I reach my plan limit?

You'll get a notification when approaching your limit. You can upgrade your plan anytime to add more workers or increase task capacity. Your existing workers keep running without interruption.

What's the difference between plans?

Plans differ by number of workers, task volume, and access to advanced features like custom integrations and priority support. All plans include the full autonomous capabilities — no features are locked behind enterprise tiers.
