# Complaints Procedure and Complaint Log - Templates

## In-House Complaints Procedure

We are committed to providing a high-quality, professional service to all clients, customers, and individuals we work with. When an issue arises, we need to know about it so we can improve our service.

### How to Make a Complaint

If you have a complaint, please put it in writing with as much detail as possible and send it to us at [company email address]. This helps us determine the best course of action for all parties.

Once a complaint has been received, we have **8 weeks** to consider the issue. If we have not resolved it within this timeframe, you may refer your complaint to [redress scheme membership name - e.g. The Property Ombudsman], an independent government-approved redress scheme.

---

### Complaints Timeline

**Stage 1 - Acknowledgement (within 3 working days)**
- We will send you a letter or email (your preference) acknowledging receipt of the complaint
- A copy of this complaints procedure will be included

**Stage 2 - Investigation and Decision (within 15 working days of acknowledgement)**
- We investigate the complaint and determine an outcome
- A formal decision and outcome is put in writing and sent to you

**Stage 3 - Review of Decision (if unsatisfied)**
- If you remain dissatisfied, contact us again specifying:
  - How you would like the complaint handled
  - How you feel the decision was unacceptable
- We will review your proposal and decide the best course of action
- We will write to you within **12 working days** of receiving your proposal to confirm our final viewpoint

**Stage 4 - Independent Review (if still dissatisfied)**
- Contact [redress scheme name] to request an independent review
- You must submit your complaint to [redress scheme name] within **6 months** of receiving our final viewpoint
- Include all evidence to support your case
- Note: [redress scheme name] requires all complaints to be addressed through our in-house procedure first

### Redress Scheme Membership

Property sourcing businesses regulated as estate agents should be members of a government-approved redress scheme. Common schemes include:

- **The Property Ombudsman (TPO)** - most widely used for property professionals
- **Property Redress Scheme (PRS)** - alternative approved scheme

Membership is mandatory for estate agents under the Estate Agents Act 1979. Include your redress scheme membership number on all marketing materials and the reservation agreement.

### Complaint Causes - Common Categories to Track

Track the nature of complaints to identify systemic issues:

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Communication | Poor or insufficient updates during a transaction |
| Due diligence | Complaint that deal information was inaccurate |
| Refund dispute | Disagreement over reservation fee refund eligibility |
| Delays | Transaction took longer than expected |
| Misrepresentation | Claim that property was misrepresented |
| AML documentation | Complaint about ID/document requests |
| Service quality | General dissatisfaction with service level |

Monthly review of complaint categories should inform training and process improvements.
