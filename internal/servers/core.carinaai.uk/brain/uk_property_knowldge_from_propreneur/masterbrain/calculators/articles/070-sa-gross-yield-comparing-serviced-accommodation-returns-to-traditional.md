# SA Gross Yield: Comparing Serviced Accommodation Returns to Traditional BTL

Published: Wed, 13 Jan 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: sa-gross-yield-comparing-serviced-accommodation-returns-to-traditional

---

SA gross yield looks spectacular compared to BTL. But like HMO, the gap between gross and net is wider because the costs are higher.\n\nUsing our Portsmouth SA with £28,105 annual revenue on a £250,000 property, the gross yield is 11.2%. The same property as a BTL would have a gross yield of 5.3%. The SA gross yield is more than double.\n\nBut after costs, the SA NOI yield is £9,168 divided by £250,000, which equals 3.7%. The BTL net yield was 3.4%. The premium narrows from 5.9% at the gross level to just 0.3% at the net level.\n\nThis is the reality of SA. The headline numbers look incredible. The actual returns are often only marginally better than BTL, and sometimes worse when you account for the extra management time and higher risk.\n\nSA can work well if you manage efficiently, achieve high occupancy, and control costs. But do not be seduced by the gross yield. Calculate your expected net yield based on realistic revenue and cost assumptions.

**The formula**

```
Gross Yield (%) = (Annual Revenue / Purchase Price) × 100
```

**Why this matters**

SA gross yield can exceed 10%, making BTL look uncompetitive. But the net yield after costs is often only 1% to 2% higher than BTL. The premium is real but smaller than the headline suggests.
