# Development Sell Exit: Net Profit on the Completed Sale

Published: Mon, 24 May 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: development-sell-exit-net-profit-on-the-completed-sale

---

The sell exit is the simplest way to realise your development profit. You complete the project, sell the units, and collect your profit. The net profit is the GDV minus all costs including sales fees.\n\nSales fees include estate agent commission at 1.5% to 2.5% plus VAT, legal fees on each sale at £800 to £1,500 per unit, and marketing costs. For a two-unit development selling at £280,000 each, the sales costs might be: agent at 2% = £11,200, legal at £1,200 per sale = £2,400, marketing = £3,000. Total: £16,600.\n\nYour net profit from the sell exit is GDV (£560,000) minus total project cost (£480,802) minus sales costs (£16,600), which equals £62,598. That is your actual cash profit.\n\nThe sell exit gives you a cash lump sum but it is taxable as trading profit at your marginal income tax rate. A £62,598 profit could incur £25,000 to £30,000 in tax, depending on your other income.\n\nThe advantage of selling is clean exit. No ongoing management. No tenant risk. The disadvantage is tax and transaction costs. Compare the net proceeds to a hold exit before you decide.

**The formula**

```
Sales Agent Fee = GDV × 1.5% (typical)
Sell Profit = GDV - Total Project Cost - Sales Agent Fee
```

**Why this matters**

Development sell exit gives you a cash lump sum but incurs sales costs and income tax. Net profit after all costs should be at least 15% of GDV. Compare to a hold exit before you decide.
