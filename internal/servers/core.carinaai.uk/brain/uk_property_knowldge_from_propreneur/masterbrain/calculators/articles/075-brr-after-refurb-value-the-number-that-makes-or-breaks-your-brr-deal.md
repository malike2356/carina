# BRR After-Refurb Value: The Number That Makes or Breaks Your BRR Deal

Published: Mon, 01 Feb 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: brr-after-refurb-value-the-number-that-makes-or-breaks-your-brr-deal

---

The after-refurb value is the most important and most dangerous number in BRR. It determines how much you can refinance. If you get it wrong, the deal does not work.\n\nARV is what the property will be worth after your refurbishment is complete. It is not what you hope it will be worth. It is what a surveyor will value it at for the refinance lender. And surveyors are conservative.\n\nFor a £150,000 purchase with £30,000 of refurb costs, you might expect the ARV to be £220,000. But if the surveyor values it at £205,000, your refinance proceeds drop significantly.\n\nThe difference between £220,000 and £205,000 is £15,000 in ARV. At a 75% LTV refinance, that means £11,250 less cash released. That could be the difference between getting all your cash out and leaving £11,000 stuck in the deal.\n\nThe best BRR investors are conservative with their ARV estimates. They use recent comparable sales, not optimistic projections. They add a 5% to 10% buffer to account for surveyor conservatism. They build a track record with lenders so the surveyor trusts their work.

**Why this matters**

ARV is the most important number in BRR. Be conservative. Use recent comparable sales for similar refurbished properties. Add a 5% to 10% buffer. If the number still works, the deal is viable.
