# R2R Profit per Door: The Per-Room Metric That Keeps Your Business Honest

Published: Mon, 21 Dec 2026 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: r2r-profit-per-door-the-per-room-metric-that-keeps-your-business-hones

---

Like HMO profit per door, R2R profit per door tells you how much each room contributes to your bottom line. But the R2R version is more important because the costs are more finely balanced.

In our four-bed example, the annual profit is £3,825. Divided by four rooms, that is £956 per door per year, or roughly £80 per door per month. That is £80 of profit for every room, every month.

If one room is consistently earning £50 less than the others, investigate why. Is the room smaller? Is it in worse condition? Is the rent too low? Every room should earn its keep.

In Portsmouth, typical R2R profit per door is £60 to £120 per month. Below £60, the numbers are too tight. Above £120, you have a strong deal.

The power of profit per door is that it scales. If you have one property with four rooms at £80 per door, that is £3,840 a year. If you scale to ten properties, that is £38,400 a year. The metric helps you evaluate whether adding more properties is worth the operational complexity.

**The formula**

```
Profit per Door = Annual Profit / Number of Sub-Tenants
```

**Why this matters**

R2R profit per door should be £60 to £120 per month. Below £60, the deal is marginal. Scale it across multiple properties and the numbers become a serious income.
