# BMV Breakeven Discount: The Minimum Discount to Walk Away with Zero Cash In

If you want to achieve no-money-down, you need to know the minimum discount required. That is the breakeven discount. It is the discount percentage that makes the remortgage proceeds equal your total cost.\n\nFor a property with a market value of £200,000 and a 75% LTV remortgage, your maximum remortgage is £150,000. If your total acquisition cost (purchase plus fees) is roughly purchase price plus 3%, you need to buy such that purchase price plus 3% equals £150,000. That means a purchase price of roughly £145,600, which is a 27% discount.\n\nIf the remortgage LTV is 80%, your maximum remortgage is £160,000. You can buy at £155,300 and achieve cash out. That is a 22% discount. The higher the LTV, the smaller the discount needed.\n
Every deal has a breakeven discount. If the actual discount is above the breakeven, you get cash out. If it is below, you leave cash in. Run this calculation before you buy to know what you are getting into.\n
In practice, most BMV deals on the South Coast offer discounts of 10% to 20%, which is below the breakeven for 75% LTV. That means you will leave some cash in. That is fine. It is still a good deal. But you need to know the number.

**The formula**

```
Breakeven Discount (%) = (Purchase Price + Bridging Fee + Legal + Valuation + SDLT - Remortgage Proceeds) / Market Value × 100
```

**Why this matters**

Breakeven discount for no-money-down at 75% LTV is roughly 25% to 30%. Most real-world BMV deals are below this. That is fine. Know your breakeven and know how much cash you will leave in.
