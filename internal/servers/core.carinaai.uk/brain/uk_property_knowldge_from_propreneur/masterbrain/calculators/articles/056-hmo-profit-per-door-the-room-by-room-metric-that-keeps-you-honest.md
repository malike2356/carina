# HMO Profit per Door: The Room-by-Room Metric That Keeps You Honest

Published: Wed, 25 Nov 2026 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: hmo-profit-per-door-the-room-by-room-metric-that-keeps-you-honest

---

HMO investors talk about profit per door. It measures how much each room contributes to your bottom line. If a room is not earning enough, it might not be worth renting.

For a five-bed HMO with an annual net profit of £4,371, your profit per door is £4,371 divided by 5, which equals £874 per room per year, or £73 per room per month.

That means each room is contributing roughly £73 a month to your bottom line after all costs. If you have a double room that is only achieving £50 a month in profit because it costs more to heat or has higher wear and tear, that room is underperforming.

Profit per door helps you make decisions. If a room consistently underperforms, consider converting it to a larger or ensuite room that can command higher rent. Or reduce the number of rooms and convert space to shared living areas.

In Portsmouth, typical HMO profit per door ranges from £60 to £150 per month, depending on location, room size, and how well the HMO is managed. Anything below £50 per door suggests the HMO is not running efficiently.

Track profit per door monthly. If it declines for three consecutive months, investigate why. It is the canary in the coal mine for HMO performance.

**The formula**

```
Annual Profit per Door = Annual Net Profit / Number of Rooms
```

**Why this matters**

HMO profit per door should be at least £60 to £150 per room per month. Below £50 per door, your HMO is underperforming. Track it monthly and investigate declines promptly.
