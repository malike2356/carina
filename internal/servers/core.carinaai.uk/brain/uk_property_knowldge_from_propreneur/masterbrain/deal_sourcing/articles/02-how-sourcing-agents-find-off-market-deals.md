# How Sourcing Agents Find Off-Market Deals

Off-market properties are the holy grail of property investment. They do not appear on Rightmove, Zoopla, or any other major portal. Yet some investors seem to find them consistently while others rely entirely on the open market. The difference is usually a sourcing agent.

Understanding how professional sourcing agents find off-market deals reveals why they are worth their fee and how you can benefit from their networks.

## What makes a deal truly off-market

An off-market property is one that is not publicly listed for sale. The seller wants to sell but has not instructed an estate agent to market the property. This could be for any number of reasons - they want privacy, they are testing interest before committing to a full marketing campaign, or they need to sell quickly and cannot wait for the traditional sales process.

The advantage for buyers is significant. Off-market deals typically come with less competition, more flexible terms, and a price that reflects the seller's motivation rather than the estate agent's valuation.

## Direct to vendor marketing

The most reliable way sourcing agents find off-market deals is by contacting property owners directly. This is not cold calling in the traditional sense. Professional sourcers use data to identify likely motivated sellers before they ever pick up the phone or send a letter.

Targets for direct to vendor marketing include:

- Properties bought within the last three to five years where the owner may be looking to exit
- Landlords with multiple properties in a single postcode who may be rationalising their portfolio
- Probate properties where the executor needs to sell the estate
- Owners who have recently moved out, leaving the property vacant
- Properties with expired planning permissions or lapsed building control notices

A targeted letter campaign to these owners can yield response rates of 2 to 5 per cent - dramatically higher than mass mailings to every landlord in a postcode.

## Agent relationships

Sourcing agents build deep relationships with local estate agents. This is not about asking agents to break their instructions. It is about being the first person an agent calls when they know a seller will accept a quick, guaranteed sale below market value.

Estate agents prefer transactions that complete. A sourcing agent who brings cash buyers and completes consistently becomes a trusted partner. When a seller tells the agent "I need to sell in four weeks," the agent picks up the phone to their sourcing contacts before listing the property publicly.

## Probate and inheritance leads

Probate is one of the most consistent sources of off-market property. When someone dies, their estate must be sold, and the executors are often motivated to complete quickly rather than hold the property through a lengthy marketing period.

Experienced sourcing agents build relationships with solicitors, will writers, and probate researchers to identify properties before they are instructed for sale. These relationships take time to develop but produce a steady pipeline of motivated sellers.

## Auction connections

Many properties that end up at auction were offered to sourcing agents first. Auction houses earn their fees only when the gavel falls. If a seller wants to avoid auction fees and uncertainty, the auction house may introduce them to a sourcing agent who can arrange a private sale before the auction date.

This pre-auction market is where some of the best deals are found. The seller avoids the risk of the property not selling, the sourcing agent gets a motivated vendor, and the investor buys below what the auction reserve would have been.

## Why off-market matters for Portsmouth and the South Coast

In markets like Portsmouth, Southampton, and the Isle of Wight, off-market sourcing is particularly valuable. These are relatively tight markets where good stock is snapped up quickly when it hits the portals.

A property in Southsea priced at GBP 220,000 on Rightmove will have ten viewings in the first week. The same property sourced off-market at GBP 185,000 gives the buyer breathing room to complete due diligence, arrange finance, and negotiate without competing against a queue of other viewers.
