# Property Sourcing: Social Media Branding & Presence

**Source:** Professional Mentorship -- Social Media Branding Training
**Purpose:** How to build a consistent, professional social media presence across all platforms for a property sourcing business
**Audience:** Carina can guide sourcers on branding, platform strategy, and content types

---

## Why You Need Social Media

The first thing people do when considering doing business with you is check your social media. If you're not there, or your presence is inconsistent/amateur, they won't trust you.

People use phones and tablets now, not desktops. Social media is where they spend their time. If you're not on it, you're invisible.

## Brand Consistency Across All Platforms

Your branding must be congruent across every platform -- Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube.

**Elements that must be consistent:**
- Logo (same design, same colours).
- Banner/image (professional, not silly).
- Profile photo (professional headshot).
- Colours and design style.
- Post structure and layout.
- Tone of voice.

When a prospect sees the same branding across multiple platforms, they see a legitimate, serious business.

## Profile Setup

**Profile photo:** A professional headshot. Not a logo, not a cartoon, not a holiday photo -- a clear, professional photo of you.

**Banner:** Your business branding with a clear message about what you do.

**Bio:** Brief description of who you are and what you do. Example: "Helping busy professionals find the best property deals."

## Content Strategy

**What to post:**
- Success stories of students/clients (not just your own wins).
- Reviews and testimonials.
- Behind-the-scenes of events and one-to-one sessions.
- Educational content (e.g. "how to check a boiler when viewing a property," "auction tips," "how to do a productive viewing").
- Property tours of completed projects.
- Motivational content.
- Day-in-the-life content (builds authenticity).

**What NOT to post:**
- Fake "rich lifestyle" content (standing in front of Lamborghinis, etc.) -- people see through it.
- Inconsistent or off-brand content.
- Overly personal content (your choice, but keep it mostly business).

## Which Platform Works Best

Currently, Instagram is the most interactive and generates the most business. But this can change. The key is consistency across ALL platforms.

- **Instagram:** Highest engagement, best for Reels, most business leads currently.
- **Facebook:** Good for community building, events, and longer posts.
- **TikTok:** Growing, good for short-form video.
- **LinkedIn:** Professional network, good for B2B and investor connections.

## Key Rules

- Be real, be honest, be upfront. People buy from real people.
- Don't post fake bravado. Authenticity wins.
- Keep it mostly business-oriented, with occasional personal content if it fits your brand.
- Consistency across platforms builds credibility.
- You don't have to be on every platform, but the ones you ARE on must look like they belong to the same business.
