# Client Due Diligence Procedure

**Xelox Properties Ltd**
**Version 1.0 - May 2026**

---

## 1. Purpose

This document sets out the procedure Xelox Properties Ltd follows to verify the identity of every client before entering into a business relationship, as required under the Money Laundering Regulations 2017.

Client due diligence (CDD) applies to:

- All investors purchasing or funding a property sourced by Xelox
- All landlords entering a rent to rent or guaranteed rent agreement with Xelox
- Any individual or entity receiving a payment from Xelox in connection with a property transaction

## 2. When to Verify

CDD must be completed before any of the following:

- Presenting a deal to an investor
- Signing a head lease or rent to rent agreement with a landlord
- Exchanging contracts on a sourced property
- Receiving funds from a client

Exceptional cases where verification may be completed after the transaction begins must be approved in writing by a director and documented fully.

## 3. What to Collect

For every client, collect the following before proceeding:

### 3.1 Proof of Identity

One of the following original documents, verified in person or via approved electronic verification:

- Valid passport (any nationality)
- Valid UK driving licence (full or provisional)
- UK or EEA national identity card

For corporate clients, also collect:

- Certificate of incorporation
- Register of directors and beneficial owners
- Proof of registered address

### 3.2 Proof of Address

One of the following, dated within the last three months:

- Utility bill (gas, electricity, water, or broadband)
- Bank or building society statement
- Council tax bill
- HMRC tax document
- Recent mortgage statement

Documents must show the client's full name and current residential address.

### 3.3 Source of Funds Declaration

For any transaction, the client must provide a written declaration of the source of funds, supported by evidence such as:

- Bank statements showing accumulated savings
- Completion statement from a recent property sale
- Share dividend certificates
- Inheritance documentation
- Business profit and loss evidence
- Loan agreement (if funds are borrowed from a third party)

For transactions over GBP 10,000, source of funds evidence must be obtained regardless of how well you know the client.

## 4. Verification Process

**Step 1:** The client provides copies of the required documents by email or secure upload.

**Step 2:** A director or authorised staff member reviews the documents against the client in person or via a live video call.

**Step 3:** The reviewer confirms:
- The document matches the person
- The photograph resembles the individual (where applicable)
- The document has not expired
- The names match across all documents provided
- The address is current

**Step 4:** For corporate clients, verify the company exists on Companies House register and confirm the identity of directors and beneficial owners with significant control (25% or more shares or voting rights).

**Step 5:** All checks are recorded on the CDD checklist and filed securely.

## 5. Simplified Due Diligence

Simplified CDD may apply only where the client is:

- A UK or EEA public authority
- A company listed on a regulated UK or EEA market
- An overseas equivalent of the above, subject to equivalent AML standards

Simplified CDD does not apply because you trust the client or have worked with them before. New verification is required each time.

## 6. Enhanced Due Diligence

Enhanced CDD is required where:

- The client is resident outside the UK or EEA
- The transaction involves a politically exposed person (PEP) or their family
- The client's circumstances appear unusual or complex
- The source of funds is unclear or inconsistent with the client's profile

Enhanced CDD means:
- Additional documentation to verify source of wealth and funds
- Senior management approval before proceeding
- More frequent ongoing monitoring

## 7. Ongoing Monitoring

CDD is not a one-off exercise. Xelox will:

- Review client records annually
- Update verification documents every three years (or earlier if documents expire)
- Flag any unusual transaction patterns or changes in client circumstances
- Record all monitoring activity

## 8. Record Keeping

All CDD records must be kept for:

- Five years after the business relationship ends
- Five years after the last transaction is completed

Records are stored securely, accessible only to authorised staff, and protected in line with data protection requirements.

## 9. Non-Compliance

If a client refuses to provide CDD documents, Xelox will:

- Not proceed with the transaction
- Consider whether a suspicious activity report (SAR) should be filed with the NCA
- Document the refusal and the reasons

## 10. Review

This procedure will be reviewed annually or whenever regulations change, whichever is sooner.
