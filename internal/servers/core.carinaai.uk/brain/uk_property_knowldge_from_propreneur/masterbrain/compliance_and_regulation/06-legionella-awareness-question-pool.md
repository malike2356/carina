### Section 1: Single Response Questions

**Q1. Who is typically assigned the role of the responsible person for Legionella control?**
A) Any employee with general maintenance knowledge
B) A public health inspector
C) A water treatment technician from a third-party company
D) A person with sufficient authority, competence, and knowledge of the systems
> **Answer: D**

**Q2. When was the Legionella bacteria first identified?**
A) 2006
B) 1977
C) 1965
D) 1954
> **Answer: B**

**Q3. Which of the following is not one of the three highest-risk systems for Legionella exposure?**
A) Spas, Pools, and Hot Tubs
B) Hot and Cold Water Distribution Systems
C) Residential Washing Machines
D) Evaporative Cooling Towers
> **Answer: C**

**Q4. Which of the following statements best describes Legionella?**
A) Legionella are fungi found primarily in wet soil
B) Legionella are bloodborne pathogens that can cause blood diseases
C) Legionella are bacterial microorganisms found in natural and artificial water systems
D) Legionella are viral microorganisms that cause common colds
> **Answer: C**

**Q5. What is the primary route of transmission for Legionellosis?**
A) Person-to-person contact
B) Contaminated food
C) Inhalation of aerosolized water droplets
D) Touching contaminated surfaces
> **Answer: C**

**Q6. What is the optimal temperature range for Legionella bacteria to grow rapidly in water systems?**
A) Between 20°C and 45°C
B) Below 20°C
C) Above 60°C
D) Between 50°C and 60°C
> **Answer: A**

---

### Section 2: Fill In The Blank Questions

**Q7. In the United Kingdom, the legally binding Approved Code of Practice specifically for Legionella control is known as ACoP \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.**
> **Answer: L8**

**Q8. For effective Legionella control in hot water systems, water should be stored at a minimum of \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_°C.**
> **Answer: 60**

**Q9. The bacteria that causes Legionnaires' disease is called Legionella \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.**
> **Answer: pneumophila**

**Q10. Exposure to Legionella-contaminated aerosol is affected by the \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ of Legionella in the air and the duration of exposure.**
> **Answer: concentration**

**Q11. Water in cold water systems should ideally be stored and distributed at a temperature below \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_°C.**
> **Answer: 20**

**Q12. The most commonly used diagnostic test for legionellosis is the Legionella Urinary \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ Test (UAT).**
> **Answer: Antigen**

**Q13. The average fatality rate of Legionnaires' disease is approximately \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_% for healthy people.**
> **Answer: 10**

---

### Section 3: True / False Questions

**Q14. There is no specific federal regulation or code of practice exclusively for Legionella control in the United States.**
> **Answer: True**

**Q15. Legionnaires' disease is the most dangerous form of legionellosis.**
> **Answer: True**

**Q16. Legionella bacteria pose a health risk primarily when contaminated water is dispersed into the air as aerosols and inhaled.**
> **Answer: True**

**Q17. User hygiene has no impact on Legionella contamination in pools and spas.**
> **Answer: False** - Poor user hygiene introduces organic matter that can feed bacterial growth.

**Q18. Chest X-ray is the most accurate method for diagnosing Legionellosis.**
> **Answer: False**

**Q19. Legionella bacteria start to die at temperatures above 45°C.**
> **Answer: True**

---

### Section 4: Multiple Response Questions

**Q20. What are the three main purposes for conducting water treatment in the context of Legionella?**
A) To reduce water temperature
B) To kill bacteria and other microorganisms
C) To reduce the amount of sediments
D) To prevent scale and corrosion
> **Answer: B, C, D**

**Q21. Which of the following are common sources for Legionella-contaminated aerosols?**
A) Bottled water
B) Showers and faucets
C) Spas and hot tubs
D) Cooling towers
> **Answer: B, C, D**

**Q22. Which of these conditions must be present for Legionellosis to occur?**
A) Water temperature at or above 45°C
B) The generation of airborne water droplets or aerosols
C) Exposure to the contaminated airborne water droplets or aerosols
D) The potential for Legionella bacteria to grow in the water
> **Answer: B, C, D** - Temperature must be within the 20-45°C growth range, not at or above 45°C where bacteria die.

### Section 5: Matching Questions

**Q23. Match each Legionella control measure with its corresponding category.**

| Control Measure | Category |
|----------------|----------|
| Flushing the system regularly | **Water Age Optimization** |
| Storing hot water at a minimum of 60°C | **Thermal Control** |
| Killing bacteria with biocides | **Water Treatment** |

**Q24. Match the water treatment method to the problem it addresses.**

| Problem | Treatment Method |
|---------|-----------------|
| Reduce the amount of sediments in water system | **Using point-of-entry filters** |
| Kill bacteria and other organisms | **Biocidal treatment** |
| Prevent scale and corrosion | **Corrosion inhibitors** |
