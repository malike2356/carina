# Property Sourcing: Area Due Diligence Using PropertyData.co.uk

**Source:** Professional Mentorship -- Area Due Diligence Training
**Purpose:** How to research and evaluate property investment areas using PropertyData.co.uk
**Audience:** Carina can guide users through the complete area due diligence workflow

---

## Overview

Area due diligence is the foundation of identifying your "Goldmine Patch" -- the target area where you'll focus your property business. Every area in the UK works for some strategy, but not every area works for YOUR strategy. Due diligence helps you match area characteristics to your chosen strategy (BTL, HMO, BRRR, serviced accommodation, rent-to-rent).

## Using PropertyData.co.uk

PropertyData aggregates data from multiple sources into one dashboard. It's the primary tool for area research.

**Access:**
- You can buy your own account (recommended if running a real business).
- Limited shared access may be available via training program (email the team).

**Basic Workflow:**
1. Go to PropertyData.co.uk -> "Local Data" tab.
2. Enter target postcode or area.
3. Initially you'll see a large radius with many data points. Contract this down.
4. Target: approximately 4,000-5,000 data points for statistically significant but focused data.
5. Adjust the map area by dragging and resizing the boundary.

## Key Metrics to Analyse

### Housing Stock and Prices
- Average asking prices by property type (1-bed up to 5-bed).
- Each dot on the map represents a property on the market -- click for listing details.
- Average sold price per square foot (e.g. £255/sqft).
- Sold prices map -- every dot is a completed sale.

### Rental Market
- Average rent by bedroom count (e.g. 2-bed: £1,089, 3-bed: £1,506, 4-bed: £2,055).
- HMO room rents (e.g. double ensuite average: £636).
- Links through to SpareRoom for live room listings.
- Compare rental demand by checking number of available properties.

### Yields
- Average yield by property type (e.g. studio apartments: 12.5% best yield, average range 6-7%).
- HMO yield data available separately.

### Market Growth
- Historic and current house price per square foot trend.
- Example analysis: Leeds -- March 2018 at £165/sqft, peaked at £227, dropped to £215 in 2024, back up to £231. Upward trend confirms good investment timing.
- Best entry point was at the bottom of the market dip (March 2024 for Leeds).

### Sales Activity (Market Velocity)
- Number of properties selling per month in the area.
- Peak activity vs current activity.
- Low sales volume + sellers still listing = buyer's market. Opportunity to negotiate.
- Example: January had only 23 sales vs peak of 326 in November 2021. Buyers holding back = opportunity for well-positioned buyers.

### Demographics
- Deprivation indices, health, education, employment, travel patterns, age profile, political affiliation.
- Helps build a picture of the tenant demographic in the area.
- Important for matching property type and strategy to tenant demand.

### Planning and Development
- Planning portal shows future development in the area.
- Every dot = a planning application.
- Low-value: extensions, conversions (under £100k).
- High-value: major developments (multi-million pound residential blocks, student accommodation, university buildings).
- Example: Leeds had 368 current applications with £250M+ total investment.
- Critical analysis: if 198 new student rooms are approved, is an HMO in that area a good idea? Supply and demand impact.

## Supplementing Online Data

PropertyData alone is not enough. Supplement with:

1. **Phone local letting and estate agents** -- ask what's happening on the ground.
2. **Local networking events** -- attend and talk to local investors/professionals.
3. **Chamber of Commerce** -- economic development intel.
4. **Council planning meetings** -- attend to understand future plans.
5. **Physical area visit** -- spend a few days in the area. Get a feel for it.
   - Is development happening?
   - Are companies moving premises?
   - Is there dereliction or empty office buildings?
   - Are cranes in the skyline?
   - General energy of the area.

## Example: Leeds City Analysis

- Population in target area: ~124,000, ~48,000 households.
- Sold price per sqft: £255.
- Average 3-bed rent: £1,506.
- Growth trajectory: upward trend, recovering from 2024 dip.
- Sales velocity: low (buyer's opportunity).
- Planning: 368 applications, £250M+ investment. Major student/residential developments.
- Verdict: strong area for property investment, especially for HMOs and student accommodation -- but check if new student housing supply will oversaturate the market.
