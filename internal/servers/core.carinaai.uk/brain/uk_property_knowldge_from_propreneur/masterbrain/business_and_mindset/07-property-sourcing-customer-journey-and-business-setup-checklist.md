# Property Sourcing: Customer Journey and Business Setup Checklist

## The 12-Step Customer Journey

The complete property sourcing deal cycle from finding to closing:

| Step | Description |
|---|---|
| Step 1 | Business Setup - company registered, compliance in place, insurance, CRM, website live |
| Step 2 | Due Diligence - area identified, strategies confirmed, research tools set up |
| Step 3 | Shortlist a Property Deal - find a property that meets minimum deal criteria |
| Step 4 | Secure an Offer - negotiate purchase price, get offer accepted by vendor |
| Step 5 | Promote the Sale - build the deal pack, prepare marketing materials |
| Step 6 | Prepare Sales Offer - complete DD pack including comparables, refurb costs, ROI analysis, photos, video |
| Step 7 | Contact Investors - email VIP list (Monday 2pm), general list (Tuesday 9am) |
| Step 8 | Review Responses - qualify interested investors (CDD check, financial capacity, investment criteria match) |
| Step 9 | Invoice and T&Cs - send reservation agreement and reservation fee invoice (£2,000 standard) |
| Step 10 | Get Paid - collect reservation fee before proceeding |
| Step 11 | Intro to Agent - introduce investor to selling agent / estate agent in writing |
| Step 12 | Hand Holding to Completion - support investor through solicitors, surveys, mortgage, exchange, completion |

**Key principle:** Steps 7-12 all happen AFTER the deal is packaged and investor is engaged. Do not skip the investor relationship-building steps that happen before Step 7 (see marketing/ and investor/ files).

## Business Setup Checklist

Complete these before taking on any investors or presenting any deals:

### Company Infrastructure
- [ ] Limited company registered (Companies House)
- [ ] Bank accounts opened (business current + savings)
- [ ] Business insurance obtained (professional indemnity minimum; public liability recommended)
- [ ] Data Protection (ICO) registration - required for holding personal data
- [ ] Property Redress Scheme membership (TPO or PRS) - legally mandatory for property agents
- [ ] VAT registration (if applicable - threshold currently £85,000 turnover)
- [ ] Anti-Money Laundering (AML) registration with HMRC
- [ ] AML training completed (and documented with training log)

### Digital and CRM Setup
- [ ] Domain name purchased
- [ ] Website built with investor opt-in capability
- [ ] CRM system configured (AllClients, HubSpot, or equivalent)
- [ ] Email marketing platform set up (Mailchimp or equivalent)
- [ ] Social media profiles created and branded consistently
- [ ] Credas (or equivalent) KYC/CDD verification set up for onboarding investors

### Accounting
- [ ] Accounts system set up (Xero recommended)
- [ ] Chart of accounts configured for property sourcing business
- [ ] Invoice templates created

### Legal Documents Ready
- [ ] Standard Reservation Agreement template (see legal/20)
- [ ] Bespoke Sourcing Agreement template (see legal/20)
- [ ] Privacy Policy / GDPR notice on website (see legal/13-14)
- [ ] AML Policy document (see legal/17)
- [ ] Client onboarding forms (see legal/18)

### Networking
- [ ] Local property networking events identified
- [ ] LinkedIn / social profiles professional and active
- [ ] 20-second pitch prepared and practised

## Step-by-Step: Presenting a Deal to Investors

Once a deal is shortlisted and a DD pack is prepared:

1. **Stage the deal information:** Create a professional one-page deal summary (teaser)
2. **VIP list alert:** Email VIP database at 2pm Monday (24 hours before general release)
3. **General list release:** Email full database at 9am Tuesday
4. **Respond to enquiries within 24 hours:** Qualify interest (budget, timescales, strategy match)
5. **CDD verification:** Confirm investor identity before presenting full DD pack (AML obligation)
6. **Send full DD pack** to verified and qualified investors
7. **Sales call:** Walk investor through the deal. Answer questions. Address objections.
8. **Issue reservation agreement and invoice:** £2,000 reservation fee (non-refundable after 14 days)
9. **Collect fee:** Do not proceed until fee is received
10. **Introduce investor to estate agent:** "Meet Your Buyer" email (template in marketing/02)
11. **Monitor progress:** Solicitors instructed within 5 days of deal proceeding
12. **Support to completion:** Follow up regularly; ensure survey, mortgage, and legal work progresses

## Key Contacts and Handoffs at Each Stage

| Stage | Who You Contact | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Finding the deal | Estate agents, vendors, below-market-value databases | Source and negotiate the property |
| Due diligence | Letting agents (3 minimum), HMO managers, council offices | Verify rental demand, licencing, Article 4 |
| After reservation | Selling estate agent (to introduce investor) | Pass buyer details for solicitor instruction |
| During sale | Solicitors (investor's and vendor's) | Monitor legal progress |
| Post-completion | Property management company | Handover for day-to-day management (if applicable) |

## The Upselling Opportunity

After a deal completes, investors who are satisfied are the warmest audience for follow-up:

1. **Repeat deal sourcing:** Many investors buy multiple deals once they trust a sourcer
2. **Bespoke sourcing:** Offer a retained bespoke search service (£1,200 upfront instruction fee)
3. **Mentorship referrals:** Investors often know other investors - ask for introductions
4. **Portfolio review calls:** Schedule regular check-ins - this surfaces new requirements
5. **VIP database upgrade:** Invite satisfied clients to VIP tier (first access to new deals)

## Revenue Model Summary

| Revenue Type | Typical Fee | When Paid |
|---|---|---|
| Reservation fee (standard) | £1,500-£3,500 | On deal agreement (before sale proceeds) |
| Bespoke sourcing instruction fee | £1,000-£2,000 | Upfront (non-refundable) |
| Finders fee (deal completion) | £3,000-£9,000 | On exchange or completion |
| VIP monthly membership | £24.99/month | Monthly recurring |
| Discovery day / education event | £1,000-£10,000 | At event booking |
| Property management referral | Commission from PM | Ongoing arrangement |

**Target:** £10,000 net income per month requires approximately 2-3 deals per month at an average fee of £3,500-£5,000 per deal, plus recurring income from VIP memberships and management referrals.
