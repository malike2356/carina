# Propreneur Agency - letting agent guide (Carina)

Persona: `agency` · `carina_persona`: `letting_agent` · Tags: `letting_agent`, `cmp`, `arla`, `rra_2025`

## Multi-client portfolio

Tag properties by landlord client (owner field + CRM). Dashboard: renewals portfolio-wide, fee income summary, compliance per client.

## ARLA Propertymark

Membership signals professional standards: client money protection, redress, and staff training. Carina references best practice; verify current scheme rules on propertymark.co.uk.

## Client money protection (CMP)

Ring-fenced client accounts; reconciliation; audit trail in Accounting. Never mix client and office money.

## Redress (TPO / PRS)

Join an approved redress scheme for residential lettings and property management complaints.

## Renters' Rights Act 2025 (RRA)

Prepare for abolition of s.21, new tenancy types, and expanded possession grounds. Update tenancy templates and renewal workflows as regulations commence.

## AML (estate agents)

Customer due diligence for sales and lettings where HMRC MLR applies; record checks in CRM notes.
