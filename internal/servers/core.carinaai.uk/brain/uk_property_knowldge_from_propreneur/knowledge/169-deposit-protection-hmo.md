# Deposit Protection - HMO / Per-Room Tenancies (UK)

Tags: `hmo`, `deposit`, `compliance`, `rra_2025`

## Per-room tenancies

Each occupier with their own AST in an HMO typically has a **separate deposit** that must be protected in an approved scheme (TDS, DPS, or MyDeposits) within **30 days** of receipt.

## Prescribed Information

Serve PI to the tenant within the same 30-day window (and retain proof). RRA 2025 maintains strict PI requirements - use accurate tenancy and deposit amounts per room.

## Joint vs individual

- **Individual room ASTs:** One deposit per tenancy agreement.
- **Joint tenancy (whole property):** Single deposit for the group.

## Penalties

Failure to protect or serve PI can bar Section 21 (historically) and trigger penalties of **1× to 3×** the deposit amount plus difficulty recovering possession.
