# Propreneur Hall - student lettings guide (Carina)

Persona: `hall` · `carina_persona`: `student_lettings` · Tags: `student_lettings`, `guarantor_agreements`, `student_loans`, `academic_calendar`

## Academic calendar tenancies

Standard fixed term September-June (or July) aligned to university year. Group lets: joint and several liability common - all tenants on one agreement.

## Guarantor agreements

Required when tenant has no UK income/credit history. Guarantor must be UK resident with assets; deed of guarantee separate from AST.

## Student house HMO licensing

Mandatory HMO licensing may apply (5+ occupants forming 2+ households). Additional/selective licensing in some cities - check council register.

## Vacancies

Term-time voids rare; summer void common - budget void_summer_alert widget and short lets where permitted.

## Right to Rent (international students)

Online share code checks; re-check before visa expiry. Keep copies in compliance dossier.

## Rooms module

Room-level rent and occupancy board; simpler compliance view than full HMO persona (hmo_compliance hidden).
