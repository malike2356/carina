# UK Automotive Industry

## Overview

The UK automotive industry contributes approximately £78 billion annually to the economy and employs approximately 800,000 people across manufacturing, retail, and aftermarket. Approximately 905,000 vehicles were produced in the UK in 2023, with approximately 75% exported (EU is the largest market).

The industry is undergoing the most significant transformation in its history: the transition to electric vehicles (EVs), governed by the ZEV (Zero Emission Vehicle) Mandate from 2024. The UK will ban the sale of new petrol and diesel cars from 2030 (hybrids from 2035).

## ZEV Mandate
- From 2024: car manufacturers must sell a minimum percentage of zero-emission vehicles
  - 2024: 22%
  - 2025: 28%
  - 2026: 33%
  - 2027: 38%
  - 2028: 52%
  - 2029: 66%
  - 2030: 80%
  - 2035: 100%
- Trading scheme: manufacturers can bank credits, borrow from future years (up to 10% for cars, 35% for vans), or buy credits from over-compliant manufacturers
- Non-compliance penalty: £15,000 per missing ZEV credit
- Vans have separate trajectory (10% in 2024, 100% by 2035)

## Key Legislation
- Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994; vehicle tax (VED). EV exemption ending from April 2025.
- Road Vehicles (Approval) Regulations 2020; type approval framework
- Motor Vehicle Block Exemption Regulation (MV-BER, retained EU law); competition rules for dealerships, servicing, parts
- End-of-Life Vehicles Regulations 2003; manufacturer responsibility for vehicle recycling
- Alternative Fuels Infrastructure Regulations; EV charge point requirements
- Automated Vehicles Act 2024; legal framework for self-driving vehicles
- Consumer Rights Act 2015; consumer protection in vehicle sales
- Supply of New Cars Order 2000; right to reject new cars

## Pain Points (Agent Opportunity)
1. **ZEV Mandate compliance**: Sales mix tracking, credit management, reporting to DfT.
2. **Type approval documentation**: UKCA/GB type approval for new vehicles. Complex technical documentation packages.
3. **EV charge point grants**: OZEV (Office for Zero Emission Vehicles) grants for home and workplace charging. Application and compliance documentation.
4. **MOT testing**: Approximately 40 million annual tests. Test quality monitoring, tester CPD, equipment calibration records.
5. **Vehicle recalls**: DVSA safety recalls. VIN identification, owner notification, completion tracking. Criminal offence to sell an unrectified recalled vehicle.
6. **Fleet management**: Company car tax (BiK) optimisation. Service scheduling, MOT management, fuel/charging card reconciliation. Grey fleet (privately-owned vehicles used for business) compliance.
7. **Consumer finance (FCA regulated)**: PCP (Personal Contract Purchase), HP (Hire Purchase), leasing agreements. Pre-contract information, affordability assessments, commission disclosure (FCA ban on discretionary commission models, 2024).
8. **End-of-life vehicle (ELV)**: Certificate of Destruction (CoD). Depollution requirements. Manufacturer take-back obligations.
9. **Warranty claims**: OEM warranty claim processing. Parts return for inspection. Labour time guides. Service history verification.
10. **Dealership compliance**: FCA authorisation for consumer finance. New block exemption rules. Manufacturer franchise standards audits.

## Key Terminology
- **ZEV**: Zero Emission Vehicle
- **ULEV**: Ultra Low Emission Vehicle (CO2 < 75g/km)
- **BiK**: Benefit in Kind. Company car tax.
- **VED**: Vehicle Excise Duty. Road tax.
- **PCP**: Personal Contract Purchase. Most common new car finance product in UK.
- **HP**: Hire Purchase. Fixed-term finance with ownership at end.
- **PCH**: Personal Contract Hire. Leasing without ownership option.
- **WLTP**: Worldwide harmonised Light vehicles Test Procedure. Emissions and fuel consumption testing standard.
- **OZEV**: Office for Zero Emission Vehicles
- **DVSA**: Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency
- **VCA**: Vehicle Certification Agency
- **NEDC/WLTP**: Emissions testing cycles. NEDC (old), WLTP (current).
- **Type approval**: Certification that vehicle meets safety and environmental standards.
- **CoC**: Certificate of Conformity. Individual vehicle type approval document.
- **ELV**: End-of-Life Vehicle
- **Block exemption**: EU/UK competition law exemption for motor vehicle distribution and servicing agreements
