# LABYRINTH SCOUT

## Observe. Verify. Control.

### Mission Control for Autonomous Agents

Autonomous agents can now access tools, execute code, send emails, query databases, interact with customers, and make decisions at machine speed.

The question is no longer whether AI can act.

The question is who remains in control when it does.

Labyrinth Scout is Mission Control for Autonomous Agents: the authority layer between autonomous systems and the real world.

Monitor behaviour in real time.

Verify trust continuously.

Intervene when necessary.

Maintain human authority at every stage.

---

## Sub-position note

"Mission Control for Autonomous Agents" is the primary sub-position. Use it on the landing page hero.

"The Authority Layer for Autonomous Systems" is architecture language. It belongs in technical documentation, not in homepage copy or hero sections.

Both are true. Only one should face customers.

---

## Observe

Visibility before action.

Scout continuously monitors agent activity, tool usage, external communications, runtime behaviour, policy compliance, and emerging threats.

The objective is simple:

Know what your agents are doing.

Know where they are going.

Know when something changes.

Before it becomes an incident.

---

## Verify

Trust should be earned, not assumed.

Every agent, workflow, action, and security event can be verified through continuous assessment, trust scoring, attestations, and audit trails.

Scout creates a living trust passport for every autonomous system under management.

Evidence.

Not promises.

---

## Control

Human authority remains absolute.

When risk exceeds acceptable limits, Scout provides direct intervention capabilities including approval workflows, policy enforcement, containment actions, egress controls, and instant kill-switch activation.

Autonomy operates within boundaries.

Humans define those boundaries.

---

## Core Capabilities

### Kill Switch

Immediately suspend autonomous activity across agents, workflows, and connected systems from a single control point.

### Trust Passport

Continuously updated identity, compliance, verification, and behavioural records for every protected agent.

### Injection Shield

Multi-layer defence against prompt injection, policy bypass, data exfiltration, and unsafe autonomous behaviour.

### Governance Engine

Human-in-the-loop approvals, risk thresholds, policy controls, and operational guardrails.

### Security Telemetry

Real-time visibility into agent actions, system behaviour, threat activity, and operational risk.

Note on Trust Receipts: cryptographic verification of agent behaviour is a real capability. It belongs in the technical documentation and as a feature card, not in the landing page hero sections. Surfacing blockchain-adjacent terminology before a visitor understands the core product is a risk-before-benefit ordering problem. Keep it in the product; remove it from the primary landing page narrative.
