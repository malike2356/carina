# Propreneur Academy - Mentor Guide (Carina)

Tags: `mentoring`, `community_learning`, `academy`

## Programme structure

- **Programme** - umbrella brand and curriculum.
- **Cohort** - time-bound group with start/end dates.
- **Package** - what the mentee buys (sessions, duration, community access).
- **Sessions** - 1:1 or group; notes and actions stored per session.

## Goals & milestones

Mentees set investment goals (income target, timeline, strategy). Milestones break the 90-day plan into measurable steps. Carina can reference active goals in baseline context.

## CPD (RICS / ARLA)

Log mentoring hours and outcomes for continuing professional development. Export session summaries where required by your professional body.

## Community learning

Courses, modules, quizzes, and discussion spaces for cohort members. Certificates issue on completion.

## Payments

Stripe checkout for packages; platform billing handles subscriptions where configured.

## Workspace mode

Persona slug: `mentor` (`Propreneur Academy`). Hides deal sourcing, rooms, HMO compliance, and shared bills.
