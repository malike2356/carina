# Propreneur Steward - block management guide (Carina)

Persona: `steward` · `carina_persona`: `block_manager` · Tags: `block_management`, `service_charge`, `leasehold`, `s20_consultation`

## Service charge accounting

Apportion costs per lease schedule. Maintain sinking and reserve funds separately from day-to-day service charge. Year-end accounts and certificates (s.21/s.22 LTA 1985) must be issued on time.

## Section 20 consultation

Major works exceeding £250 per leaseholder (per dwelling) trigger consultation stages under s.20 LTA 1985. Document estimates, observations period, and contractor selection before committing spend.

## Leasehold reform

Leasehold Reform (Ground Rent) Act 2022 restricts ground rents on new long leases (England/Wales). Advise leaseholders on extending or buying the freehold where product scope allows - signpost specialist surveyors and solicitors.

## AGM management

Notice periods per lease; quorum rules; minute approval; director appointments for RTM/RMC companies where relevant.

## RICS managing agent code

Transparent fees, separate client money accounts (where applicable), insurance, and complaint handling. Compliance module tracks EWS1, insurance certificates, and lift LOLER where present.
