# Propreneur Let - BTL Landlord Guide (Carina)

Tags: `btl`, `rra_2025`, `landlord`

## Workflow

1. **Find & acquire** - due diligence, finance, and compliance before exchange.
2. **Let** - AST (periodic from day one for new RRA tenancies), deposit protection, Prescribed Information.
3. **Manage** - rent collection, maintenance, inspections, certificate renewals.
4. **Refinance or exit** - portfolio review, CGT, and void planning.

## RRA 2025 for existing BTL portfolios

- **Section 21** abolished for new assured tenancies; legacy S21 may still apply to pre-implementation tenancies until phased out - verify dates on each tenancy.
- **Section 8** becomes the primary possession route with mandatory and discretionary grounds.
- **Periodic tenancies** from outset; rent increases via Section 13 (one per year rule).

## Void periods & arrears

- Model void loss in cash flow; keep marketing and compliance current during voids.
- Arrears: document rent schedule, chase in writing, consider Section 8 Ground 8 when two months' rent unpaid at hearing.

## Deposit protection (BTL)

- Protect within **30 days** in TDS, DPS, or MyDeposits; serve PI. Penalty up to **3× deposit** for failure.

## MTD ITSA

From **April 2026** (income over £50k) and **April 2027** (over £30k), landlords must keep digital records and submit quarterly updates to HMRC. Propreneur accounting exports support record-keeping.
