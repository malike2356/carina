# Propreneur Bridge - rent-to-rent guide (Carina)

Persona: `bridge` · `carina_persona`: `r2r_operator` · Tags: `r2r`, `guaranteed_rent`, `management_agreement`, `r2r_legal_structure`

## Legal structure

Operator takes headlease or management agreement from landlord; sublets to occupiers. Must not present as owner without authority. Written agreement essential.

## Guaranteed rent vs management agreement

Guaranteed rent: operator pays fixed rent regardless of voids (margin risk). Management agreement: fee % of rent collected (lower risk, lower upside). Model both in R2R calculator.

## Subletting permission

Headlease must permit subletting or licence to occupy. Mortgage and insurance must allow R2R use class.

## R2R yield

Head rent + costs vs achievable rent roll. Include voids, licensing, and compliance. Dashboard: margin summary, rent vs sublease income, expiry pipeline.

## Management agreement templates

Define repair liability caps, insurance, inventory, break clauses, and rent payment dates. Legal review recommended.

## RRA 2025

Occupier protections flow through to sub-tenancies; review notice periods and deposit rules as Act commences.
