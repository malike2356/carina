# 102 - Property Personal Brand: VBE Framework and Networking

## The VBE Framework (Visible, Valuable, Vocal)

The VBE framework is built on **Courage** - the willingness to put yourself out there.

- **Visible** - Show up consistently. Attend events, be present, be seen. Authority is built on repeated exposure, not a single appearance.
- **Valuable** - Provide genuine value every time you engage. Give insights, share knowledge, help others without expecting immediate returns. This is what makes people remember you.
- **Vocal** - Speak up. Command the rooms you walk into. Share your perspective. Silence builds nothing.

When you combine all three, you build **authority** - the foundation for trust in property.

---

## The Elevator Pitch (Value-First)

The biggest mistake: leading with your name and title.

**Wrong approach:**
> "Hi, my name is Dave, I'm a property sourcer."

**Right approach:**
> "I help busy investors find below-market deals in Warrington. HMOs that cash flow from day one."

**The rule:** Start with the problem you solve, not your name. Lead with the value you create. The name is secondary - the value is what people remember.

## Speaking at Events

- **Start small** - Begin with 3-minute presentations. You don't need to headline a conference to build a speaking reputation.
- **No slides** - Avoid PowerPoint ("death by PowerPoint"). Slides are a crutch and a barrier between you and the audience.
- **Walk the room** - Don't hide behind a podium. Move around. Connect physically with the space.
- **Eye contact** - Lock in with individuals, not the crowd. Make people feel seen.
- **Check the back row** - The people at the back are the most disconnected. Engage them specifically. It draws the whole room in.

---

## Networking Best Practices

- **Collect contacts, not sales** - Networking is relationship building, not a transaction. The goal is to know people, not to close them on the spot.
- **Follow up within 24 hours** - Strike while the iron is hot. A quick follow-up message or call within 24 hours cements the connection.
- **The trust principle:** People do business with people they know, like, and trust. All three must be present.

**Case study:** Stayed until 3-4am at a networking event. Result: 27 leads at approximately £10-12 cost per lead. The returns came from being present when others had left.

## Authority Building

- **Write a book with AI** - Use the tools available to you. Write from your life story and lived experience - AI can help structure and produce it.
- **Publish on Amazon** - Get 20-30 people to buy within the same hour. Amazon's algorithm recognises this velocity and awards a "Best Seller" badge.
- **Social proof** - Collect Trustpilot and Google reviews with real names attached. Verified reviews build far more trust than anonymous ones.

---

## The Digital Foundation

Before anyone does business with you, they **Google you**. What they find must be compelling.

- **Website** - Clear value proposition that communicates who you help and how within seconds.
- **Photo of you in a property context** - A professional image that says "I work in property" without words.
- **Blog posts** - Regular content for SEO ranking. Show up when people search for property advice in your area.

---

## Interviewing Local Business Owners

- **Feature them** - Interview local business owners on your blog or social media channels.
- **Mutual benefit** - They get visibility and exposure. You get warm referral relationships.
- **Keep it short** - A quick interview covering their business, why they chose the area, and their perspective.
- **Reciprocal value** - The people you feature become your advocates. They send referrals your way naturally.

---

## Agent Relationships

- **Primary source of off-market deals** - Local estate agents control deal flow that never hits Rightmove/Zoopla.
- **Be reliable** - Show up when you say you will. Deliver on commitments.
- **Be professional** - Treat agents as partners, not as a means to an end.
- **Pay quickly** - When you do transact, pay agents promptly and without fuss. Speed of payment builds trust and priority status for future deals.
