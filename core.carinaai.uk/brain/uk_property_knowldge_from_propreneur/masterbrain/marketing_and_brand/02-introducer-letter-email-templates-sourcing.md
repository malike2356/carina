# Introducer letters: investor to agent and investor to landlord (templates)

**Updated:** 2026-05-03  
**Kind:** External practice guide (Carina RAG).  
**Source notes:** Letter templates (DOCX). Replace bracketed placeholders; keep a file note of which party consented to introduction.

## Template 1: Introducing investor to estate agent

**Subject:** Introduction - [PROPERTY ADDRESS]

Good morning [AGENT NAME],

Thank you for confirming the successful offer of "[OFFER AMOUNT]" for "[PROPERTY ADDRESS]".

As mentioned during the viewing, either myself or one of the investors I represent will purchase the property if our bid was successful. On this occasion, one of my investors will be proceeding.

"[INVESTOR NAME]" is ready to proceed and has everything in place. Please contact "[INVESTOR NAME]" as soon as practical to agree next steps; they will send required documentation directly to you.

**Investor details**

- Name: [NAME]  
- Address: [ADDRESS]  
- Email: [EMAIL]  
- Phone: [PHONE NUMBER]  

If anything blocks progress, contact me. Thank you again for your help; I hope for a smooth completion.

I look forward to working with you again.

Best regards,

[YOUR NAME]
