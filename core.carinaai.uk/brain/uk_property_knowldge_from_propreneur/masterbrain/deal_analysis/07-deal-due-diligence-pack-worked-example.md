## Section 5: Location Analysis

### Why the Location Suits the HMO Strategy

**General development context:**
- 10 minutes from Manchester Ancoats - direct beneficiary of Manchester's general development
- Situated on Oldham Rd, an emergency route in and out of Manchester
- Manchester house prices have increased approximately 330% over 20 years
- Area is proven for HMO and Serviced Accommodation

**HMO saturation demand for Failsworth (SpareRoom data):**
- Room supply: 8
- Demand for rooms: 829
- Saturation ratio: 165.8 - classified as "In Demand"

**Article 4 situation:**
- No Article 4 direction present in Failsworth at time of analysis
- Industry contacts expect Article 4 to be introduced within 12 months
- If Article 4 is implemented, any existing licenced HMO gains "grandfather rights"
- This provides protection from future planning restrictions and supports long-term capital appreciation

## Section 11: Video and Photo Requirements

A complete DD pack requires:

**Internal:**
- Every room in the house
- Fuse board / consumer unit
- Boiler
- All walls and ceilings
- Any suspicious areas (cracks, damp, staining)

**External:**
- Front, back, and sides of property
- Roof and windows
- Garden and any outbuildings

**Additional:**
- Google Earth view for land and surroundings
- Floor plan of current layout and proposed changes
- Video walkthrough (full property tour, cellar, any outbuildings)
