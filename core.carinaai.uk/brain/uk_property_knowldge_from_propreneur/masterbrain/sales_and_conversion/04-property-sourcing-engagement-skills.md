# Property Sourcing: Engagement & Know-Like-Trust

**Source:** Professional Mentorship -- Client Engagement Training
**Purpose:** How to build regular, meaningful engagement with investors so they know, like, and trust you enough to buy
**Audience:** Carina can guide sourcers on engagement cadence, content strategy, and investor training

---

## The Engagement Problem

Most sourcers make the same mistake: they collect contacts at networking events but never engage until they have something to sell. By then, the prospect barely remembers them. This approach fails because:

- People need an average of **7 interactions** before they trust you enough for a high-value purchase.
- Property sourcing deals are £2,000-£5,000+ per transaction -- trust is everything.
- You cannot constantly bombard prospects with sales offers. Value must come before the sale.

## The Engagement Cadence

**Monday 2:00 PM -- VIP Database**
- Only for paying VIP members (£24.99/month).
- First access to new deals before general release.
- Trains VIPs to expect and act on your emails.

**Tuesday 9:00 AM -- General Database**
- All 10,000+ contacts.
- Deals that weren't reserved by VIPs, or general market updates.
- Even if you have no deals to send, still send an email (market update, area analysis, etc.).

**Sporadic throughout the week -- Value-Add**
- Not a sale, pure value.
- Examples: five top tips for viewing a property, a golden nugget you discovered, breaking news affecting the property market, a social media poll, a YouTube video.

## Train Your Investor

The specific timing (Monday 2PM, Tuesday 9AM) is intentional -- it trains investors to watch, listen, and pay attention when you send an email.

Goal: Your investors are waiting by their computer for your email to land. They must act quickly to reserve deals, so they need to be prepped and trained to expect communication at those exact times.

## Multi-Channel Engagement

Don't rely on email alone. Engage across multiple fronts:
- **Email database** -- structured weekly cadence.
- **Social media** -- polls, value posts, property viewing clips.
- **YouTube** -- educational content.
- Direct outreach when appropriate (but always add value first).

## The Prospect-to-Client Journey

A prospect is NOT a client until they've paid you. Prospects only become clients when:
1. You add enough value.
2. You engage with them regularly.
3. They know you, like you, and trust you enough to pay you.

Every interaction should have an agenda -- know whether this interaction is for value-add or for a sale. Never mix the two in the same interaction.
