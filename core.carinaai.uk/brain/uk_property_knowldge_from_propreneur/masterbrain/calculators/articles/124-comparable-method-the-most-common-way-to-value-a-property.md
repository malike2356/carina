# Comparable Method: The Most Common Way to Value a Property

Published: Wed, 21 Jul 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: comparable-method-the-most-common-way-to-value-a-property

---

The comparable method is how most properties are valued. You find similar properties that have recently sold, adjust for differences, and arrive at a value. It is the method estate agents use, surveyors use, and most buyers use.\n\nFor a three-bed terrace in Portsmouth, you look at recent sales of similar three-bed terraces within a quarter-mile radius. If three comparable properties sold for £250,000, £260,000, and £245,000, the comparable value is roughly £250,000 to £260,000 depending on the specific differences.\n\nAdjustments are made for property size, condition, location, parking, garden, and any other factors that differ between the comparable and the subject property. Each adjustment adds or subtracts from the comparable sale price to arrive at an adjusted value.\n\nThe comparable method works well for standard residential properties in active markets. It works less well for unique properties, commercial buildings, or markets with few recent sales. In those cases, the investment method or residual method may be more appropriate.\n
When valuing a property for purchase, look for at least three recent comparable sales. If you cannot find three, the comparable method is unreliable for that property.

**The formula**

```
Property Value = Adjusted Comparable Sale Price
Adjustment = Comparable Price × Adjustment Factor per Attribute
```

**Why this matters**

The comparable method is the standard for residential valuation. Use at least three recent comparable sales within a quarter-mile radius. Adjust for differences in size, condition, and location.
