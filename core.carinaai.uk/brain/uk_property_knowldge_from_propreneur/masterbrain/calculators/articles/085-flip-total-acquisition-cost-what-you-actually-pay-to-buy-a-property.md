# Flip Total Acquisition Cost: What You Actually Pay to Buy a Property

Every flipper knows the purchase price. The ones who lose money forget everything else they paid to buy the property. Total acquisition cost includes everything you spend before you own the keys.\n\nFor a £150,000 flip property in Portsmouth, your costs might be: stamp duty on second home at £6,250, legal fees at £1,500, survey at £500, mortgage arrangement fee at £1,500, and valuation fee at £350. Total acquisition cost: £160,100. You are £10,100 over the purchase price before you have spent a penny on refurb.\n\nThat £10,100 is invisible profit erosion. Every flip budget should start with the total acquisition cost, not the purchase price. If you think you are buying at £150,000, you are really buying at £160,100.\n\nThe mistake new flippers make is subtracting the purchase price from the sale price and calling it profit. On a £150,000 purchase and £220,000 sale, that gives £70,000. The real profit after all costs is typically £20,000 to £30,000. That difference is where flips turn from winners to losers.\n\nInclude every acquisition cost in your budget. If you cannot afford the full acquisition cost, you cannot afford the deal.

**The formula**

```
Total Cost = Purchase Price + SDLT + Legal Buy + Survey + Refurb + Finance/Holding + Marketing + Other
```

**Why this matters**

Total acquisition cost is the real purchase price. On a £150,000 flip, expect £155,000 to £165,000 after all costs. Budget for it before you make an offer.
