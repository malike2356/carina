# BTL Void Allowance: The One Month a Year That Changes Everything

Published: Mon, 19 Oct 2026 09:00:00 +0000

Slug: btl-void-allowance-the-one-month-a-year-that-changes-everything

In the BTL cashflow calculation we just ran, the void allowance was £92 a month, or £1,100 a year. That is one month of lost rent. It is the difference between a property that barely cashflows and one that does not.

Void allowance for BTL is typically one month per year, or roughly 8% of your gross rent. In practice, voids vary depending on location, property type, and tenant demand. In Portsmouth, where the rental market is strong, voids might be as low as two to three weeks between tenancies. In weaker markets, they can stretch to two months.

The key insight is that void allowance is a cost of doing business, not an unexpected disaster. You should plan for it. If you budget for zero voids, you are budgeting for a fantasy.

If you own a portfolio of ten BTLs and each has a one-month void per year, that is ten months of lost rent across your portfolio. At £1,000 per property per month, that is £10,000 in lost income every year.

The best way to reduce voids is to retain good tenants. A tenant who stays for three years gives you two void periods instead of five or six. That is three to four extra months of rent over the holding period. On a £1,000-a-month property, that is £3,000 to £4,000 in your pocket. Good tenant retention is the most undervalued skill in BTL investing.

**The formula**

```
Annual Void Allowance = Monthly Rent × Void Months
```

**Why this matters**

Budget for one month void per year per BTL property. Good tenant retention is the single most effective way to reduce voids. A tenant who stays three years saves you thousands in lost rent and letting fees.
