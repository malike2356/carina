# SA Monthly Revenue: Breaking Down Your Serviced Accommodation Income

Monthly revenue in SA is less useful than annual because it fluctuates wildly. July in Portsmouth might generate twice the revenue of January. A bad month with low occupancy or a maintenance issue can halve your income.

But you should still calculate it for budgeting. Your average monthly revenue is your annual revenue divided by 12. If your annual revenue is £28,105, your average monthly revenue is £2,342.

The reality is that summer months might bring £3,500 to £4,000 while winter months bring £1,500 to £2,000. You need to budget for these swings. Set aside surplus from summer months to cover winter shortfalls.

The advantage of SA over BTL is that you can actively manage your revenue. You can raise prices during peak periods, offer discounts during slow periods, and adjust your marketing to drive bookings. A passive BTL cannot do any of this.

In Portsmouth, the peak season runs from May to September, driven by tourism, events, and good weather. The shoulder months of April, October, and November can still perform well with the right pricing. December to February is typically the slowest period.

**The formula**

```
Monthly Revenue = Nightly Rate × Occupancy Rate × 30.4167
```

**Why this matters**

SA monthly revenue fluctuates significantly. Summer months in Portsmouth can be double winter months. Budget conservatively and build cash reserves from peak season to cover the quieter months.
