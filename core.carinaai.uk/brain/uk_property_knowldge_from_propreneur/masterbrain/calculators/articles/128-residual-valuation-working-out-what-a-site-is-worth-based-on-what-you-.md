# Residual Valuation: Working Out What a Site Is Worth Based on What You Can Build

Residual valuation is the standard method for valuing development land. You start with the GDV of the finished development, subtract all costs including your target profit, and the remainder is what the land is worth.\n\nA site could support four flats worth £280,000 each, giving a GDV of £1.12 million. Build costs are £600,000. Professional fees at 12% are £72,000. Contingency at 10% is £60,000. Finance costs are £70,000. Your target profit is £200,000. The residual land value is £1,120,000 minus £600,000 minus £72,000 minus £60,000 minus £70,000 minus £200,000, which equals £118,000. That is the most you can pay for the land.\n\nResidual valuation protects you from overpaying for land. If the seller wants £250,000 and your residual calculation says £118,000, the deal does not work. You either need to increase GDV, reduce costs, accept lower profit, or walk away.\n\nThe residual method is sensitive to every assumption. A 10% change in GDV or build costs creates a much larger percentage change in the residual land value. Run multiple scenarios with conservative assumptions.

**The formula**

```
Land Value = GDV - Build Costs - Finance - Profit Requirement
```

**Why this matters**

Residual valuation tells you the maximum you can pay for development land. It is highly sensitive to assumptions. Run conservative scenarios. If the residual is below the asking price, the deal does not work.
