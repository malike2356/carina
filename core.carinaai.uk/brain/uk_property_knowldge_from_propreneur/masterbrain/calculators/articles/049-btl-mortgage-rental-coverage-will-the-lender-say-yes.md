# BTL Mortgage Rental Coverage: Will the Lender Say Yes?

Published: Mon, 02 Nov 2026 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: btl-mortgage-rental-coverage-will-the-lender-say-yes

---

You may love a property. But the lender decides whether you can buy it. And their decision is based on one number: can the rent cover the mortgage?

Mortgage rental coverage is the ratio of monthly rent to the monthly mortgage payment. Most BTL lenders require the rent to be at least 125% of the mortgage payment at a stressed rate. If your rent is £1,100 and the stressed payment is £750, you need the rent to be at least £938. Your £1,100 passes.

But if the stressed payment is £900, you need the rent to be at least £1,125. Your £1,100 fails. The lender says no.

The stress rate varies by lender. Some use 5.5%. Others use the pay rate plus 1%. Higher rate taxpayers face a 145% coverage requirement instead of 125%. This makes BTL harder for higher earners.

The practical impact is that you need more equity or a cheaper property to pass the test. A larger deposit reduces the loan amount, which reduces the stressed payment. If you are struggling to pass the stress test, consider whether a bigger deposit or a lower-priced property makes more sense.

Run the stress test before you make an offer. If the numbers do not work, you will waste time and money on a property you cannot finance.

**The formula**

```
Rental Coverage Ratio = Monthly Rent / Monthly Mortgage Payment
```

**Why this matters**

The mortgage rental coverage test is the gatekeeper for BTL finance. Run it before you make an offer. If the rent at 5.5% does not cover 125% of the payment, the lender will likely decline.
