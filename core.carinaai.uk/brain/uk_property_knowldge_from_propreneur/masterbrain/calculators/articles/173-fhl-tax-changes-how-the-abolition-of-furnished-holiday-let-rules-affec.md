# FHL Tax Changes: How the Abolition of Furnished Holiday Let Rules Affects You

Published: Mon, 10 Jan 2028 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: fhl-tax-changes-how-the-abolition-of-furnished-holiday-let-rules-affec

---

The Furnished Holiday Let regime was abolished in April 2025. Previously, FHL properties received favourable tax treatment including full mortgage interest relief and capital allowances. From April 2025, FHL properties are treated as standard rental properties for tax purposes.\n\nThis change has significant implications for SA operators. The ability to claim capital allowances on furniture and equipment is gone. Mortgage interest relief is now restricted to the 20% tax credit. The post-tax return on SA properties has reduced, especially for higher rate taxpayers.\n
If you operate SA properties, review your tax position for the current year. The abolition of FHL treatment means your tax bill will be higher. Consider whether your SA investment still delivers the post-tax return you need, or whether a different structure would be more tax-efficient.
