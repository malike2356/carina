# SA Cleaning Cost per Stay: The Hidden Cost That Eats Your Margin

Published: Mon, 25 Jan 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: sa-cleaning-cost-per-stay-the-hidden-cost-that-eats-your-margin

---

Cleaning is the cost that SA operators underestimate most. It is not just a turnover clean between guests. It is laundry, restocking supplies, and deep cleans every few months.\n\nIf your cleaning cost is £40 per stay and the average stay is three nights, that is £40 for every three nights booked, or £13.33 per night. At an ADR of £110, cleaning represents 12% of your revenue. That is significant.\n\nIf stays are shorter, cleaning costs per night go up. A one-night booking with a £40 clean has a cleaning cost of £40 per night, which is 36% of revenue at £110 ADR. That is why many SA operators set minimum stays of two or three nights.\n\nThe formula for cleaning cost per stay is your cleaning cost percentage of annual revenue divided by the number of stays. If cleaning costs are 12% of revenue and you have 85 stays a year, each stay costs 12% of the annual cleaning budget.\n\nTo reduce cleaning costs, optimise your stay length. Longer stays mean fewer turnovers, which means lower cleaning costs as a percentage of revenue. Also consider whether you need a full turn for every booking or can offer a mid-stay clean only for bookings longer than a week.

**The formula**

```
Cleaning Cost per Stay = (Cleaning % × Annual Revenue) / (Occupied Nights / Avg Stay Length)
```

**Why this matters**

Cleaning typically costs 10% to 15% of SA revenue. Longer stays reduce cleaning costs per night. Set minimum stays of two to three nights to keep cleaning costs under control.
