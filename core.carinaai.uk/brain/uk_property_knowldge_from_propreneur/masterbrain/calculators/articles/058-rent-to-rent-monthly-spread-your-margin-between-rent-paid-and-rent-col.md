# Rent-to-Rent Monthly Spread: Your Margin Between Rent Paid and Rent Collected

Published: Wed, 02 Dec 2026 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: rent-to-rent-monthly-spread-your-margin-between-rent-paid-and-rent-col

---

Rent-to-rent is the lowest barrier to entry in property. You do not need a deposit or a mortgage. You lease a property from a landlord and sublet it at a profit. The difference between what you pay the landlord and what you collect from tenants is your spread.

The spread is simple maths. If you lease a four-bed property from a landlord for £1,200 a month and sublet the rooms for £450 each, your total income is £1,800 and your spread is £600 a month.

But that spread is not all profit. You still have costs: management, utilities, insurance, council tax, and maintenance. The spread is your gross margin, not your net profit.

In the South Coast market, typical spreads on rent-to-rent range from £300 to £800 per month depending on the property size and location. A spread below £300 per month is too tight. After costs and voids, you will be earning less than minimum wage for the work involved.

The key to rent-to-rent is finding properties where the spread is wide enough. Look for landlords who want a guaranteed income without the hassle of managing tenants. They accept below-market rent in exchange for certainty. That is where the spread comes from.

**The formula**

```
Monthly Spread = Subtenant Income (g) - Landlord Rent (l)
```

**Why this matters**

Rent-to-rent spread is your gross margin between what you pay and what you collect. A spread below £300 per month is too tight. Above £600, the numbers start to work.
