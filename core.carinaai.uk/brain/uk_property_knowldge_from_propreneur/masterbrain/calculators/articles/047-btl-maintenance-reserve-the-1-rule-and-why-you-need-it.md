# BTL Maintenance Reserve: The 1% Rule and Why You Need It

Published: Mon, 26 Oct 2026 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: btl-maintenance-reserve-the-1-rule-and-why-you-need-it

---

Every property needs maintenance. Boilers break. Roofs leak. Gardens need tending. The question is not whether something will need fixing, but when.

The standard maintenance reserve for BTL is 0.5% to 1.5% of the property value per year. For a £250,000 property, that is £1,250 to £3,750 a year. The percentage depends on the age and condition of the property. An older property with outdated systems sits at the higher end. A modern flat with a new boiler sits at the lower end.

Many landlords ignore this cost until something breaks. Then they scramble for the cash. The disciplined approach is to set aside the maintenance reserve every month so it is there when you need it.

On a £250,000 property with a £1,100 monthly rent, a 10% maintenance reserve means setting aside £110 a month. After two years, you have £2,640 saved. That covers most emergency repairs. After five years, you have £6,600, which covers a new boiler and a rewire.

If you own multiple properties, the maintenance reserve becomes your maintenance fund. Unexpected across a portfolio is predictable. You will spend roughly 0.5% to 1.5% of your portfolio value on maintenance every year. Plan for it.

**The formula**

```
Annual Maintenance = Property Value × Maintenance % (typically 0.5-1.5% per year)
```

**Why this matters**

Set aside 0.5% to 1.5% of property value per year for maintenance. A 10% of rent maintenance reserve is a reasonable starting point. If you do not budget for maintenance, it will budget for you.
