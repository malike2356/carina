# WAULT: The Most Important Number in Commercial Property

Published: Mon, 28 Jun 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: wault-the-most-important-number-in-commercial-property

---

WAULT stands for Weighted Average Unexpired Lease Term. It measures how long the average tenant's lease has left to run, weighted by the rent they pay. A WAULT of eight years is strong. A WAULT of two years is risky.\n\nFor a building with three tenants paying different rents, the WAULT calculation weights each lease by its rent contribution. Tenant A pays £50,000 with ten years left. Tenant B pays £30,000 with five years left. Tenant C pays £20,000 with two years left. The WAULT is (50,000 times 10 plus 30,000 times 5 plus 20,000 times 2) divided by 100,000, which equals 7.1 years.\n\nWAULT determines the risk profile of a commercial property. A long WAULT means stable income and lower risk. A short WAULT means near-term lease events and higher risk. Lenders and investors both look at WAULT when assessing a commercial investment.\n\nA WAULT of five years or more is generally considered investment grade. Below three years, the property is considered asset-managed, meaning the investor needs to actively manage lease renewals and re-lettings. Properties with WAULT below two years are essentially trading assets, not income investments.\n
If you are buying commercial, always ask for the WAULT. It tells you more about the risk than almost any other number.

**The formula**

```
WAULT (years) = Σ(Unexpired Lease Years × Annual Rent) / Σ(Annual Rent)
```

**Why this matters**

WAULT of five years or more is investment grade. Below three years, you are buying an asset management project, not a passive income stream. Always ask for the WAULT before buying commercial.
