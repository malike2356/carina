# BRR Cash Left In: How Much of Your Own Money Is Still Stuck in the Deal

Published: Mon, 08 Feb 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: brr-cash-left-in-how-much-of-your-own-money-is-still-stuck-in-the-deal

---

The goal of BRR is to get as much of your cash back as possible. Cash left in is the amount of your own money that is still tied up after the refinance.\n\nIf your total cost is £196,950 and your refinance proceeds are £165,000, your cash left in is £31,950. That means you have £31,950 of your own cash sitting in the property after the refinance.\n\nCompare that to a standard BTL where your cash left in is your entire deposit plus costs, typically £60,000 to £80,000. BRR reduces your cash commitment significantly.\n\nA successful BRR aims for cash left in of 10% to 20% of the ARV. In our example, £31,950 cash left in on a £220,000 ARV is 14.5%. That is good.\n\nAn excellent BRR gets cash left in to zero or negative, meaning you have pulled all your cash out and still have some left over. This is the holy grail: a property you own that cost you nothing.\n\nCash left in determines your return on equity. If you have £31,950 left in and the property generates £3,000 a year in cashflow, your ROE is 9.4%. That is excellent.

**The formula**

```
Cash Left In = max(0, Total Cost - Refinance Proceeds)
```

**Why this matters**

Cash left in is the most practical measure of BRR success. Aim for 10% to 20% of ARV. Zero cash left in is the holy grail. Your ROE on the remaining cash should be significantly higher than a standard BTL.
