# Development Contingency: The 10% Buffer That Saves Projects

Published: Wed, 28 Apr 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: development-contingency-the-10-buffer-that-saves-projects

---

Every development needs a contingency. Unexpected costs always arise. The ground is harder than expected. The roof needs more work than the survey suggested. A new building regulation requires additional fire protection.\n\nThe industry standard contingency is 5% to 10% of the total construction cost. For a £210,000 build, that is £10,500 to £21,000. This money is not spent unless it is needed. If it is not spent, it goes back to profit.\n\nThe size of the contingency depends on the project type. A simple refurb of a known property type with full surveys might need only 5%. A complex conversion of an old building with unknown structural issues might need 15% to 20%.\n\nExperienced developers have a rule. They release contingency funds only with written approval and evidence of the need. They do not treat contingency as extra budget to spend. It is insurance, not an allowance.\n\nIf you complete a project and use less than half your contingency, you did well. If you use all of it and the project still delivers your target profit, you planned well. If you exceed it, you underestimated the project risk.

**The formula**

```
Contingency = Construction Cost × Contingency Rate (typically 10-15%)
```

**Why this matters**

Contingency of 5% to 10% of construction cost is standard. Treat it as insurance, not spending money. If you use less than half, you managed the project well.
