# Incorporation and SDLT: Transferring Properties Into a Limited Company

Published: Wed, 29 Dec 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: incorporation-and-sdlt-transferring-properties-into-a-limited-company

---

Many landlords consider transferring their existing portfolio into a limited company to benefit from full mortgage interest relief. But transferring ownership triggers SDLT on the market value of the property, not the original purchase price. This can be expensive.\n\nIf you have a portfolio worth £1.5 million and you transfer it into a company, you pay SDLT as if the company were buying the properties at market value. At 5% for the additional dwelling surcharge, that is £75,000 in SDLT. That might outweigh the tax benefits of incorporation.\n
There are reliefs available in some circumstances. Transfers between spouses or between companies in the same group may be exempt. Take professional advice before transferring. The SDLT cost of incorporation can be significant and needs to be weighed against the ongoing tax savings.

**Why this matters**

Transferring properties into a limited company triggers SDLT on the current market value. This can cost tens of thousands. Take professional advice before incorporating.
