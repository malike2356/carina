# Payback Period: How Long Until You Get Your Money Back?

Published: Mon, 30 Aug 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: payback-period-how-long-until-you-get-your-money-back

---

Payback period is the simplest investment metric. It answers one question: how long until the cash you have received equals the cash you invested? For a BTL with £70,000 invested and £3,000 annual cashflow, the payback period is £70,000 divided by £3,000, which is 23.3 years. Few investors hold a BTL for 23 years just to get their money back.\n\nPayback period is useful for quick decisions but it has a major flaw. It ignores cashflows after the payback date and ignores the time value of money. A property that pays back in five years and generates income for another fifteen years is better than one that pays back in five years and generates no more income. Payback period treats them the same.\n\nFor flipping and development, payback period is more relevant because the exit is the payback. A flip that returns your capital and profit in six months has a six-month payback period. That is excellent.\n
Use payback period as a quick filter. If the payback period for a BTL is more than 15 years, the cashflow is too low and the investment relies entirely on capital growth. For flips and BRR, a payback period of 12 to 18 months is typical and acceptable.

**The formula**

```
Payback Period (months) = Total Cash Invested / Monthly Net Cashflow
```

**Why this matters**

Payback period is a quick reality check. A BTL payback period above 15 years means you are relying on capital growth. For flips and BRR, 12 to 18 months is the target.
