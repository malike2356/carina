# GDC: Gross Development Costs -- Everything You Spend Before Completion

Published: Mon, 03 May 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: gdc-gross-development-costs-everything-you-spend-before-completion

---

Gross Development Costs is the sum of everything you spend on the development before you sell or refinance. It includes the site purchase price, construction costs, contingency, professional fees, planning and legal costs, finance costs, and marketing costs.\n\nFor our Portsmouth conversion, the GDC might look like this. Site purchase: £150,000. Stamp duty and legal: £11,500. Construction: £210,000. Contingency at 10%: £21,000. Professional fees at 10% of construction: £21,000. Planning and building regs: £5,000. Development finance interest: £20,000. Marketing and sales: £8,000. Total GDC: £446,500.\n\nThe ratio of GDC to GDV is your development efficiency. With a GDV of £560,000 and GDC of £446,500, your efficiency ratio is 79.7%. That means 79.7% of the finished value goes to costs and 20.3% is profit.\n\nA good development has an efficiency ratio of 75% to 80%. Below 75%, you have a very efficient project. Above 85%, your margins are too thin and the project is risky.\n\nTrack every cost against your GDC budget monthly. If any line item is overrunning, adjust others to compensate. The total GDC is your commitment. Do not let it drift upward.

**The formula**

```
GDC = Purchase Price + Construction Cost + Contingency + Professional Fees
```

**Why this matters**

GDC is your total cost commitment. The efficiency ratio (GDC divided by GDV) should be 75% to 80%. Above 85%, the margins are too thin. Track every cost against budget monthly.
