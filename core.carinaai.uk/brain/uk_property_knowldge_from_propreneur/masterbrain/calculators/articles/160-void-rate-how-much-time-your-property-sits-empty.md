# Void Rate: How Much Time Your Property Sits Empty

Published: Wed, 24 Nov 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: void-rate-how-much-time-your-property-sits-empty

---

The void rate is the percentage of time your property is unoccupied and not generating rent. It is calculated as void days divided by total days in the year. A 5% void rate means the property is empty for roughly 18 days a year.\n\nThe average void rate for UK BTL properties is 5% to 10%, depending on location and market conditions. In strong rental markets like Portsmouth, voids of 3% to 5% are achievable. In weaker markets, voids of 10% to 15% are common.\n\nVoid rate is the most important operational metric for landlords. A single prolonged void can wipe out a year's profit. If a £1,000-a-month property sits empty for three months, you lose £3,000 in rent. On a property with £1,000 a year in net cashflow, that is three years of profit lost in one void.\n
Minimising voids is the highest-leverage activity in property management. Good tenant retention, proactive marketing, and quick turnaround between tenancies all reduce the void rate and protect your income.

**The formula**

```
Void Rate (%) = (Total Void Days / Total Available Days) × 100
```

**Why this matters**

Void rate should be below 10% for a healthy BTL. Three months of void on a £1,000-a-month property costs £3,000. That can wipe out years of profit. Prioritise void reduction.
