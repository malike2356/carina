# Price per Room or Unit: A Simple Comparison Tool for Property Sourcing

Published: Mon, 16 Aug 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: price-per-room-or-unit-a-simple-comparison-tool-for-property-sourcing

---

Price per room or unit is a quick comparison tool, especially useful for HMOs and multi-unit properties. It standardises the purchase price by dividing by the number of rooms or units, letting you compare properties of different sizes.\n\nA five-bed HMO costing £250,000 has a price per room of £50,000. A four-bed HMO costing £220,000 has a price per room of £55,000. Despite the five-bed being more expensive overall, it is cheaper per room.\n\nPrice per unit works the same way for blocks of flats. A block of four flats at £600,000 costs £150,000 per unit. A block of six flats at £900,000 costs £150,000 per unit. The price per unit is the same, so the comparison is on rent per unit and yield.\n\nThe limitation of price per room is that it ignores the quality and size of individual rooms. A room with an ensuite and a separate shower room is worth more than a box room. But as a quick filter, it is useful.\n
Use price per room alongside GRM for a quick evaluation. If the price per room is above £70,000 in Portsmouth, the HMO is expensive and the yield will be tight. If it is below £40,000, the property is cheap per room and worth investigating.

**The formula**

```
Price per Room = Purchase Price / Number of Rooms
Price per Unit = Purchase Price / Number of Units
```

**Why this matters**

Price per room or unit is a useful sourcing filter. It helps you compare properties of different sizes on an apples-to-apples basis. Use it alongside GRM for a quick deal assessment.
