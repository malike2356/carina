# Rent per Square Foot: The Standard Commercial Property Metric

Published: Wed, 07 Jul 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: rent-per-square-foot-the-standard-commercial-property-metric

---

Rent per square foot is the most common way to compare commercial properties. It standardises rent across different sizes. A 1,000 square foot unit at £20,000 a year rents at £20 per square foot. A 500 square foot unit at £12,000 rents at £24 per square foot. The rent per square foot tells you which is more expensive relative to its size.\n\nIn commercial property, rent is always quoted per square foot per year. A shop quoting £30 per square foot is paying £30 for every square foot of floor space. A 1,500 square foot shop at that rate costs £45,000 a year.\n\nTypical rent per square foot on the South Coast varies by sector and location. Prime Portsmouth retail: £35 to £60 per square foot. Secondary retail: £15 to £30. City centre offices: £15 to £25. Industrial and warehousing: £5 to £12.\n\nWhen comparing commercial properties, always use rent per square foot rather than total rent. It tells you whether the rent reflects the quality and location of the space. A high rent per square foot on a small unit can be better value than a low rent per square foot on a large unit.

**The formula**

```
Rent PSF = Annual Rent / Gross Internal Area (sq ft)
Rent PSM = Annual Rent / Gross Internal Area (sq m)
```

**Why this matters**

Rent per square foot is the standard commercial comparison metric. Use it to assess whether a property is fairly priced relative to its size and quality. Local market knowledge is essential for accurate comparisons.
