# Total Development Project Cost: The True Number Before You Sell

Published: Mon, 17 May 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: total-development-project-cost-the-true-number-before-you-sell

---

Total project cost is GDC plus finance costs plus all other expenses. It is the number against which your GDV must be compared to determine profit.\n\nFor our Portsmouth conversion, the total project cost is: GDC of £446,500 plus development finance interest £22,604 plus arrangement fee £6,698 plus monitoring surveyor fees £3,000 plus legal fees for lender £2,000. Total: £480,802.\n\nYour profit is GDV of £560,000 minus total project cost of £480,802, which equals £79,198. Your profit on cost is £79,198 divided by £480,802, which is 16.5%. Your profit on GDV is £79,198 divided by £560,000, which is 14.1%.\n\nThe 14.1% profit on GDV is below the 20% target. The project is marginally viable. This is the reality of development on the South Coast, where high build costs and high site prices squeeze margins.\n\nTo improve the margin, you need either a lower site price, lower build costs, a higher GDV, or a faster build. Every improvement flows directly to the bottom line.

**The formula**

```
Total Project Cost = GDC + Arrangement Fee + Finance Interest
```

**Why this matters**

Total project cost is GDC plus all finance costs. A 20% profit on GDV is the target. If your total project cost pushes profit below 15%, the project is marginal and needs careful risk management.
