# DCF: Discounted Cash Flow Valuation for Property Investments

Published: Wed, 28 Jul 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: dcf-discounted-cash-flow-valuation-for-property-investments

---

DCF is the most sophisticated valuation method. It projects all future cashflows from a property, discounts them back to today's value using a required rate of return, and sums them up. The result is the net present value. If it is positive, the investment beats your required return.\n\nFor a BTL held for ten years, your DCF includes annual net cashflows plus the projected sale proceeds at the end. If your required return is 8%, you discount each year's cashflow back by 8% per year. A cashflow of £3,000 in year five is worth £2,041 in today's money at 8%.\n\nDCF is rarely used by individual BTL investors because it requires assumptions about future rents, costs, capital growth, and exit timing. But professional investors and commercial property buyers use it because it forces them to be explicit about their assumptions.\n\nThe key insight from DCF is that future cashflows are worth less than current cashflows. A property that delivers strong income today is worth more than one that promises strong income in five years. DCF quantifies this difference.\n
If you are serious about property investment, learn to build a simple DCF model. It will transform how you evaluate deals.

**The formula**

```
Value = Σ[CF(t) / (1 + r)^t]  for t = 1 to n
       + Terminal Value / (1 + r)^n
```

**Why this matters**

DCF is the gold standard for property valuation. It accounts for the time value of money and forces explicit assumptions about future performance. A positive NPV means the investment beats your required return.
