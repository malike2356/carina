# Commercial Yield Shift: How Yield Changes Affect Your Capital Value

Published: Wed, 23 Jun 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: commercial-yield-shift-how-yield-changes-affect-your-capital-value

---

In commercial property, a small change in yield creates a large change in capital value. This is called yield shift. Understanding it is essential for managing commercial property risk.\n\nIf yields shift from 7% to 6.5% on a property with £25,000 net income, the capital value changes from £357,143 to £384,615. That is a gain of £27,472, or 7.7%, from a 0.5% yield change.\n\nIf yields shift the other way, from 7% to 7.5%, the capital value drops to £333,333. That is a loss of £23,810, or 6.7%. The same 0.5% change in the wrong direction.\n\nThis leverage effect means commercial property is sensitive to interest rates and market sentiment. When the Bank of England raises rates, commercial yields tend to increase (soften), which reduces capital values. When rates fall, yields harden and values rise.\n\nThe key metric is the yield shift sensitivity. For a property yielding 7%, a 1% increase in yield reduces the capital value by roughly 12.5%. For a property yielding 5%, the same 1% increase reduces value by roughly 16.7%. Lower-yielding properties are more sensitive to yield shifts.

**The formula**

```
New Capital Value = Income / New Yield
Capital Gain = New Capital Value - Old Capital Value
Capital Gain % = (Capital Gain / Old Capital Value) × 100
```

**Why this matters**

Yield shift is the biggest risk in commercial property. A 0.5% yield change delivers a 6% to 8% capital value change. Monitor market yields and interest rates closely. Lower-yielding properties are more sensitive.
