# Business Cashflow Forecast Model - Property Sourcing Business

## Overview

A property sourcing business has multiple income streams that build over time:
1. **Deal Sourcing Fees** - primary income, paid per deal sold
2. **Rental/R2R Income** - recurring monthly cashflow from own portfolio
3. **Property Management Fees** - recurring income from managing others' properties
4. **Staging Income** - project-based income from furnishing/staging SA properties

The model below shows a typical year-one ramp-up trajectory for a new property sourcing business.

---

## Income Categories

| Income Stream | Description | Typical Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Deal Sourcing Fee | One-time fee per sourced deal sold to investor | £3,000-£9,000 per deal |
| Rental Income | Net cashflow from own R2R or BTL properties | £200-£1,500/month per property |
| Property Management Fee | % of rent for managing client properties | 10-15% of rent |
| Staging Fee | Setup and furnishing SA properties for clients | £6,000-£10,000 per property |
| Loan/Finance (startup) | Bridge funding for early-stage operations | One-off |

## Revenue Ramp-Up Pattern

This model reflects the typical property sourcing business ramp-up:

### Months 1-3 (Setup Phase)
- Focus: building systems, marketing, finding area
- Income: low/zero sourcing fees, possibly one deal
- Expense profile: fixed costs only, no employees
- Cash burn: ~£1,000-£1,500/month

### Months 4-6 (First Deals Phase)
- First deal completions
- Marketing spend increases to build pipeline
- Target: 1-2 deals per month at £3,000-£6,000 each
- First R2R properties potentially acquired

### Months 7-9 (Scaling Phase)
- Consistent deal flow (2-3/month)
- R2R income adds recurring base
- Staging services begin if offered

### Months 10-12 (Systematic Phase)
- 3+ deals per month
- Recurring income building
- Staging/management as secondary revenue streams
- Consider first employee or VA

## Goal Setting Framework

**Primary Goal:** £10,000 revenue per month (break-even for solo operator + modest salary)

This requires:
- 2-3 deals/month at £3,000-£5,000 each, OR
- 1 deal at £6,000 + 2 R2R properties generating £1,500 combined cashflow + staging, OR
- Combination of income streams adding to £10,000+

---

## Key Financial Controls

- Review cashflow weekly, not monthly
- Separate business bank account from day one
- Track every expense against category for tax reporting
- Set aside 25-30% of gross income for tax (self-employed)
- Keep 3 months' expenses as cash reserve before scaling
- Never spend staging cost income before the staging fee is received
