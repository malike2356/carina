# Refurbishment Cost Calculator

## How to Use This Calculator

1. List all required work items
2. Assign quantity and unit price from the reference table below
3. Sum all items for Total Costs
4. Add miscellaneous (5%) for builder overruns and small extras
5. Add contingency (10-30% depending on risk) for unknowns
6. Grand Total = Total + Misc + Contingency

## Contingency Framework

| Contingency Level | Rate | When to Apply |
|---|---|---|
| Miscellaneous (builder extras) | 5% of Total Costs | Always |
| Best Case Contingency | 10% of Total Costs | Good condition property, known builder, tight scope |
| Mid Case Contingency | 20% of Total Costs | Some unknowns, typical older terraced property |
| Worst Case Contingency | 30% of Total Costs | Older property, suspected hidden issues, first-time refurb |

### Grand Total Formula
```
Grand Total = Total Item Costs
            + Miscellaneous (5%)
            + Contingency (10-30%)
```

The table below shows typical item costs for a standard terraced property refurb:

| Item | BTL Cost | HMO Cost | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fire doors | £0 | £1,750 | +£1,750 |
| Fire alarm (wired) | £0 | £2,000 | +£2,000 |
| Ensuites | £0 | £5,250 (3 rooms) | +£5,250 |
| Standard doors | £450 | £450 | £0 |
| Kitchen | £4,500 | £4,500 | £0 |
| Flooring | £1,760 | £1,760 | £0 |
| Decoration | £2,700 | £2,700 | £0 |
| Damp proofing | £2,500 | £2,500 | £0 |
| **Typical Total** | **~£20,000-22,000** | **~£28,000-32,000** | **+£8,000-10,000** |

**Key insight:** HMO refurbs cost ~£8,000-£10,000 more than BTL refurbs for the same property, but deliver significantly higher monthly cashflow.

## HMO-Specific Mandatory Items

These items are **mandatory** for HMO licensing compliance and must be included in every HMO refurb budget:

1. HMO fire doors (FD30) - all habitable rooms
2. Wired interlinked fire alarm system (or equivalent)
3. Fire safety signage, CO detectors, fire extinguishers
4. UPVC windows with fire escape capability in ground/basement rooms
5. Adequate cooking facilities (minimum 1 hob + oven per 5 occupants)
6. HMO licence application fee (£985+ - varies by local authority)
7. Fire risk assessment from accredited officer
