# Manchester (UK): HMO licensing standards and licence conditions

**Updated:** 2026-05-03  
**Kind:** Local authority practice guide (Carina RAG).  
**Source notes:** Manchester City Council HMO standards and conditions PDFs. **Always read the current council PDFs and your licence conditions**; national law also applies. Contact examples: `planning@manchester.gov.uk`, `building.control@manchester.gov.uk`.

## 1. Legal framework (summary)

- HMO definitions under **Housing Act 2004** (standard test, self-contained flat test, converted block test) as applicable.
- Mandatory licensing when criteria met.
- **Article 4 direction** in Manchester: change of use to HMO commonly needs **planning permission** (verify current direction map and exceptions).

## 2. Minimum bedroom floor areas (licence-style table)

| Occupancy | Minimum area |
| --- | --- |
| One person aged under 10 | **4.64 m²** (exemptions for charities or night shelters per original guidance) |
| One person aged 10 or over | **6.51 m²** |
| Two persons aged 10 or over | **10.22 m²** |

- Combined sleeping and living rooms for a person over 10: **10 m²** minimum where that model is used.
- Any room under **4.64 m²** must not be used as sleeping accommodation.

## 3. Amenity ratios

**Kitchen floor areas**

| Persons | Minimum kitchen size |
| --- | --- |
| 2 to 4 | **5.5 m²** |
| 5 to 10 | Base **5.5 m²** plus **1.4 m²** per extra person up to cap in source table |

**Dining space**

| Persons | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| 2 to 5 | **9 m²** |
| Each extra person | add **1.86 m²** |

**Sanitaryware ratio (1:5 style stepping)**

| Persons sharing | Bath or shower | WHB and WC | Separate WC from bathroom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 1 | 1 | Fifth person needs separate WC from bathroom |
| 6 to 9 | 2 | 2 | 1 separate WC |
| 10 to 14 | 3 | 3 | 2 separate |
| 15 to 19 | 4 | 4 | 3 separate |

**Location rules in source:** bathroom not more than **two floors** from sleeping accommodation; WC not more than **one floor** away; WC compartment must include a wash hand basin **inside the same room**.

## 4. Kitchen equipment ratios

| Persons sharing | Sinks | Cooking | Cold storage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 5 | 1 sink | 1 cooker, minimum **4 rings** | 1 fridge plus freezer space |
| 6 to 8 | 1 sink plus dishwasher **or** double-bowl sink | 1 cooker plus combi microwave **or** extra hob **or** range with 4+ rings and 2 ovens | 1 fridge plus 1 separate freezer |
| 9 to 10 | 2 sinks | 2 cookers | 2 fridges plus 1 separate freezer |
| 10 to 14 | 2 sinks plus dishwasher **or** 2 double-bowl sinks | 2 cookers plus combi microwave **or** extra hob | 2 fridges plus 2 separate freezers |
| 15 | 3 sinks | 3 cookers | 3 fridges plus 3 separate freezers |

**Also:** fire blanket by cooker exit; extractor achieving at least **six air changes per hour** in training text; sufficient electrical sockets.

## 5. Bedsit extra standards (where applicable)

**Cooking minimums**

- One person: **2** rings or hot plates plus about **28 litre** oven with grill.  
- Two persons: **3** rings plus full-size oven and grill.  
- Three or more: full-size cooker.

**Worktop:** minimum depth **0.5 m** and length **0.5 m** plus **0.5 m** per extra person (interpret with building control).

**Storage:** about **500 mm** wide base or wall cupboard per person; sink zone excluded from food storage count.

**Fridge volume:** about **28 litres** per person plus freezer compartment maintained at food-safe temperature.

## 6. Fire safety (themes)

- Common parts **Fire Risk Assessment** under **Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005** where you manage those parts.
- Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms to current standards (CO where solid fuel present).
- Fire doors with closers on HMO bedroom doors where required.
- Wired interlinked alarms or emergency lighting when layout demands it (professional fire risk assessor).

## 7. Mandatory licence condition themes (examples from pack)

**Gas:** annual Gas Safe certificate available to council.

**Electrical:** safe installation; **five-yearly** inspection cycle cited; produce evidence on demand.

**Fire:** alarms maintained; CO where required.

**Space:** no sleeping room under **4.64 m²**; notify council of substandard rooms; remedy breaches in stated timescales.

**Waste:** comply with municipal scheme; adequate internal and external bins; waste carrier checks for contractors.

**Management:** written tenancy terms; ASB complaints process; referencing policy; deposit protection information supplied.

**Documents to retain:** fire detection service certificate (annual); firefighting equipment service (annual); gas safety (annual); electrical certificates; PAT where used; EPC.

**Enforcement:** unlimited fines, civil penalties, licence revocation, management orders.

## 8. Housing Health and Safety Rating System (HHSRS)

Twenty-nine hazard categories include damp and mould, excess cold, fire, electrical hazards, structural collapse, crowding and space, falls on stairs or level surfaces.

## 9. Other cross-cutting UK requirements (headlines only)

- **Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998** annual Gas Safe checks on relevant appliances and flues.
- **Electrical safety** periodic inspection and Part P compliance for notifiable works.
- **Furniture and Furnishings (Fire) (Safety) Regulations 1988** for supplied furnishings.
- **EPC minimum standards** for private rented sector (thresholds and exemption routes have changed over time; verify current EPC band rules).
- **Legionella** risk assessment where you control water systems.
- **Anti-social behaviour:** reasonable landlord steps where occupiers cause harm.
