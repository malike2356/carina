## AML Risk Assessment - Business-Level Risks Reference

| Potential Risk | Risk Level | Controls |
|---|---|---|
| Individuals - possible money laundering | Medium | CDD including ID, address and source of funds verification. Individual risk assessment before proceeding. Report suspicious activity to MLRO. |
| Limited company clients - hidden ownership | Medium | Screen via Companies House and HMRC. Complete company risk assessment form. |
| Sector targeted by fraudsters selling property they don't own | Medium | Due diligence on the seller as well as the buyer. |
| Overseas clients - different legal frameworks | Low | Additional ID checks (2 forms) + 3 proof of address documents. |
| Overseas clients not met face to face | Medium | Video conference required. ID documents verified by embassy or relevant professional. |
| Legislative and regulatory changes | Low | Monitor legal updates. Implement changes promptly. |
| Property sector attractive for money laundering | Medium | Due diligence on source of funds for all parties. |
