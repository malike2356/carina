# UK Recruitment Industry

## Overview

The UK recruitment sector is the largest in Europe, with annual revenue of approximately £43 billion (2023). The sector places approximately 1.1 million people into permanent roles and 1 million into temporary assignments daily. Over 39,000 recruitment businesses operate in the UK.

The market splits approximately 55% temporary/contract and 45% permanent placement. Professional services, IT, healthcare, and engineering are the highest-value segments.

## Structure

### By Sector
- Professional services (finance, legal, consulting): 22% of market
- IT and technology: 18%
- Healthcare and social care: 14%
- Engineering and manufacturing: 11%
- Education: 8%
- Commercial/office support: 12%
- Industrial and driving: 10%
- Hospitality and retail: 5%

### Major Players
- Global/FTSE: Hays (largest UK recruiter, £1.4bn UK revenue), PageGroup (£1.1bn), Robert Walters
- Professional: SThree (STEM), Harvey Nash, Michael Page
- Generalist: Reed, Adecco UK, ManpowerGroup, Randstad UK
- Healthcare: NHS Professionals (majority NHS-owned), Medacs, Pulse
- Education: Teaching Personnel, Randstad Education, Supply Desk
- IT contractors: many operate through umbrella companies and personal service companies
- Boutique/executive: Odgers Berndtson, Spencer Stuart, Egon Zehnder

### Models
- **Contingency**: Fee only on successful placement (15-30% of first-year salary)
- **Retained/executive search**: Upfront fee, typically 25-35% of first-year package
- **Temporary/contract**: Margin on hourly rate paid to worker
- **RPO (Recruitment Process Outsourcing)**: Embedded recruitment function
- **MSP (Managed Service Provider)**: Management of multiple recruitment suppliers
- **Statement of Work (SoW)**: Outcome-based consultancy engagement rather than time-based

| GLAA (Gangmasters and Labour Abuse Authority) | Licenses labour providers in agriculture, food processing, shellfish |
| REC (Recruitment and Employment Confederation) | Trade body. Code of Professional Practice. |
| ICO | GDPR compliance in candidate data handling |

| FCA | Regulated finance roles (SMCR) |

### Key Legislation
- Employment Agencies Act 1973 and Conduct Regulations 2003; core framework. Prohibits charging fees to work-seekers, regulates temp-to-perm fees.
- IR35 (Off-Payroll Working Rules); determines whether contractors working through personal service companies are deemed employees for tax purposes. Reforms in public sector (2017) and private sector (2021). Major commercial impact on contractor market.
- Agency Workers Regulations 2010; after 12 weeks in same role, agency workers entitled to same basic working conditions as direct employees
- Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations 2003; specific rules on temp placements, client accounts, records
- Modern Slavery Act 2015; supply chain transparency. Particularly relevant for labour-intensive sectors.
- Equality Act 2010; prohibits discrimination in recruitment advertising, selection, and placement
- Immigration Act 2016; illegal working offence, right to work checks
- Skilled Worker visa system; post-Brexit points-based immigration. Sponsorship licence requirements for employers.
- Data Protection Act 2018 / UK GDPR; sensitive personal data handling, retention periods, subject access requests from candidates

## Market Data
- Total UK recruitment market: £43 billion
- Number of recruitment businesses: 39,000+
- Permanent placements: 1.1 million per year
- Temporary workers on assignment daily: 1 million
- Average permanent placement fee: 18-22% of first-year salary
- Average temp margin: 15-25%
- Contractor market: approximately 1.8 million people working through limited companies or umbrella companies
- IR35 changes shifted approximately 30% of contractors to inside-IR35 (PAYE) status
- Average time-to-hire: 28-45 days (varies significantly by sector and seniority)
- Cost-per-hire (in-house): approximately £3,000-5,000

## Pain Points (Agent Opportunity)
1. **Candidate sourcing and matching**: CV parsing, skills extraction, and job matching. High volume, keyword-driven.
2. **Right to work checks**: Manual document checking. Digital right to work verification (Home Office online service) growing but not universal.
3. **IR35 status determination**: SDS (Status Determination Statement) requirements for medium/large clients. Complex multi-factor assessment.
4. **Compliance record keeping**: Candidate records, placement records, temp working time records. EAS inspection risk.
5. **Contractor onboarding**: Umbrella company verification, insurance checks, qualification verification.
6. **Timesheet processing**: Weekly timesheet collection, approval, and invoicing for temporary workers.
7. **Candidate communication**: Interview scheduling, feedback collection, placement follow-up.
8. **Reference and background checking**: Employment references, DBS checks, qualification verification, financial probity for regulated roles.
9. **Job advert compliance**: Equality Act requirements, salary transparency (some clients mandate), geographical salary differentials.
10. **Supply chain management (MSP)**: Multi-agency performance management, rate cards, compliance auditing.
