# UK Logistics & Transport Industry

## Overview

The UK logistics sector contributes approximately £130 billion annually to the economy (2023) and employs approximately 2.7 million people across 200,000+ businesses. The sector is the backbone of UK trade, handling 95% of all imported and exported goods by volume (predominantly through sea ports, with the Channel Tunnel and air freight for high-value goods).

The UK has one of the most developed e-commerce logistics markets globally. Road freight dominates domestic transport, carrying 89% of all goods moved within the UK.

## Structure

### Road Freight
- Approximately 360,000 registered HGVs in the UK
- Major operators: DHL Supply Chain, Wincanton, XPO Logistics, Eddie Stobart (Culina Group), Gregory Distribution
- Approximately 30,000 road haulage operators (many SMEs, owner-operators)
- HGV driver shortage: approximately 60,000 shortage (improved from 100,000 during 2021 crisis)
- Average HGV driver salary: £35,000-45,000

### Maritime
- UK ports handle approximately 480 million tonnes of cargo annually
- Major ports: Felixstowe (largest container port, 4.3m TEU), Southampton, London Gateway, Liverpool, Immingham, Dover (busiest roll-on/roll-off port in Europe)
- Container shipping: dominated by global alliances (Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, COSCO)
- Short sea shipping: significant for UK-EU trade post-Brexit

### Rail Freight
- Approximately 5% of UK freight moved by rail
- Major operators: DB Cargo UK, Freightliner, GB Railfreight
- Key flows: intermodal containers from ports to inland terminals, aggregates, automotive

### Air Freight
- Dominated by express/parcel operators and belly cargo in passenger aircraft
- Major hubs: Heathrow (largest UK cargo airport), East Midlands (DHL/UPS hubs), Stansted (FedEx)
- Handles approximately 2.5 million tonnes annually

### Warehousing
- Approximately 600 million square feet of warehouse space in the UK
- Major operators: GLP (Gazeley), Prologis, Segro, Tritax Big Box
- Amazon occupies approximately 80 million sq ft across 80+ fulfilment centres
- Strategic locations: "Golden Triangle" (M1/M6/M69 corridor in the Midlands); approximately 70% of UK population reachable within 4.5 hours
- Third-party logistics (3PL): DHL, XPO, GXO, Culina, Wincanton

### Parcels and Last-Mile
- Approximately 5.5 billion parcels delivered annually in the UK (2023)
- Major carriers: Royal Mail (31% market share), Amazon Logistics (17%), Evri (formerly Hermes, 14%), DPD (11%), Yodel (7%), UPS (6%)
- Same-day delivery: growing segment. Deliveroo, Uber Eats expanding beyond food into grocery and retail.

## Key Regulation

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| DVSA (Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency) | HGV testing, operator licensing, roadside enforcement |
| Traffic Commissioners | Issue and regulate operator licences (O-licences) |
| Office of Rail and Road (ORR) | Rail regulation, road monitoring |
| Maritime and Coastguard Agency (MCA) | Maritime safety and regulation |
| Civil Aviation Authority (CAA) | Air transport regulation |
| HMRC | Customs declarations, excise movements, AEO certification |
| HSE | Workplace transport safety, warehousing safety |
| Home Office / Border Force | Border security, customs checks |
| Environment Agency | Emissions regulations, waste carrier licensing |

### Key Legislation
- Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act 1995; operator licensing regime
- Road Traffic Act 1988 and Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988; drivers' hours, tachograph rules, roadworthiness
- Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020; post-Brexit customs framework. Full border controls phased in through 2024.
- UKCA / CE marking; product safety marking. UKCA regime now running alongside retained CE recognition.
- Climate Change Act 2008 (as amended); net zero target affects fleet decarbonisation timeline
- Transport Act 2000; local transport plans, road user charging powers
- Cabotage rules; restrictions on non-UK hauliers operating domestic UK movements
- Working Time Regulations; particularly for mobile transport workers

## Market Data
- UK logistics spend: approximately £130 billion
- Road freight (tonne-km): 89% of domestic freight
- HGV drivers: approximately 300,000 (down from 320,000 pre-Brexit)
- Parcels: 5.5 billion per year
- Combined transport: growing but still small (<1% of domestic freight)
- Online retail returns: 22-30% (heavily impacts reverse logistics)
- Fuel duty: 52.95p per litre (diesel and petrol). Single largest operating cost for road hauliers.
- Average warehouse rent: £7-9 per sq ft (Midlands), £15-25 per sq ft (London)

## Pain Points (Agent Opportunity)
1. **Customs declarations**: Post-Brexit, exporters and importers must file customs declarations. HMRC CDS (Customs Declaration Service). Approximately 260 million declarations per year.
2. **Operator licence compliance**: Transport Manager responsibilities. Vehicle maintenance schedules, drivers' hours monitoring, tachograph analysis.
3. **Proof of delivery (POD)**: Paper-based POD still prevalent in general haulage. Digitisation opportunity.
4. **Fleet maintenance scheduling**: Preventative maintenance intervals (6-weekly for HGVs). MOT management. Defect reporting and rectification.
5. **Supply chain visibility**: Real-time tracking across multiple carriers and modes. Port congestion alerts.
6. **Warehouse inventory**: WMS accuracy, pick-and-pack error rates (typically 1-3%), cycle counting.
7. **Dangerous goods (ADR)**: Classification, packaging, documentation, driver training records. High compliance stakes.
8. **Incoterms and trade documentation**: Bills of lading, certificates of origin, EUR1/UK certification, letters of credit.
9. **Reverse logistics**: Returns processing, refurbishment, recycling/disposal. Growing with e-commerce.
10. **Route optimisation**: Fuel costs, driver hours constraints, congestion charge zones (ULEZ expansion, CAZ zones), customer delivery windows.

## Key Terminology
- **O-licence**: Operator's licence. Required to operate HGVs over 3.5 tonnes.
- **CPC (Certificate of Professional Competence)**: Transport Manager qualification. Mandatory for O-licence holders.
- **Driver CPC**: 35 hours periodic training every 5 years for professional drivers.
- **Tachograph**: Records driving time, breaks, rest periods. Digital tachographs mandatory for vehicles first registered after 2006. Smart tachographs (version 2) from 2023.
- **CDS**: Customs Declaration Service. HMRC's digital customs platform.
- **EORI**: Economic Operators Registration and Identification number. Required for UK-EU trade.
- **AEO**: Authorised Economic Operator. Trusted trader status for simplified customs.
- **GVW (Gross Vehicle Weight)**: Maximum permitted weight of vehicle and load.
- **TEU**: Twenty-foot Equivalent Unit. Standard container measure.
- **Incoterms**: International Commercial Terms. Define buyer/seller responsibilities for delivery, insurance, customs.
- **3PL / 4PL**: Third/Fourth-Party Logistics. Outsourced logistics management.
- **WMS**: Warehouse Management System.
- **TMS**: Transport Management System.
