# UK Agriculture & Food Industry

## Overview

UK agriculture contributes approximately £13 billion annually to the economy (2023) and employs approximately 470,000 people directly, with the wider agri-food sector supporting approximately 4 million jobs. The UK is approximately 60% self-sufficient in food overall, though this varies significantly by product.

Total agricultural land: approximately 17.2 million hectares (70% of UK land area). The sector is undergoing the most significant policy change in decades, transitioning from EU Common Agricultural Policy (CAP) to new UK agricultural support schemes post-Brexit.

## Structure

### Farming
- Approximately 218,000 farm holdings in the UK
- Average farm size: approximately 85 hectares (England)
- Arable (crops): wheat (15m tonnes), barley, oilseed rape, potatoes, sugar beet
- Livestock: sheep (33 million; largest flock in Europe), cattle (9.6 million), pigs (5 million), poultry (180 million birds)
- Dairy: approximately 1.9 million dairy cows, 15 billion litres milk produced annually
- Horticulture: fruit (apples, strawberries, raspberries), vegetables, ornamentals
- Farm types: owner-occupied (64%), tenanted (31%), mixed (5%)

### Agri-food Processing
- Food and drink manufacturing is the UK's largest manufacturing sub-sector (£35 billion turnover)
- See Manufacturing and Retail domains for downstream

### Agricultural Support Reform
- Basic Payment Scheme (BPS, EU CAP legacy): being phased out. 2024 payments reduced. Delinked payments from 2024. Zero by 2028.
- Environmental Land Management (ELM): three new schemes replacing BPS
  - Sustainable Farming Incentive (SFI): pays farmers for environmental actions (soil health, hedgerows, pest management, nutrient management)
  - Countryside Stewardship (CS): pays for specific environmental land management
  - Landscape Recovery: long-term, large-scale habitat restoration

## Key Regulation

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| DEFRA (Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs) | Agricultural policy, environmental regulation, animal health |
| RPA (Rural Payments Agency) | Administers agricultural payments (SFI, CS, BPS legacy) |
| Environment Agency | Water abstraction, waste, nitrate vulnerable zones, flood risk |
| Animal and Plant Health Agency (APHA) | Animal disease surveillance, plant health, veterinary medicines |
| Food Standards Agency (FSA) | Food safety from farm gate onward |
| HSE | Farm safety (agriculture has highest fatal injury rate of any UK sector: 18.7 per 100,000 workers) |
| HMRC | Agricultural property relief (APR) for inheritance tax |
| GLAA (Gangmasters and Labour Abuse Authority) | Licenses labour providers in agriculture, horticulture, and food processing |

## Key Legislation
- Agriculture Act 2020; framework for post-Brexit agricultural policy. Powers for ELM schemes, supply chain fairness, fertiliser regulation.
- Animal Welfare Act 2006; duty of care for kept animals
- Welfare of Farmed Animals (England) Regulations 2007; minimum standards for farm animal welfare
- Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2013; medicines for food-producing animals
- Nitrate Pollution Prevention Regulations 2015; NVZ (Nitrate Vulnerable Zones) rules
- Water Resources Act 1991; abstraction licensing
- Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000; public access, SSSIs
- Tenant farming: Agricultural Holdings Act 1986, Agricultural Tenancies Act 1995
- Seasonal Worker visa scheme; 45,000 visas available for 2024 (up from 30,000). Horticulture and poultry only.

## Market Data
- Total agricultural output: approximately £32 billion (including subsidies)
- Agri-food sector GVA: approximately £127 billion
- UK self-sufficiency in food: approximately 60% overall
  - Products where UK self-sufficient: wheat (90%), barley (100%), milk (100%), eggs (90%), poultry (80%)
  - Products where UK import-dependent: fresh fruit (17% self-sufficient), fresh vegetables (55%), tomatoes (12% in winter)
- Agricultural workforce: approximately 470,000 (plus approximately 60,000 seasonal workers)
- Average farm business income: approximately £44,000 (varies dramatically by farm type, from negative for grazing livestock to £100,000+ for large arable and dairy)
- Total CAP payments to UK farmers (pre-Brexit): approximately £3.4 billion annually
- New ELM budget: approximately £2.4 billion annually by 2027/28

## Pain Points (Agent Opportunity)
1. **SFI/CS applications**: Complex environmental action selection and mapping. Digital land management plans required.
2. **NVZ compliance**: Nitrate vulnerable zone record-keeping. Fertiliser planning, livestock manure N loading calculations (170 kg/ha limit).
3. **Cross-compliance**: Historic CAP requirements still relevant in transition. GAECs (Good Agricultural and Environmental Conditions).
4. **Livestock records**: Birth registrations, movement reporting (BCMS; British Cattle Movement Service), medicine records, mortality reporting.
5. **Food safety and traceability**: From farm to fork. Assured Food Standards (Red Tractor), farm assurance inspections.
6. **Seasonal worker management**: Right to work checks, accommodation compliance, gangmaster licensing.
7. **Crop protection records**: Pesticide application records (legal requirement). Sprayer testing (mandatory NSTS testing). Pesticide registers.
8. **Tenancy agreements**: Complex succession planning. AHA 1986 vs ATA 1995 tenancy rights. End-of-tenancy compensation for improvements.
9. **Inheritance tax planning**: Agricultural Property Relief (APR) and Business Property Relief (BPR). Capital gains tax rollover relief.
10. **Carbon and environmental reporting**: Growing supply chain pressure. Carbon footprint calculators. Biodiversity net gain requirements from developers.
