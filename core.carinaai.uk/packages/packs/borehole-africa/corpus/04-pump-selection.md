# Pump Selection - Ghana Boreholes

## Hand Pumps (No Grid Power)

### Afridev
Standard for CWSA community projects. Yield up to ~30 LPM at moderate head.
Widely available spare parts in Ghana. VLOM (village level operation) compatible.

### India Mark II
Higher yield capacity; used for larger communities or higher head.
Heavier duty lever mechanism; more maintenance than Afridev in sandy water.

Use hand pumps when: yield under 30 LPM, no reliable electricity, community management model.

## Submersible Pumps

Common makes in Ghana: Grundfos SP, Pedrollo, Franklin Electric.

Sizing formula (field estimate):
- Hydraulic power (kW) = Q x H x 9.81 / 6000 where Q is LPM and H is total dynamic head in metres
- Motor kW = hydraulic kW / 0.65 (pump efficiency)

Total dynamic head = static lift + drawdown + friction losses + delivery head.

## Solar Pumping

Popular for off-grid community and agricultural schemes.
Rule of thumb: solar array kWp approximately 1.5 x motor kW for Ghana insolation.
Include storage tank for evening demand when using solar direct.

## Generator Backup

Common on rig sites for drilling; also used for interim community supply before grid connection.
Size generator 1.25x motor nameplate kW for submersible start current.
