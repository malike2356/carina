# Casing and Screen Specifications - Ghana

## uPVC Casing Grades

| Class | SDR | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Class B | SDR 21 | Shallow wells; static water level above 20m |
| Class C | SDR 17 | Standard community boreholes |
| Class D | SDR 13.6 | Deep or high-pressure applications |

Standard community borehole diameter: 125mm (5 inch) uPVC.
Nominal wall thickness must meet CWSA rural water supply standards.

## Screen Slot Sizes

- 0.5mm slots: fine sand aquifers, alluvial layers
- 1.0mm slots: crystalline basement fracture zones, mixed aquifers
- 1.5mm slots: coarse gravel aquifers (rare in Ghana community wells)

Screen length: typically match productive aquifer interval; minimum 3m for CWSA.

## Annular Sealing

Sanitary seal: neat cement grout from surface to minimum 6m depth,
or 1m below static water level (whichever is deeper).

Gravel pack: washed quartz sand or river gravel 2-4mm between borehole wall and screen.
Pack thickness typically 50mm annulus.

## Installation Notes

- Centralisers every 3m on casing string
- Thread compound on uPVC threads; do not over-torque
- Develop borehole after casing: surging, air lifting, or pumping until clear
- Cap borehole when not drilling; prevent contamination
