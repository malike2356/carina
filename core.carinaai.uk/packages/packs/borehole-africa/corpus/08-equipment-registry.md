# Drilling Equipment Common in Ghana

## Rig Manufacturers

### Massenza (Italy)
Widely imported into West Africa. MI series truck-mounted and trailer rigs.
Known for: reliable hydraulics, good dealer support in Accra.
Typical depth capacity: 150-300m depending on model.

### Hauhinco (Germany)
Heavy-duty mining and water well rigs. Higher capital cost.
Used by larger contractors for deep municipal boreholes.

### Hausherr (Germany)
Legacy rigs still operating in Ghana from 1990s-2000s imports.
Parts availability declining; maintenance intensive.

### Longyear / Ingersoll Rand heritage
Deep hole capability; less common on community projects.

### Diedrich D-50 / D-90
American-style rigs; seen with NGOs and mining sector contractors.

## Ancillary Equipment

- Air compressors: 750-1050 CFM for DTH operations
- Mud pumps: centrifugal for mud rotary circulation
- Generators: 50-150 kVA on active drill sites
- Water bowser: drilling fluid makeup and community relations
- Rod and casing handling: breakout wrenches, elevators, slips

## Maintenance Intervals (Typical)

- Hammer service: every 200 drilling hours (DTH)
- Compressor oil and filters: per manufacturer schedule
- Hydraulic fluid: check daily on active sites
- Wireline and hoist cables: inspect before each spud

Report equipment faults in ABBIS immediately for critical breakdowns (rig down).
