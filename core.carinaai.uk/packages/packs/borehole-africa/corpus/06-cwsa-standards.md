# CWSA Standards - Ghana Community Water

## Siting Criteria

Minimum distance requirements (typical CWSA rural water guidelines):
- 30m from latrine or soakaway (some districts require 50-100m)
- 150m from pit latrine in high-risk areas (verify local district assembly rules)
- Upslope from pollution sources where possible
- Avoid burial grounds, cattle kraals, and surface runoff paths

## Construction Requirements

- Rotary drilled hole with full uPVC casing string
- Sanitary seal (grout) from surface to minimum 6m
- Gravel pack around screen section
- Borehole cap to prevent surface contamination
- Apron and drainage around pump pad

## Completion Report Contents

CWSA-format completion report typically includes:
- Client name and location (GPS coordinates)
- Borehole ID and total depth
- Casing design (plain pipe, screen, sump lengths)
- Static water level and yield from pump test
- Driller name, company, and licence reference
- Date of completion and witness signatures

## Pump Test

Standard: 24-hour constant rate test with recovery measurement.
Record: pumping rate (LPM), drawdown at intervals, recovery to static level.

## EPA Ghana

Environmental permit may be required for commercial or bulk water abstraction.
Discharge of drilling mud must not contaminate surface water bodies.
Check EPA regional office for projects over district threshold.
