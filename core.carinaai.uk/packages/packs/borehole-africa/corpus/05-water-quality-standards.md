# Water Quality Standards - Ghana and WHO

## WHO Drinking Water Guidelines (Key Parameters)

| Parameter | WHO Guideline |
|---|---|
| E. coli | 0 per 100 mL (no detectable) |
| Total coliforms | 0 per 100 mL |
| Turbidity | 5 NTU (preferably under 1 NTU at point of use) |
| pH | 6.5 - 8.5 |
| Iron (Fe) | 0.3 mg/L |
| Manganese (Mn) | 0.4 mg/L |
| Fluoride (F) | 1.5 mg/L |
| Nitrate (NO3) | 50 mg/L |
| Arsenic (As) | 0.01 mg/L |

## Ghana Standards Authority (GSA)

Ghana adopts WHO guidelines for most community water supply parameters.
Local labs report in mg/L or NTU; confirm units on certificate.

## Common Ghana Aquifer Issues

- **Iron and manganese**: common in Birimian basement and reducing Voltaian aquifers; causes staining and taste
- **Fluoride**: elevated in some northern granite areas; dental fluorosis risk above 1.5 mg/L
- **Turbidity**: high after drilling; must clear before bacteriological sign-off
- **Hardness**: calcium/magnesium; not health risk but affects soap and scaling

## Testing Protocol

1. Sample after 24-hour pump test and disinfection
2. Bacteriological: E. coli and total coliforms mandatory for CWSA completion
3. Chemical: minimum iron, manganese, pH, turbidity, nitrate
4. Retest if any parameter fails after treatment installation

Not a substitute for Ghana EPA or GSA certified laboratory sign-off.
