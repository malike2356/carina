# Propcalc.uk competitor benchmark (UK deal calculators)

**Tagline:** UK's strategy-first deal calculator: BRR, R2R, HMO, SA and more, with show-your-working underwriting and a built-in refurb budget builder.

**Review date:** March 2026  
**Scope:** Public calculator / deal-analysis tools used by UK residential investors. Not a paid mystery-shopper test; feature matrix is based on published product pages, docs, and our own codebase audit.

**How to read this:** Strong = clearly offered for that strategy or use case. Partial = basic or manual synthesis required. Weak = not a focus or not found publicly. N/A = not applicable to that product type.

## Executive summary

| Platform | Primary role | Best for |
|----------|--------------|----------|
| **PropertyData** | Market data and research | Area comps, yields, valuations, sourcing |
| **PaTMa Deal Finder** | Deal plans + portfolio CRM | BTL/HMO/flip plans with local data and PDF export |
| **Budgerty** (paid tiers) | Multi-scenario modelling | 10+ strategies, saved reports, sensitivity (paid) |
| **My Property Organiser** | Free multi-strategy analyser | Quick tabs for BTL, HMO, BRRR, STL, R2R |
| **PropertyEngine** | Sourcing + calculators | BRR with long-term equity projection |
| **The Property Calculator** | BTL/BRR web calc | Bridging + refinance + void weeks |
| **Excel / Etsy templates** | Custom models | Maximum control; user maintains formulas |
| **Propreneur / propcalc.uk** | Strategy-first underwriting engines | BRR/R2R depth, show working, refurb catalogue, free public calc |

**Honest positioning:** Propcalc is **strongest** where investors need **strategy-specific underwriting** (especially BRR capital recycle + hold, and R2R operator economics) with **formula transparency**. It is **weaker** than PropertyData/PaTMa on **live market comps**, portal browser extensions, and **automated PDF deal packs** (email gate saves to waitlist today; PDF delivery is planned).

## Per-competitor notes

### PropertyData ([propertydata.co.uk](https://propertydata.co.uk))

- **Strengths:** Sold prices, rents, yields, plot map, valuations, SDLT/yield/mortgage calculators, PDF exports for research.
- **Gap vs Propcalc:** Calculators are mostly **top-line** (gross yield, ROI); investors still build **multi-year cashflow and strategy logic** elsewhere. No dedicated **R2R operator** model or **show-your-working** on every output.
- **When PropertyData wins:** "What is this postcode worth / letting for?"
- **When Propcalc wins:** "Does **this** BRR or R2R deal stack after all costs?"

### PaTMa ([patma.co.uk](https://www.patma.co.uk))

- **Strengths:** Deal Finder plans (BTL, HMO, buy-to-sell), refurb toggles, voids, agent fees, local planning/crime data, PDF export, CRM for offers.
- **Gap vs Propcalc:** R2R and BRR are **plan types** inside a broader product; less emphasis on **transparency of every formula** and **dual-phase BRR hold underwriting** in one public surface.
- **When PaTMa wins:** End-to-end **find, plan, track, manage** with UK data baked in.
- **When Propcalc wins:** **Deep strategy engines** and **free, instant** underwriting without portfolio software.

### Budgerty ([budgerty.com](https://budgerty.com/property-deal-calculator/))

- **Strengths:** 10+ strategies, sensitivity, saved reports and exports on **paid** tiers.
- **Gap vs Propcalc:** Free tier is simpler; **show working** and **UK refurb catalogue** are not advertised as core features.
- **When Budgerty wins:** Saved scenarios and **long-horizon** comparison for paying users.
- **When Propcalc wins:** **Free** depth on BRR/R2R/HMO plus **formula narrative** on every calculator.

### My Property Organiser ([mypropertyorganiser.co.uk/deal-analyser](https://mypropertyorganiser.co.uk/deal-analyser))

- **Strengths:** Free tabs for BTL, HMO, BRRR, STL, R2R; 5-year return; claims comprehensive UK coverage.
- **Gap vs Propcalc:** Less evidence of **line-item refurb builder**, **per-output working steps**, and **19-tool** financial suite (DSCR, NPV, etc.) on the same public hub.
- **When MPO wins:** Fast **all-in-one free** tabbed analyser tied to their portfolio product.
- **When Propcalc wins:** **R2R/BRR operator-grade** fields and **audit-style** show working.

### PropertyEngine ([propertyengine.co.uk](https://propertyengine.co.uk/calculators/buy-refurbish-refinance-calculator))

- **Strengths:** BRR with bridging, SDLT, refinance, **30-year equity** projection; sourcing integration.
- **Gap vs Propcalc:** Bundled with **sourcing subscription**; R2R and **show-your-working** less prominent on public calc pages.
- **When PropertyEngine wins:** BRR plus **automated deal sourcing** in one paid platform.
- **When Propcalc wins:** **Multi-strategy** public suite and **hold-phase stress** without sourcing lock-in.

### The Property Calculator ([thepropertycalculator.co.uk](https://www.thepropertycalculator.co.uk/))

- **Strengths:** BRR with bridging, refinance LTV, void weeks, maintenance/management %, mortgage stress ratio.
- **Gap vs Propcalc:** Narrower strategy set; no **R2R**, **Strategy Finder**, or **refurb catalogue**.
- **When they win:** Simple **BRR/BTL** in one page.
- **When Propcalc wins:** Breadth + **transparency layer**.

### Heavy Excel models (investor templates)

- **Strengths:** Full control, lender-specific tabs, tax scenarios, version history in the user's hands.
- **Gap vs Propcalc:** Error-prone, no shared **API**, no **instant public** access, maintenance burden.
- **When Excel wins:** Bespoke lender packs and tax structuring.
- **When Propcalc wins:** **Validated server-side engines**, consistent assumptions, **show working** for teaching and compliance.

### "PropAnalyser" and similar names

No single dominant UK product consistently branded **PropAnalyser** was found in public search (March 2026). Investors often mean **generic deal analysers** (Budgerty, MPO, PaTMa, or spreadsheets). Treat any "PropAnalyser" claim as **compare the feature row**, not the brand.
