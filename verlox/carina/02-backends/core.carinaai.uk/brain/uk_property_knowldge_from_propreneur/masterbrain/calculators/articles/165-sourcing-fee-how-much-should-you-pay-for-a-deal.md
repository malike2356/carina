# Sourcing Fee: How Much Should You Pay for a Deal?

Published: Mon, 13 Dec 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: sourcing-fee-how-much-should-you-pay-for-a-deal

---

A sourcing fee is what you pay to a sourcing agent for finding a deal. It is typically a percentage of the purchase price or a fixed fee. Standard sourcing fees in the UK range from £3,000 to £10,000, or 1% to 2% of the purchase price for larger deals.\n\nThe sourcing fee should be proportional to the value the agent creates. If an agent finds you a property valued at £200,000 that you buy for £160,000, the saving is £40,000. A £5,000 sourcing fee (3.1% of purchase price) is reasonable because you are still saving £35,000.\n\nBut if the agent finds a property with only a £15,000 discount, a £5,000 fee consumes a third of the saving. That is less attractive. The sourcing fee should be a small fraction of the value created.\n
When working with a sourcing agent, agree the fee structure upfront. Fixed fees are simpler but percentage fees align the agent's incentives with finding you a good discount. A good sourcing agent earns their fee by finding deals you could not find yourself.

**The formula**

```
Sourcing Fee = Purchase Price × Sourcing % (typically 1-2%)
Or: fixed fee per deal (£3,000-£10,000+ depending on market)
```

**Why this matters**

Sourcing fees of £3,000 to £10,000 are standard. The fee should be a small fraction of the value created. Agree the structure upfront. A good sourcing agent earns their fee through access to deals.
