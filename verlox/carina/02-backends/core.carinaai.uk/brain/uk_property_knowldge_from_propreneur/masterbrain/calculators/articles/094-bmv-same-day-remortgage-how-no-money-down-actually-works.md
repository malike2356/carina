# BMV Same-Day Remortgage: How No Money Down Actually Works

Published: Wed, 07 Apr 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: bmv-same-day-remortgage-how-no-money-down-actually-works

---

The no-money-down strategy is the holy grail of property investing. Buy below market value, close the purchase, and remortgage on the same day to pull your cash back out. It sounds like magic. It is actually just maths.\n\nThe strategy works like this. You find a property worth £200,000 that you can buy for £160,000. You use a bridging loan to buy it quickly. On the same day you complete the purchase, you remortgage at the full market value of £200,000. At 75% LTV, you get £150,000 from the remortgage. That repays the bridging loan of £160,000. You are short by £10,000.\n\nBut here is the key. If the discount is bigger, or if you can get a higher LTV on the remortgage, the numbers change. A 25% discount on a £200,000 property (purchase at £150,000) with a 75% LTV remortgage gives you £150,000. You have zero cash left in. No money down.\n\nThe same-day remortgage requires a lender who will lend based on the market value, not the purchase price. Not all lenders do this. You need a specialist lender or a standard lender who accepts a desktop valuation at the higher value.\n
The strategy is real but rare. Genuine no-money-down deals require a deep discount, a cooperative lender, and a property in good enough condition to justify the higher valuation. If you can find one, it is the most capital-efficient deal in property.

**The formula**

```
Bridging Amount = Purchase Price (100% bridging)
Remortgage Proceeds = Market Value × Remortgage LTV
Bridging Fee = Purchase Price × Arrangement Fee %
```

**Why this matters**

Same-day remortgage for no money down requires a 25%+ BMV discount and a lender who values at market price rather than purchase price. It is possible but rare. Focus on finding the discount first.
