# BRR Equity Created: How Much Value You Built Through the Refurb

Published: Wed, 17 Feb 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: brr-equity-created-how-much-value-you-built-through-the-refurb

---

Equity created is the difference between your after-refurb value and your total cost. It is the value you built through your refurbishment work.\n\nIf your ARV is £220,000 and your total cost is £196,950, your equity created is £23,050. That £23,050 belongs to you. It sits in the property as equity until you sell or refinance again.\n\nEquity created is taxed differently from income. It is not realised until you sell. This makes BRR tax-efficient compared to flipping, where profit is taxed immediately on sale.\n\nThe key is that equity created should be significantly more than the refurb cost. If you spend £30,000 on refurb and create £23,050 in equity, you have not achieved a good return on your refurb spend. You want every £1 of refurb to create at least £1.50 to £2 of equity.\n\nIn the South Coast market, where refurb costs are high, the ratio of equity created to refurb spend is typically 1.2 to 1.8. If you can consistently achieve 2:1 or better, you have a strong BRR strategy.

**The formula**

```
Equity Created = Post-Refurb Value - Refinance Proceeds
```

**Why this matters**

BRR equity created should be at least 1.5 times your refurb spend. If your refurb creates less equity than it costs, the deal is not working. Focus on properties where value uplift exceeds refurb costs.
