# Positive Leverage: When Borrowing Money Actually Boosts Your Returns

Published: Mon, 11 Oct 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: positive-leverage-when-borrowing-money-actually-boosts-your-returns

---

Positive leverage occurs when the property's net yield exceeds your mortgage interest rate. If your net yield is 5% and your mortgage rate is 4.5%, borrowing money amplifies your return. If the yield drops below the rate, you have negative leverage and debt reduces your returns.\n\nThe positive leverage condition is the spread between yield and cost of debt. A positive spread means leverage works for you. A negative spread means leverage works against you. In the current rate environment, many South Coast BTLs have negative leverage because yields are below mortgage rates.\n\nIf you have negative leverage, a larger deposit (reducing the loan amount) can improve your position. A 50% deposit means half the debt, which means half the leverage impact. The cash-on-cash return improves even though the LTV is lower.\n
Understand whether your portfolio has positive or negative leverage. If it is negative, the properties need to deliver capital growth that exceeds the negative spread. If they do not, you would be better off with less debt.

**The formula**

```
Cap Rate > Mortgage Constant
```

**Why this matters**

Positive leverage means debt boosts your returns. Negative leverage means debt hurts you. In the current rate environment, many BTLs have negative leverage. Know which camp you are in.
