# Development Equity Required: How Much Cash You Need to Bring

Published: Wed, 19 May 2027 09:00:00 +0000
Category: Calculations
Slug: development-equity-required-how-much-cash-you-need-to-bring

---

The equity required is your own cash that goes into the development. It is the gap between the development loan and the total project cost. If the loan is £334,875 and the total cost is £480,802, your equity requirement is £145,927.\n\nThat is a significant amount of cash. The developer equity in a typical South Coast development is 25% to 35% of the total cost. For a £500,000 project, that is £125,000 to £175,000 of your own money.\n\nThe equity can come from your own savings, a joint venture partner, or other investors. Many developers use a JV structure where a partner provides the equity and receives a share of the profit.\n\nThe equity required determines whether a development is accessible to you. If you have £50,000 in available cash, a project requiring £150,000 equity is out of reach unless you bring in partners.\n\nEquity can also be reduced by negotiating a lower site price, reducing build costs, or finding a lender who offers higher LTC. Every pound of equity saved is a pound that can go to the next project.

**The formula**

```
Equity Required = GDC - Development Loan
```

**Why this matters**

Development equity required is typically 25% to 35% of total cost. If you cannot cover this from your own cash, consider a JV partner. The equity required determines whether the project is accessible.
