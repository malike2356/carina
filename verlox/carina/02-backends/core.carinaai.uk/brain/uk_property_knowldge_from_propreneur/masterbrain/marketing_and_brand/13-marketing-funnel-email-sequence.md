**Source:** Property Sourcing Intensive (PSI) - Module 3: Marketing, Sessions 12-13
**Instructor:** the course instructor
**Extracted:** 14 May 2026

---

## Core Principle: Create Value First

Before you can sell anything, you must create value and give it away for free. This builds the know/like/trust factor. People who only see your social media posts won't buy from you. You need to move them through a structured journey.

**The journey:** Stranger → Free value (lead magnet) → Database → Nurture emails → Know/Like/Trust → Free call → Sale

---

## The Lead Magnet (Free Giveaway)

Something you give away for free to capture contact details. Must be property/investment related.

### Examples of Lead Magnets
- A spreadsheet that calculates total investment on a property
- A free book/PDF on property investing
- A free 15-minute consultation call showing how to find BMV properties
- Being a guest on a podcast

### Rules
- Must be high value
- Must relate to property investment
- Solve a specific problem your ideal investor has

## The 17-Day Email Sequence

This automated sequence moves a lead from stranger to trusting prospect ready for a sales call.

### Email 1 (Immediate - Welcome)
- Thank them for joining
- Confirm their freebie is on its way (book, PDF, spreadsheet)
- Warm, casual tone
- "We'll be in touch to help you out"

### Email 2 (Day 3-4 - Deal Showcase)
- Share a real deal you recently completed
- Include: purchase price, refurb cost, final value
- Add photos or video
- 1-2 paragraphs max - don't make it long
- Example: "We sourced this property for £65,000, £20k refurb, valued at £115,000. Great deal."

### Email 3 (Day 5-7 - How Your Business Works)
- Explain your process
- "If you want to buy a deal from us, here's what to expect"
- Clear steps: payment, timelines, what they need to provide

### Email 4 (Day 9-11 - Compliance)
- Show you're legitimate
- AML registration, data protection, ICO, etc.
- Builds trust through professionalism

### Email 5 (Day 14 - Testimonial)
- Share a testimonial from a happy investor
- Video testimonial is ideal
- Social proof is the most powerful trust builder

### Email 6 (Day 17 - Call to Action)
- Offer a free Zoom call
- "I'd love to jump on a call and find out your needs"
- Position it as helping them, not selling

## Virtual PA Follow-Up

7-14 days after someone joins your database, have a Virtual PA call them.

### VPA Script
> "Hi [Name], I noticed you joined our database and ordered [freebie]. I'm just giving you a call to make sure you received everything and to find out if there's anything else you need."

### What This Achieves
- 10-15% of people will answer
- Builds the customer experience
- Keeps your brand top of mind
- Shows you care - builds know/like/trust

VPA cost: ~$2-4/hour via freelancers (Upwork, Freelancer, etc.)

---

## The Free Zoom Call Structure

Once a lead is warmed up (after the 17-day email sequence), offer a free 45-minute call.

| Section | Time | Purpose |
|---------|:----:|---------|
| Value (advice) | ~30 mins (75%) | Listen to their needs, give genuine advice |
| Pitch | ~15 mins (25%) | Present your services and how you can help |

### Key Principles
- 75% value, 25% pitch - don't sell too early
- Listen more than you talk
- Position yourself as an expert who can solve their problem
- If they don't buy, they'll remember you for later

---

## Summary

1. Create a valuable free lead magnet (property-related)
2. Run small paid ads ($5-10/day) to drive traffic
3. Capture leads into a database
4. Run the 17-day automated email sequence
5. Have a VPA call them 7-14 days after signup
6. Offer a free Zoom call at day 17
7. 75% value → 25% pitch on the call
8. Close the deal

**Everything hinges on the value you give away first.** If the value is high and the targeting is right, 5-8% conversion is achievable.
