# Social Media Post Creation and Engagement Tactics

## The Core Principle: Conversation, Not Broadcast

Social media posts should be like conversations. You do not talk **to** people - you talk **with** people. A speaker on stage who lectures without interaction loses their audience immediately. The best speakers are the most engaging, and the same principle applies to social media.

**The test:** Every post should require the reader to do something - interact, respond, or act.

---

## The Engagement Formula

### Ask Questions

Questions are the most powerful engagement tool. Examples that work:
- "What has been your biggest profit margin on a refurb?"
- "What has been your biggest breakthrough this year?"
- "What has been your biggest obstacle in property investing?"

**Why questions work:**
- Some people want to show off (they'll share their best results)
- Some are realistic and honest (they'll share genuine experiences)
- Some will share challenges (which tells you what your market needs)
- Every response - positive, negative, or neutral - is an **interaction**

**Rule:** All engagement is valuable engagement. The goal is interaction, not just positive responses.

---

## Call-to-Action Elements

Every post must include at least one clear call-to-action:

| CTA Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Comment | "Comment below your biggest property challenge" |
| DM | "DM me for more information" |
| Share | "Share this if you found it useful" |
| Click | "Click the link to register for our free deals list" |
| One-word response | "What's the one word you'd advise investors for success?" |
| Request | "Send me your deal analysis - I'll review it for free" |

The CTA forces the algorithm to recognise your content as interactive.

---

## Post Length and Format

Research from content platform data suggests:
- Optimal post length: **30-50 words**
- Not a one-liner - enough context and background to be substantive
- Not an essay - keeps attention

**Video promotion post structure:**
1. Introduce yourself and what you do (brief)
2. State the benefit to the viewer (what's in it for them)
3. Call-to-action: tell them exactly what to do (visit website, register, click link)
4. Specificity: "Register by Tuesday and I'll send you deals at 10am every week"

**Example structure for promoting a mailing list:**
> "My name is [Name] and I find great deals in [Area]. If you're looking for property investment deals in [Area], click the link below, register on my website, and I'll send you deals every [Day] at [Time]."

## The Algorithm Activation Trick

Before you post, spend **10-15 minutes** actively engaging with other content:

1. Find posts similar to your content or in your target audience space
2. Hit the like button on at least 5-10 posts
3. Leave genuine, thoughtful comments
4. Share a positive review or interaction that requires the original poster to respond

**Why this works:**
- Instagram and other platforms classify you as a **creator**, not just a consumer
- The algorithm prioritises creator accounts in distribution
- Engagement before posting signals to the algorithm you are active and interactive
- Your next post gets pushed to a wider audience as a result

**Rule:** You get love by giving love first. Engage before you post.

## Consumer Psychology - What Your Audience Cares About

The fundamental rule of all marketing content:

> **"Consumers only care what's in it for them."**

They do not care about you, your business name, or your achievements until they understand what you can do for them.

**Every post must answer one of these questions from the reader's perspective:**
- What do I learn from this?
- What do I get if I act?
- How does this help me with my property goals?
- Why should I trust this person?

The purpose of social media is to create interaction. The more engaging your posts, the more interaction you generate. Interaction builds the know-like-trust relationship that eventually converts followers into clients.

## Content Format Options for Property Sourcers Who Avoid Camera

### Option 1: Screen Record + Voiceover
1. Find a relevant news article about your area (regeneration, investment, market trends)
2. Screenshot the article
3. Open Instagram Reels, use the "Green Screen" effect with the screenshot as background
4. Record your voiceover commentary on why this matters for investors

### Option 2: AI Avatar
1. Upload a clear selfie to an AI avatar generation tool
2. Write your script
3. AI generates a video of your likeness speaking the script
4. Publish without being on camera

**Key point:** You do not need to be on camera. But you do need to be seen. Content without you (even a representation of you) builds less trust than content that shows a face.

---

## Quick Checklist Before Every Post

- Does this post ask a question or require an action?
- Have I spent 10-15 minutes engaging with others before posting?
- Is the benefit to the reader clear in the first 5 words?
- Is the call-to-action specific and easy to follow?
- Is the post 30-50 words (for text-based), or does the video have a clear intro and CTA?
- Am I posting consistently (3x per week minimum)?

---

## Platform-Specific Timing and Consistency

- VIP deal alerts: **Monday 2pm** (24 hours before general release)
- General database deals: **Tuesday 9am**
- Value content posts: **2-3 times per week** on social platforms
- Consistency at fixed times trains your audience to expect your content

When investors know your deal email arrives every Tuesday at 9am, they anticipate it. The same applies to social media content - predictability builds habit and trust.
