# 104 - Property Decision Making, Risk Management & Credit Health

> **Source:** Entrepreneur Bootcamp (Bootcamp talk, May 2026)
> **Context:** Direct instruction from Alistair to property entrepreneurs on how to make fast decisions, manage risk, maintain credit files, and overcome procrastination in a property business.

## 1. Best/Worst/Likely Case Decision Framework

Before any major decision - every deal, every joint venture, every partnership - run this framework:

- **Best case:** What is the absolute best that can happen? (e.g., you get the deal, make money, gain freedom, grow your network)
- **Worst case:** Be realistic, not catastrophic. What actually happens? (e.g., you lose your investment, no change in your life, you learn a lesson)
- **Likely case:** The most probable outcome - what will probably happen? (e.g., you grow, you learn, you make some money, you build your network)

Then ask yourself: **Can I live with the likely case?**

If yes, proceed. If the worst case is acceptable and the likely case is positive, move forward immediately.

> "I do it every deal, every joint venture, every partnership. Best case, worst case, likely case. Then 'can I live with the likely case?' And if yes, one, two, three - go."

This framework stops overthinking and analysis paralysis. It forces a decision quickly rather than consulting ten different people who don't know any more than you do.

## 2. Speed of Implementation Principle

- **"One, two, three - go."** Count down and act before your brain talks you out of it.
- **"Done is the new perfect."** Stop waiting to get it perfectly right. Get it done, then make it perfect.
- **"If you wait until you're certain, you'll never start."** Certainty is a myth in business.

### Real case study from the bootcamp:
A buyer (Joe) secured a 5-bed property in Leeds in less than 48 hours. Timeline:
- Thursday: Investor asked Joe to house 5 guests in Leeds for a year
- Thursday: Joe went on Rightmove, made calls, arranged viewings
- Thursday: Went out, viewed, signed the contract same day
- Friday/Saturday: Found furniture, painted, got the property ready
- Saturday: Tenants moved in

The result: £35/guest/night = £175/day = £5,250/month. After rent and bills, approximately £3,000/month profit. Secured in under two days because he acted immediately instead of overthinking.

> "Speed of implementation. No excuses. Any secure deal."

### Practical application:
When a new lead comes into your CRM (Capex), ring them straight away. Speed of implementation will always lead to the deal.

## 3. Credit File Management

### Check your credit report
- Use **Check My File** or **Experian** (approx £7.99/month)
- Check it every month - do not assume it is correct
- Credit reports often contain errors that can impact mortgage lending

### Fix errors immediately
**Case study (from Alistair's personal experience):**
Alistair checked his credit file and found Mercedes listed twice - two separate accounts with two different balances. This was impacting his credit score. He wrote to Mercedes and Experian explaining the error. They fixed it within a week, and his credit report improved immediately.

### Remove incorrect linked associates
- Check for linked associates on your credit file: ex-partners, old flatmates, former business partners
- **Real example:** A colleague moved into an apartment. The previous tenant was still linked on his credit file because debt letters were still arriving at that address. The previous tenant had multiple CCJs. This affected the colleague's ability to get approved for even a mobile phone.
- If the previous occupant is still receiving mail at your address, it can link them to your credit file
- Appeal and get incorrect associates removed

### Electoral roll registration
- **This is mandatory** for mortgage applications
- Not being on the electoral roll is one of the biggest red flags mortgage companies see
- It impacts your ability to get mortgage lending approved
- Register where you live. Do not skip this.

### Credit card best practice
- Use credit cards for everything, then pay off in full every month
- This builds your credit file, gives Section 75 protection, and keeps your utilisation low
- Keep credit utilisation under 50% (if your limit is £5,000, don't carry a balance above £2,500)
- Never pay only the minimum payment - credit agencies see this as inability to pay
- Do not cancel credit cards - cut them up if you must, but keep the accounts open

## 4. CCJ Case Study (What Not To Do)

### The £20K CCJ case
A real person got a CCJ for £20,000 on a property deal where they tried to hold a deposit unprofessionally. The issues:
- Missing documents
- No proper contracts in place
- Trying to cut corners destroyed their credibility

The lesson: **Get proper legal documentation for every single deal.** Do not try to hold money without the correct legal framework.

### Alistair's personal CCJ experience
Alistair had a CCJ placed on his file for £187 from Companies House - a late filing fee. How it happened:
- He moved addresses in 2022 and updated Companies House online
- Companies House changed the address in their system
- Seven or eight months later, Companies House sent a late filing notice - to the **old address**
- Alistair never received it. Sent solicitors letters to old address too. Sent court letters to old address.
- He didn't show up to court, so the CCJ was automatically granted
- He only discovered it when applying for a mortgage refinance. The mortgage was declined.

**The cost:**
- £18,000 in legal fees to get the CCJ removed
- The bridge stayed at 2% per month instead of 1% = an extra £1,000/month in interest
- It took 9 months to remove = £9,000 in extra interest
- **Total cost: £27,000** - all because of a £187 late filing fee sent to the wrong address

**Key takeaway:** It is your responsibility to ensure filings are up to date and your address is correct everywhere. Even if you pay someone else (e.g., an accountant), the ultimate responsibility is yours.

## 5. Companies House Management

### Ensure filings are up to date
- Late filing fines start at **£150** and escalate significantly
- The **confirmation statement** must be filed annually
- **Annual accounts** must be filed on time
- Dormant company filings must be handled properly - do not ignore them
- Change of address must be updated with Companies House and confirmed

### Address management is critical
When you move address:
1. Update Companies House online
2. Verify the change has taken effect
3. Notify all relevant parties (HMRC, banks, suppliers) of the new address
4. Ensure your accountant knows and handles filings on time

**Real cost of failure:** One missed filing fee of £187 led to £27,000 in costs and 9 months of delays.

> "It's up to me to make sure these things are all done. I can't delegate liability."

## 6. Risk Management Principles

### Know what you don't know
- Ask for help from experienced people. Do not try to fake expertise.
- Be in environments (Zoom calls, coaching, masterminds) where you can learn from those ahead of you
- Accept that you will make mistakes, but minimise them by getting advice before acting

### Don't fake expertise
- If you don't know something, say so and ask for guidance
- Investors and partners see through bluffing immediately
- Your credibility is your currency

### Have a cash buffer
- Maintain a minimum of **3 months' operating expenses** in the bank
- If you don't have 10K in your bank account, you should not be doing passive investment. Everything should be about generating cash.
- **Build cash first:** Make active income, stack it, then invest in passive assets

### Insurance and compliance
- **Public liability insurance** in place
- **Professional indemnity insurance** in place
- Compliance with **anti-money laundering regulations** if you are sourcing deals
- Sourcing deals without proper compliance damages the industry and your credibility

### Risk-aware growth
- Do not go "all in" without cash reserves
- Case study: Ryan went from zero to 14 properties in 6 months, mostly on borrowed money. He didn't factor in common problems. Every property lost £600-700/month. He ended up £48K in debt to friends and family, losing £8-9K/month.
- The best business owners have a money thermometer: they know their baseline. If they drop below it, they treat themselves as broke and focus entirely on generating cash.

## 7. Overcoming Procrastination

### The 5-Second Rule
Count **5-4-3-2-1** and act before your brain talks you out of it. Your brain will manufacture reasons not to act. The countdown interrupts that pattern.

### Focus on the first step, not the whole mountain
- Overthinking comes from looking at the entire journey instead of just the next action
- "You know, one of the big mistakes is people plan over progress."
- Instead of planning endlessly behind a laptop, get boots on the ground

### "Broke people don't make decisions. Successful people make decisions quickly."
- Rich and successful people make multi-million-pound decisions in moments
- Broke people consult with everyone, ask 101 different opinions, and still don't act
- Speed of decision-making is a habit. Practice it.

### The compounding effect of excuses
- Excuses start small but compound
- The less you do, the more your excuses justify themselves
- Eventually the excuse replaces the action entirely
- Break the cycle by making a decision and acting immediately

### Practical tools for action:
1. Every time an opportunity presents itself, run best/worst/likely case
2. Ask: "Can I live with the likely case?"
3. Count **5-4-3-2-1** and act
4. Remember: **"Done is the new perfect"**
